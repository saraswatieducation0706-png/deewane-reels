# YouTube rules for every video and reel (all channels)

Applies to every video, Short and reel made in this repo: Christ's Blessings (`christs-blessings/`),
Deewane job reels (`tooling/`, deewane-job-reels skill) and GATE90 reels (`gate90/`).
Naveen's instruction (21 Oct 2026): all content must follow YouTube's rules so the channels can be
monetized and stay monetized. These rules come from the YouTube Help Center (checked 21 Oct 2026) and India's
IT Rules amendment 2026. Shared version for people outside the team: "YouTube AI Content Rules – Religion &
Community Channels" (PDF, Oct 2026).

Run the **pre-publish check** at the end on every video before booking it. If a video fails a point and you
can't fix it in the run, do not book it; list it in the final message with the reason.

## 1. Originality (inauthentic & reused content) — the biggest risk
Mass-produced or repetitive content is not monetizable, with or without AI, and YouTube judges it at
channel level.
- Every video has its own script and its own point. Never re-upload a script with a few words swapped.
- Add real value: explanation, story, reflection, teaching, worked solution. No bare readings or text
  slideshows over a background.
- Vary scenes, layouts, hooks, music and pacing between videos; no numbered series of look-alike videos.
- No clips from other channels, TV serials, films, news channels, kathas or sermons, even with permission.

## 2. AI label (altered or synthetic content)
- Set YouTube `isAiGeneratedContent: true` when a video shows **realistic** AI-made people, places or events,
  makes a real person appear to say or do something, or uses AI-generated music as the main focus.
- Stylised or animated visuals, AI help with scripts/titles/thumbnails, and an AI narration voice that is not a
  copy of a real person do not need the YouTube label. Keep Instagram `isAiGenerated: true` (AI narration).
- When unsure, label it. The label does not reduce reach or monetization; failing to label can.

## 3. Religion, caste and community
Religion, caste, ethnicity, nationality and other protected groups are covered by YouTube's hate speech
policy and by Indian law (BNS ss. 196, 299, 353).
- Never mock, insult, stereotype or put down any religion, caste, community, region or group — in the
  video, title, thumbnail, description, tags or pinned comment.
- Christ's Blessings: speak warmly about the Christian faith; never criticise other faiths, churches or
  denominations, and never take sides between them.
- No AI images that make holy figures, saints or places of worship look disrespectful or sensational.
- No content built on riots, communal incidents or tragedies.

## 4. Real people
- No AI likeness or voice clone of a real, identifiable person (religious leaders, officials, politicians,
  celebrities, toppers' faces). Toppers may be named only as the existing GATE90 procedure allows.
- No persona pretending to be a human expert giving medical, legal, financial or political advice.

## 5. Health, money and false promises
- No cure claims, no "stop your treatment", no promised miracles, healing, jobs, money or exam results.
- Comfort and prayer are fine; promising outcomes is not. Keep the existing helpline line on sleep/anxiety
  videos.
- Never ask viewers for money or to message privately; no outside payout links.
- No fear or guilt bait ("share or bad luck", "God's warning", fake prophecies, "last chance" pressure that
  isn't true).

## 6. Job reels and exam content (Deewane, GATE90)
- Every fact (posts, dates, eligibility, fees, pay) from the official notification or official site, as the
  skill already requires. Never invent or round up numbers to sound bigger.
- Never present a reel as an official government announcement: no State Emblem of India, no organisation logo
  used as if it were our branding, no "official" wording for our own content.
- No "guaranteed selection / job" claims for any course or test series.

## 7. Titles, thumbnails, descriptions, tags
- Title and thumbnail must match what the video actually contains. No misleading clickbait, no fake "LIVE".
- No profanity, gore, shocking or sexual imagery, and nothing from section 3 in the thumbnail or title.
- Tags and hashtags relevant to this video only; no keyword stuffing or unrelated trending tags.

## 8. Copyright and audio
- Music only from the repo's own generators or properly licensed sources. No film songs, label bhajans or
  worship songs, no music copied from other videos.
- Scripture: Christ's Blessings quotes the King James Version only (public domain in the US), word for word.
- No copied images, characters, logos or artwork from others.

## 9. Audience and channel health
- `madeForKids: false` on all current channels (none of them is made for children). If a future series is
  aimed at children, ask Naveen first: it must then be set as Made for Kids.
- Keep uploads steady; never flood a channel with many near-identical uploads in one day.
- Do not delete or hide videos to escape a policy problem; flag the problem to Naveen instead.

## Pre-publish check (every video)
1. Original script and point, not a near-copy of an earlier video?
2. Real value for the viewer?
3. No third-party clips, music or images?
4. AI label set correctly (section 2)?
5. No real person's likeness or cloned voice?
6. Nothing against any religion, caste, community or group, anywhere (incl. thumbnail and tags)?
7. No cure, miracle, money, job or result promises; no money requests or private-contact requests?
8. Facts checked against the official source (job/exam content) or KJV text (scripture)?
9. Title and thumbnail honest; no shocking imagery or profanity?
10. `madeForKids` set correctly?
