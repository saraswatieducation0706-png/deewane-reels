What are engineering jobs in government sector?

Which engineering is best for government job?

Civil Engineer jobs are best in Government Sector. Here some best engineering jobs are

✔️ Electrical Engineers   
✔️ Computer Engineers   
✔️ ECE Engineers

Which engineering branch is best for government jobs?

The below disciplines / branches are the best govt jobs and highest paid jobs in Government Sector:

✔️ Civil Engineering   
✔️ Mechanical Engineering   
✔️ Electrical Engineering   
✔️ Electronics and Communications Engineering

How can I get government job after engineering?

Candidates who have passed Bachelor's Degree in Engineering or Technology and final year engineering students apply for Govt Jobs through following steps:

✔️ First Apply and attend Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) Examination. If you get good score in GATE exam, you eligible to apply for Public Sector companies (PSU) Jobs.   
✔️ You can also apply Engineer jobs without GATE Exam: Check Latest Engineer Jobs list in "www.indgovtjobs.in Engineer Jobs 2020 page". Click appropriate Govt Organization and then get details.   
✔️ Then, visit your clicked Govt Engineer Job notification page, then click apply online link to apply before closing date.   
✔️ If your application was shortlisted, you will be called for selection process like Written Test / Interview / Group Discussion etc.

What is the qualification for engineering govt jobs?

Engineering based Government Jobs minimum qualifications are:

✔️ Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.)   
✔️ Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.)   
✔️ Associate Member of Institution of Engineers (AMIE)
