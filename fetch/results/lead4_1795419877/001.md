## JKSSB Junior Engineer (Mechanical) Recruitment 2026: 127 PWD (R&B) Vacancies, Eligibility and Pay

The Jammu and Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB) has released Advertisement Notification No. 10 of 2026 for 127 posts of Junior Engineer (Mechanical) in the Public Works (R&B) Department. The pay is Level-6 (₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400). Selection is on merit in a written/OMR examination only, and the fee is ₹600, or ₹500 for SC, ST-1, ST-2, EWS and PwBD candidates. Find more such openings on our [Jammu and Kashmir government jobs](https://govtjobsalert.in/jammu-and-kashmir-govt-jobs/) page.

Online applications and online fee payment run from 23 November 2026 (12:00 AM) to 22 December 2026 (11:59 PM), as fixed by the Board's corrigendum dated 19 November 2026. The corrigendum also reads the qualification as per SRO 180 of 1997, with an equivalence note for Aeronautical, Automobile and Production Engineering. Only domiciles of the UT of Jammu and Kashmir can apply. All other terms of the advertisement stay unchanged, and other qualifications are rejected at any stage.

### What is the Eligibility for JKSSB Junior Engineer (Mechanical) 2026?

#### Educational Qualification

The corrigendum dated 19 November 2026 reads the qualification as per the J&K Engineering (Subordinate) Service Recruitment Rules, 1997 (SRO 180 dated 26.05.1997). Read it as the routes below and keep your certificate ready in original.

Route| Requirement  
---|---  
Diploma| 3 years Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from a Government recognised institute  
Degree| Indian University Degree in Mechanical Engineering  
AMIE| AMIE Section (A and B), India  
Equivalence| An Indian University Degree or a three-year Diploma from a Government recognised institute in Aeronautical, Automobile or Production Engineering is equivalent to a Degree or Diploma in Mechanical Engineering, respectively  
  
Diploma holders can also look at our [diploma government jobs](https://govtjobsalert.in/diploma-govt-jobs/) page, and degree holders at [engineering government jobs](https://govtjobsalert.in/engineering-govt-jobs/). If you claim an equivalent qualification, produce an Equivalence Certificate when asked. The Board's decision on such a claim is final. You must hold the qualification by the last date to apply.

#### Conditions That Can Cancel Your Candidature

These lines are easy to skip and each one can end your application.

Condition| What it means for you  
---|---  
Domicile| You must hold a valid Domicile Certificate in the prescribed format on or before the cut-off date, i.e. the last date to apply (22.12.2026). Original is checked at Document Verification  
Wrong qualification| Anyone with a qualification other than the one prescribed shall not be eligible. The candidature is summarily rejected at any stage  
Degree from outside J&K| If your degree is from a recognised university or college outside the UT of J&K (other than a Central University), you need a Bonafide Certificate in the Board's format from the Controller or Registrar  
Proof of qualification| Shortlisted candidates must show originals earned on or before the cut-off date, failing which the candidature is cancelled  
Provisional entry| Your candidature stays provisional until documents are verified. An ineligible candidate is not allowed into later stages  
Wrong claims| If any claim in your form is not substantiated, the candidature is cancelled and criminal proceedings may follow  
Category certificate| It must be valid on the cut-off date, in the prescribed format, and uploaded with the form. No second chance to upload is given. Other formats are summarily rejected, because you alone are responsible for getting the prescribed format within the validity period  
Fake certificate| A fake or forged reservation certificate means debarment from Board examinations, besides penal action  
Ex-Servicemen| You need a Form XIV certificate. An affidavit sworn before a 1st Class Judicial Magistrate is needed at scrutiny. Horizontal reservation, once availed on joining a post, cannot be claimed again. It applies to posts up to Level-6E (35900-113500)  
PwBD| Eligible PwBD candidates can take a scribe and 20 minutes per hour of extra time, with Annexure-B and Annexure-C. You must have applied as PwBD. A fraudulent PwBD claim means debarment  
Own scribe| You arrange your own scribe. The scribe must not be a candidate for the same exam, a relative, or a student of the same centre, and must hold a lower qualification than you and not more than the post's minimum qualification. If the scribe's qualification is found different from what you declared, you forfeit your right to the post. If you are caught assisting another PwBD candidate as scribe, both candidatures are cancelled  
One application| Only one online application per item number is allowed. If you appear more than once, the candidature is cancelled and you are debarred  
No corrections| Changes in the form after submission are not entertained  
Photo and signature| A blurred or illegible photograph or signature gets the application summarily rejected  
Name details| Write name, date of birth, father's name and mother's name exactly as in your matriculation certificate, or the candidature may be cancelled  
Exam day| Carry two recent colour photographs and an original photo-ID. Without them you will not be allowed to appear  
Devices| Mobile phones (even switched off), smart watches and similar devices are banned, with disciplinary action and a ban from future exams  
Document Verification| If you do not appear or do not produce originals on the scheduled date, you will not be considered for selection. Where a certificate has a prescribed format, bring it in that format, otherwise the candidature is liable to be cancelled or rejected  
Travel| No TA/DA is paid for the written test or Document Verification  
In-service candidates| You must send the printed form through the proper channel (your Head of Department)

### What is the Selection Process for JKSSB Junior Engineer (Mechanical) 2026?

Stage| Detail  
---|---  
Written/OMR examination| Objective type multiple choice questions, in English only  
Marking| Negative marking of one-fourth of the marks of that question for each wrong answer  
Document Verification| Shortlisted candidates bring originals and self-attested copies  
  
Selection is made on merit obtained in the written/OMR examination only. The exam date, centres and syllabus are not announced yet. The Board will notify them on its website. Exam centres cannot be changed on request. Admit cards will be downloadable about one week before the exam, and no roll number slip will be sent by post. Check the [syllabus page](https://govtjobsalert.in/syllabus/) on our site once the Board publishes the details. The number of questions, total marks and exam duration are not given in this notification.

Selection rests on this one exam, and each wrong answer costs one-fourth of that question's marks. Document Verification is not a formality either. You must bring your originals and self-attested copies, and anyone who fails to appear on the scheduled date is not considered.

Step| Action  
---|---  
1| From 23 November 2026 (12:00 AM) to 22 December 2026 (11:59 PM), open the JKSSB application portal (link in Essential Links below) and log in, or follow the new-user steps shown there  
2| Read the form-filling instructions the Board says it will publish separately on its website  
3| Fill every field carefully and select the item you are applying for  
4| Upload your live photograph, your signature (10 KB to 20 KB), your category certificate and self-attested qualification certificates (PDF, maximum 200 KB each)  
5| Pay the fee online by net banking, credit card or debit card  
6| Submit the form, note the application number and keep a printout for Document Verification  
  
Do not post or email the hard copy to the Board office. Keep it with you for Document Verification. The Board advises you not to wait for the closing days, because the portal can fail to load when many people log in together. Fill the form once, carefully, since the Board will not accept any request to change an entry after you submit.
