# JKSSB JE Mechanical Recruitment 2026 New Dates Out: Apply Online for 127 Posts from 23 November

By DK Malik | Oct 21, 2026 |  Updated: Nov 20, 12:05 pm 

[ Telegram ](https://t.me/quick_sarkari) [ WhatsApp ](https://whatsapp.com/channel/0029VaxxbrREquiPoXTWM229) [ YouTube ](https://www.youtube.com/@Quick_Sarkari) [ Instagram ](https://www.instagram.com/quicksarkari_com/) [ Facebook ](https://www.facebook.com/quicksarkari) [ X ](https://x.com/quick__sarkari)

JKSSB JE Mechanical Recruitment 2026: The Jammu & Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB) has officially released the recruitment advertisement for Junior Engineer (Mechanical) posts in the Public Works (R&B) Department. Under Advertisement Notification No. 10 of 2026, a total of 127 vacancies will be filled through direct recruitment. The JKSSB JE Mechanical Recruitment 2026 notification has been released on October 21, 2026. Latest Update (19.11.2026): JKSSB has announced the new dates for the online application. Eligible candidates can now apply online from 23 November 2026 (12:00 AM) to 22 December 2026 (11:59 PM), and the fee can also be paid online till 22 December 2026. Earlier, the application process scheduled to start on 16 November 2026 was postponed on 15 November 2026.

This is a UT cadre post in Pay Level 6 (Rs. 35,400 – Rs. 1,12,400/-), and selection is based only on the written exam. You must hold a valid J&K Domicile Certificate to apply, so arrange it before the last date.

### Age Limit, Vacancy Details, and Qualification

### J&K Domicile Certificate Requirement

Only domiciles of the UT of Jammu & Kashmir can apply. You need a valid Domicile Certificate in the prescribed format, issued on or before the last date of application. Candidates without one cannot apply.

### JKSSB JE Mechanical Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The selection process for JKSSB JE Mechanical Recruitment 2026 includes the following stages:

  * Written / OMR Examination
  * Document Verification



### How to Apply for JKSSB JE Mechanical Recruitment 2026

Follow these steps to apply for JKSSB JE Mechanical Recruitment 2026:

  * Visit jkssb.nic.in from 23.11.2026 and click on the "Log-in (Apply on Various Posts)" tab.
  * Click on "New User / Sign Up" and register with a valid email ID and mobile number; verify it with the OTP sent to your email.
  * Fill in your personal, educational, and category details as per your certificates.
  * Upload your Domicile Certificate and category certificate in the prescribed format. You won’t get another chance to upload them.
  * Pay the fee online and submit the form before 22.12.2026 (11:59 PM). The form cannot be edited after final submission.
  * Print the submitted form for future reference.
