RSSB JE Recruitment 2026: The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), Jaipur, has released the revised Junior Engineer (JEN) Combined Direct Recruitment 2026 notification under Advertisement No. 10/2026, increasing the vacancies from 874 to 1,909 Junior Engineer posts. The recruitment covers Civil, Electrical and Mechanical/Electrical Engineering positions for Degree and Diploma holders in six Rajasthan Government departments. Candidates with a relevant Engineering Degree or Diploma and knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari script and Rajasthani culture can apply. The general age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 1 January 2027, with applicable relaxations. The online application process has reopened on 18 November 2026, and eligible candidates can apply through the [Rajasthan SSO Portal](https://sso.rajasthan.gov.in/) until 17 December 2026 at 11:59 PM.

The RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026 revised notification, released on 17 November 2026, includes 1,814 vacancies in Non-Scheduled Areas and 95 vacancies in Scheduled Areas. Candidates who applied under the original advertisement between 16 August and 14 September 2026 do not need to submit fresh applications. Eligible previous Civil JE applicants can modify their department preferences free of charge from 22 to 31 December 2026. The recruitment examination is tentatively scheduled from 3 to 5 February 2027. Candidates should carefully review the revised vacancy distribution, eligibility requirements and department preference rules before submitting or modifying their applications.
