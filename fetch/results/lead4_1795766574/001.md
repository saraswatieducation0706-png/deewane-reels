## Selection Procedure

The detailed selection procedure has been mentioned in the official notification of BEL Panchkula Trainee Engineer Recruitment 2026. But we are giving you here the short summary of the selection procedure. The candidates are advised to refer to official notification for selection procedure. 

Selection Procedure:

  * Written Test: Selection will be conducted through a Written Test of 100 marks. There will be 1/4th negative marking for each incorrect response.
  * Merit List: Candidates shall be selected in the order of merit, category-wise and discipline-wise.
  * Minimum Qualifying Marks:
    * General/OBC-NCL: 35%
    * SC/ST/PwBD: 30%
  * Tie-Breaking: In case of a tie in marks, the overall percentage of marks obtained in the qualifying degree shall be considered to determine merit and resolve the tie.
  * No TA/DA: No traveling expenses/allowance will be given for attending the written test.
  * Medical Fitness: Provisional appointment is subject to being found ‘Medically Fit’ by the Company’s medical authorities as per BEL’s medical fitness standards.

Other Important Information:

  * Eligibility: Only Indian citizens can apply. Cut-off date for age and qualification is 01.11.2026.
  * Post Qualification Experience: Nil (no post-qualification experience required).
  * Engagement Period: Trainee Engineer-I will be initially engaged for TWO years, extendable for one more year based on project requirement and performance.
  * Advancement to Project Engineer-I: After completing two years as Trainee Engineer with performance assessment score of 80% and above (aggregate), attendance above 80%, good discipline, and no adverse remarks, the candidate may be advanced to Project Engineer-I for a period of 3 years (extendable up to 1 more year).
  * Retention Bonus: Project Engineers completing overall tenure of 4 years are entitled to a Retention Bonus of Rs. 25,000/- per completed year (total Rs. 1,00,000/- payable at end of 4 years).
  * Tough Location Allowance: 10% of consolidated remuneration for engineers posted to far-flung/tough locations.
  * Gratuity: Admissible as per BEL Employees’ Gratuity Scheme.
  * Age Relaxation: SC/ST: 5 years; OBC (NCL): 3 years; PwBD (40% or more disability): 10 years additional; Ex-Servicemen: as per Govt. guidelines. Maximum age limit considering all relaxations: 50 years.
  * Caste/Disability Certificate: Must be strictly in the format available on BEL website, otherwise candidate will be considered under General category.
  * OBC Certificate: Must be issued on or after 01.11.2025. EWS Certificate must be issued for year 2026/2026-27.
  * Currently Working in BEL: Trainee Engineers/Project Engineers currently working in BEL are NOT eligible to apply. If applied, the application will be summarily rejected.
  * Jurisdiction: Any dispute arising out of this advertisement shall be subject to the sole jurisdiction of Courts situated at Panchkula/Chandigarh.
  * Disclaimer: BEL does not demand any fee other than the application fee mentioned. Beware of fraudsters claiming to be BEL representatives.
  * Contact: For queries, email: PKHRRECT@BEL.CO.IN
