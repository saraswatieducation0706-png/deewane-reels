## Eligibility

### Educational Qualification

A full-time Bachelor's degree in Electrical, Electronics, Power Supply, Instrumentation and Control, Industrial Electronics, Electronics & Instrumentation, Applied Electronics, Digital Electronics or Power Electronics Engineering, or a combination of these.

### Minimum Marks

  * UR/EWS candidates (and SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwBD candidates applying against unreserved posts): First class or at least 60%.
  * SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwBD candidates against reserved posts: At least 50%.



The degree must be from an AICTE/UGC-recognised institution. Sections A & B of the Institution of Engineers (India) are also accepted. If the marksheet shows only CGPA and the university has no conversion formula, 6 on a 10-point scale is treated as 60%.

### Experience

At least 2 years of post-qualification experience in electrical engineering fields such as railway electrification, general electrical installations, site supervision, power distribution, electrical machines, or construction and maintenance of electrical equipment in buildings.

Training (except on-the-job), internship, teaching, academic, fellowship and PhD research periods are not counted. Apprenticeship under the Apprenticeship Act, 1961, in the relevant field can count towards the minimum experience.

## Age Relaxation

The following age relaxations are applicable over the maximum age limit of 32 years:

  * SC/ST: 5 years
  * OBC-NCL: 3 years
  * J&K domicile: 5 years
  * PwBD: 10 years, in addition to any SC/ST/OBC-NCL relaxation
  * RITES regular or contract employees meeting the criteria: 5 years



Ex-servicemen are covered under extant government orders. PwBD relaxation applies whether or not the post is reserved for them, as long as the post is identified as suitable.

For this post, the identified categories are locomotor disability (OA, OL, leprosy cured, acid attack victims) and hearing impairment.

## Selection Process

The selection process consists of three stages: written test, document scrutiny and interview.

### 1\. Written Test

  * Total Questions: 125 objective-type questions
  * Marks: 1 mark for each question
  * Duration: 2.5 hours
  * Negative Marking: No negative marking
  * PwBD candidates get 50 minutes of compensatory time.
  * Minimum qualifying marks: 50% for UR/EWS and 45% for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwBD candidates against reserved posts.



### 2\. Document Scrutiny

Based on test results and the number of vacancies, RITES scrutinises uploaded documents and shortlists candidates for interview. Original documents are verified before the interview.

### 3\. Interview

  * Candidates are called in the ratio of 1:6 to the vacancies.
  * Technical & Professional Proficiency: 30 marks
  * Personality, Communication & Competency: 10 marks
  * Minimum qualifying marks: 60% for UR/EWS and 50% for reserved categories.



### Final Merit Weightage

Selection Stage | Weightage  
---|---  
Written Test | 60%  
Interview | 40%  
Total | 100%  
  
In case of a tie in marks, the older candidate ranks higher. Candidates can take the selection process in Hindi or English. Appointment is subject to medical fitness.

## Written Test Syllabus

The written examination covers the following nine units:

  1. Electric Circuits and Fields: Network theorems, three-phase circuits, electric and magnetic fields.
  2. Signals and Systems: Fourier, Laplace and Z transforms, sampling theorem.
  3. Electrical Machines: Transformers, DC machines, induction and synchronous machines.
  4. Power Systems: Transmission, distribution, load flow, fault analysis, protection and HVDC.
  5. Control Systems: Transfer functions, Routh, Nyquist, Bode and state space.
  6. Electrical and Electronic Measurements: Bridges, instruments, oscilloscopes and error analysis.
  7. Analog and Digital Electronics: Diodes, BJT, FET, op-amps, logic circuits and microprocessor basics.
  8. Power Electronics and Drives: Thyristors, MOSFETs, IGBTs, converters, choppers and inverters.
  9. Application/Utilisation of Electrical Energy: Electrification, lighting, sub-stations, DG sets, solar, HVAC, lifts, fire alarm, CCTV, planning, estimation, installation, testing and commissioning.



## Exam Centres

The written test will be conducted at the following examination centres:

  * Delhi/Gurgaon
  * Kolkata
  * Bangalore
  * Mumbai
  * Bhubaneswar
  * Guwahati



Candidates must provide two examination centre preferences while applying. However, RITES may allot a different centre. All centres except Delhi/Gurgaon are tentative.

  3. Upload all required documents under the "Upload Document" section: 
     * High school certificate (proof of date of birth)

     * Identity and address proof

  4. Pay the application fee under the "Make Payment" section and download the final application form.
  5. Keep a printed and signed copy of the application form to carry if called for document verification.



An offer letter alone is not accepted as proof of experience. Hard copies should not be sent by post or courier, and each application is final once submitted.
