Karnataka PU Lecturer Recruitment 2026: The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has released a recruitment notification dated 26 November 2026 for filling 778 Pre-University Lecturer (Group B) vacancies in Government Pre-University Colleges under the School Education (Pre-University) Department, Karnataka. The recruitment covers 13 subjects, including Kannada, English, History, Economics, Political Science, Sociology, Geography, Commerce, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology and Computer Science. Candidates generally require a relevant Master's Degree with at least 55% marks and a Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) or equivalent qualification, with prescribed concessions and separate Computer Science eligibility provisions. The age limit varies from 40 to 45 years depending on the category. Selected candidates will receive a pay scale of Rs. 69,250 to Rs. 1,34,200. Online applications can be submitted from 26 November to 25 December 2026 through [cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea](https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/).

The KEA PU Lecturer Recruitment 2026 includes 773 regular vacancies and five backlog positions under the Residual Parent Cadre (RPC). Commerce has the highest number of vacancies, followed by English and Computer Science. Selection includes a compulsory Kannada Language Examination and a competitive examination comprising two subject-related papers. Candidates must obtain the prescribed qualifying marks in the Kannada examination, unless exempt under the applicable rules. The competitive papers carry 150 marks each, with negative marking for incorrect answers. The last date to submit the online application is 25 December 2026, while the examination fee can be paid until 26 December 2026. Applicants should review the qualification, category, syllabus and reservation requirements before submitting the application.

General Merit and other categories (Category-2A / 2B / 3A / 3B): Rs. 750. SC / ST / Category-1 / Ex-servicemen / Third gender: Rs. 500. Persons with disabilities: Rs. 250.

Candidates who have written the qualifying examination and are awaiting results. Persons permanently debarred by UPSC, any State PSC, KEA or any recruitment examination.
