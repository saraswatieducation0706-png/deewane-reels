Chhattisgarh State Power Generation Company Limited (CSPGCL) has announced 66 vacancies, including Graduate Apprentice and Diploma Apprentice posts. The last date to apply online is 01 December 2026. Candidates can apply through the official website, cspdcl.co.in. 

## CSPGCL Quick Summary

2026 · 66 Posts

COMPANY NAME Chhattisgarh State Power Generation Company Limited

POST NAME Graduate Apprentice (Technical) and Diploma Apprentice

QUALIFICATION B.Tech/B.E, Diploma

VACANCY 66 Posts

LAST DATE 01-12-2026

Chhattisgarh State Power Generation Company Limited (CSPGCL), Korba, has invited applications for 66 posts in 8 roles under Advt No. 20-02/पीजीटीआई/ए.पी.टी./1442. The posts include Graduate Apprentice (Civil Engineering), Graduate Apprentice (Mechanical Engineering), Graduate Apprentice (Electrical Engineering), Graduate Apprentice (CSE/EEE/IT) and 4 more. Applications are accepted online until 1 December 2026.

The posts are for Hasdeo Thermal Power Station, Korba West, Shyama Prasad Mukherjee Thermal Power Station, Korba East, Atal Bihari Vajpayee Thermal Power Station, Marwa Tendubhatha, District Janjgir-Champa, Hasdeo Bango Hydro Electric Plant, Machadoli. Qualification required: Graduate Apprentice: graduate in Engineering/Technology in the relevant faculty from a recognised institute/university. Diploma Apprentice: diploma in the relevant faculty from the State Technical Board/recognised institute. The pay is Stipend Rs. 12,300 per month (Graduate Apprentice) and Rs. 10,900 per month (Diploma Apprentice). Selection is by merit list prepared on the basis of marks obtained in the qualifying examination and marks obtained in the interview. Post-wise eligibility, dates and the steps to apply are given below.

## How to Apply for CSPGCL Recruitment 2026 Online

  1. Register on the NATS portal (www.nats.education.gov.in) and get a valid 16-digit registration number.
  2. Apply online to CSPGCL through the Establishment tab of the NATS portal; this is mandatory.
  3. Fill the application form in the attached format and enclose self-attested copies of the listed documents.
  4. Send the offline application by post or submit it in person at the PGTI office, Korba East, between 10.00 AM and 05.30 PM.
  5. Applications must be received by 01 December 2026; later applications will not be considered.

## CSPGCL Recruitment 2026 - FAQs

### Q1. What is the last date to apply for CSPGCL Recruitment 2026?

The last date is 1 December 2026.

### Q2. How many vacancies are there in CSPGCL Recruitment 2026?

There are 66 vacancies: 2 Graduate Apprentice (Civil Engineering), 17 Graduate Apprentice (Mechanical Engineering), 16 Graduate Apprentice (Electrical Engineering), 3 Graduate Apprentice (CSE/EEE/IT), 2 Diploma Apprentice (Civil Engineering), 22 Diploma Apprentice (Mechanical Engineering) and 2 more posts.

### Q3. How do I apply for CSPGCL Recruitment 2026?

Apply online through the link in the Important Links table, on or before 1 December 2026.

### Q4. What is the salary for this recruitment?

It depends on the post: Rs. 12,300 per month for Graduate Apprentice (Civil Engineering), Rs. 12,300 per month for Graduate Apprentice (Mechanical Engineering), Rs. 12,300 per month for Graduate Apprentice (Electrical Engineering), Rs. 12,300 per month for Graduate Apprentice (CSE/EEE/IT), Rs. 10,900 per month for Diploma Apprentice (Civil Engineering).

### Q5. What qualification is needed for this recruitment?

Graduate Apprentice: graduate in Engineering/Technology in the relevant faculty from a recognised institute/university. Diploma Apprentice: diploma in the relevant faculty from the State Technical Board/recognised institute. The requirement for each post is in the eligibility table above.

### Q6. Are there other conditions for this recruitment besides qualification and age?

Yes. Candidate must be a native resident of Chhattisgarh State. Applicant must meet all DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) requirements on NATS 2.0 by the contract date, or selection will be cancelled. Selection follows Chhattisgarh Government reservation rules.

### Q7. Who cannot apply for this recruitment?

Candidates who have completed five years since passing the qualifying examination as on 01 October 2026.

### Q8. How will candidates be selected for this recruitment?

Merit list prepared on the basis of marks obtained in the qualifying examination and marks obtained in the interview. Merit list from marks in the qualifying examination and marks in the interview. Main selection list and waiting list published on the company website. Selected candidates informed by email to appear on a fixed date for contract.

### Q9. What are the terms of engagement for this recruitment?

Apprenticeship training is for one year only. No entitlement to any temporary or permanent employment in CSPGCL after training. Engagement is against seats falling vacant in financial year 2026-27.

### Q10. Which places are covered by CSPGCL Recruitment 2026?

Hasdeo Thermal Power Station, Korba West, Shyama Prasad Mukherjee Thermal Power Station, Korba East, Atal Bihari Vajpayee Thermal Power Station, Marwa Tendubhatha, District Janjgir-Champa, Hasdeo Bango Hydro Electric Plant, Machadoli.
