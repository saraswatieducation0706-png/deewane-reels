Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), Panchkula Unit, has released the notification for 49 Trainee Engineer posts. Eligible candidates can apply online at bel-india.in till 10 December 2026. 

## BEL Quick Summary

2026 · 49 Posts

COMPANY NAME Bharat Electronics Limited

POST NAME Trainee Engineer

QUALIFICATION B.Tech/B.E, B.Sc

VACANCY 49 Posts

AGE LIMIT 28 Years (Max)

LAST DATE 10-12-2026

Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL), a Navaratna company and a Govt. of India Enterprise under the Ministry of Defence, is engaging 49 Trainee Engineers for its Panchkula Unit at Rs. 40,000 per month consolidated remuneration in the first year. The posts are in Electronics (28), Mechanical (20) and Electrical (1) for holders of a 4-year B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Engineering degree with pass class. Applications are online only, and the last date is 10 December 2026.

## About BEL

The notice describes Bharat Electronics Limited as a Navaratna company and India's premier professional electronics company with over 350 products in areas such as military communication, radars, naval systems, C4I systems, weapon systems, homeland security, telecom and broadcast, electronic warfare, tank electronics and electro optics. This recruitment is for its Panchkula Unit. The advertisement was released on 25 November 2026.

## Vacancy Details

The notice lists 49 Trainee Engineer posts in three disciplines: 28 + 20 + 1 = 49. The number of posts may vary based on the actual requirement at the time of selection.

Post| Vacancies| UR| EWS| OBC| SC| ST  
---|---|---|---|---|---|---

## Who Can Apply

Experience required for all three disciplines: Nil. Only Indian citizens can apply. The cut-off date for deciding the maximum permissible age and qualification is 01 November 2026.

Post| Qualification| Discipline  
---|---|---  
Trainee Engineer – Electronics| B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Engineering degree (4 yr course) from an AICTE approved college/Institute or a recognised University in relevant discipline with PASS Class| Electronics / Electronics & Communication / Electronics & Telecommunication / Telecommunication / Communication  
Trainee Engineer – Mechanical| Same degree and pass class as above| Mechanical  
Trainee Engineer – Electrical| Same degree and pass class as above| Electrical / Electrical & Electronics  
  
  * Applicants whose discipline or specialisation in the degree certificate does not tally with the prescribed discipline will not be considered for selection.
  * For all posts, candidates should have qualified from an AICTE/UGC approved college/institute or a recognised university.
  * Where CGPA or credits are used, candidates must attach the university's formula for converting CGPA/credits to percentage, and proof of percentage issued by the university/institution/college along with the awarded class.
  * The provisional or final degree certificate must be attached as proof of completion, showing class and percentage of marks.
  * Trainee Engineers/Officers presently working in BEL are not eligible to apply for the post of Trainee Engineer/Officer.



### Age Limit and Relaxation

The age limit should not be more than 28 years for General/EWS candidates as on 01.11.2026. This applies to all three posts.

Category| Relaxation  
---|---  
OBC (NCL)| 3 years  
SC/ST| 5 years  
PwBD (40% disability or more)| 10 years, in addition to the relaxation of the category above  
Ex-servicemen| As per Govt. guidelines  
  
The upper age limit admissible considering all relaxations is 50 years. SSLC/SSC/ISC mark card or any other valid document is accepted as proof of date of birth. Candidates seeking relaxation must attach a copy of the certificate with the application and show the original in case of selection or at any later stage for verification.

For the discipline-specific eligibility of each post, candidates should refer to the official notification.

## Pay and Tenure

Designation| Year| Consolidated Remuneration per Month  
---|---|---  
TE-I| 1st year| Rs. 40,000/-  
TE-II| 2nd year| Rs. 45,000/-  
TE-III| 3rd year| Rs. 50,000/-  
  
Additional benefits of Rs. 12,000 per year are payable towards insurance premium, attire allowance, stitching charges, footwear allowance, etc. The same pay applies to all three disciplines.

  * Trainee Engineer-I will be engaged initially for two years, which may be extended by one more year based on project requirement and individual performance.
  * After two years, based on organisational requirement, the Trainee Engineer may be advanced to Project Engineer-I if the performance assessment scores are 80% and above (aggregate of both years), attendance is above 80% in the preceding year with good general discipline, and there are no adverse remarks in the preceding 2 years.
  * A Trainee Engineer who fails to meet these criteria will continue as Trainee Engineer-III for one further year, subject to requisite approval, and the engagement ends on completion of that year.
  * After advancement to Project Engineer-I, the engagement is for 3 years, which may be extended up to a maximum of one year with the competent authority's approval.
  * Project Engineers completing an overall tenure of 4 years are entitled to a Retention Bonus of Rs. 25,000 for every completed year, payable at the end of 4 years, i.e. Rs. 1,00,000.
  * Gratuity is admissible as per the applicable provisions of the BEL Employees' Gratuity Scheme.
  * Engineers posted to far-flung and tough locations are paid 10% of the consolidated remuneration as Tough Location Allowance, or such other allowance as may be notified.



## Application Fee

Category| Fee  
---|---  
Trainee Engineer – I (applicants not exempted)| Rs. 150 + 18% GST  
SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-servicemen| Exempted  
  
The fee is paid through SBI Collect, online or at an SBI branch. Fees once paid are not refunded under any circumstances, and no cheque, DD or cash is accepted.

## How to Apply for BEL Recruitment 2026

  1. Confirm that you meet the eligibility criteria in the advertisement as on 01.11.2026.
  2. Apply online by clicking the relevant link provided against the advertisement.
  3. Pay the fee through SBI Collect unless exempt: go to [www.onlinesbi.sbi](https://www.onlinesbi.sbi), select State Bank Collect, accept the terms and click Proceed.
  4. Select state of corporation: All India; type: PSU – Public Sector Undertaking; then Bharat Electronics Limited and Submit.
  5. Select payment category "Trainee Engineer – Panchkula Unit" and complete the payment. To pay at an SBI branch, select the SBI branch option, print the challan, and deposit the fee with bank charges, taking the bank official's seal and signature.
  6. Enter the SBI Collect Reference No. in the application form, using the same mobile number and email ID as in the application.
  7. Enter all details correctly and upload the prescribed documents. Changes are not permitted after submission.



No manual, paper or hard copy application is accepted. The notice prints no start date for applications.

### Documents to Be Uploaded

  * Recent photograph and signature.
  * Self-attested copy of SSLC/Matriculation certificate (proof of age).
  * Self-attested copy of PUC/12th Class/Diploma marks card.
  * Self-attested copies of degree marks cards showing all semesters/years passed.
  * Self-attested copies of provisional/final degree certificate(s).
  * CGPA to percentage conversion formula certificate, duly certified by the university/institution, wherever applicable.
  * Self-attested copy of community/caste certificate for OBC (NCL)/SC/ST candidates.
  * Self-attested copy of disability certificate for PwBD candidates.
  * Post-qualification experience certificates, if applicable (teaching is not considered relevant experience).
  * Fee payment receipt, if applicable.
  * Government issued ID card such as Aadhaar/PAN/Voter ID/Driving Licence.
  * Any other relevant certificates.



## Selection and Interview

Selection is through a written test of 100 marks. Candidates are selected in order of merit, category-wise and discipline-wise. Each incorrect response carries 1/4th negative marking. The notice does not mention the written test date or the exam centres.

Category| Minimum Qualifying Marks (Written Test)  
---|---  
General/OBC-NCL| 35%  
SC/ST/PwBD| 30%  
  
In case of a tie in marks, the overall percentage of marks in the qualifying degree decides the merit. Screening and selection are based on the details given in the application form. No travelling expenses or allowance are given for attending the written test. The results of the final selection will be made available on the BEL website, and the provisional appointment order will be issued to selected candidates by email.

  * Candidates need at least one valid email ID, entered compulsorily in the application form and kept active for at least one year. It cannot be changed once entered, and all correspondence is by email only.
  * Requests to change the declared category, mailing address, email ID or post will not be entertained.
  * The caste/disability certificate must be in the format available on the BEL website; otherwise the candidate is considered under the General category, provided all other General criteria are met.
  * OBC (NCL) candidates must produce a certificate issued on or after 01.11.2025; EWS candidates must produce a certificate issued for the year 2026/2026-27.
  * Candidates working in PSUs/Govt./quasi-Govt. organisations must submit a No Objection Certificate; those unable to produce it will not be considered for the selection process.
  * Current BEL Trainee Engineers/Project Engineers/Field Operation Engineers must give prior intimation to the HR department of their Unit/SBU/CSG. If a current BEL Trainee Engineer applies, the application will be summarily rejected.
  * Appointment of the provisionally selected candidate is subject to being found medically fit by the Company's medical authorities.
  * Postings are based on the Company's requirement, and candidates may be deployed on other projects, outstation sites or units. Canvassing in any form results in disqualification.
  * Any revision, corrigendum or time extension will be hosted on the careers section of the BEL website. For queries, write to [[email protected]](https://www.freejobalert.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#cd9d86859f9f888e998d8f8881e38e82e38483).

## BEL Recruitment 2026 - FAQs

### Q1. How many vacancies are there in BEL Recruitment 2026 at Panchkula?

There are 49 Trainee Engineer posts: 28 in Electronics, 20 in Mechanical and 1 in Electrical. The number may vary based on actual requirement at the time of selection.

### Q2. What is the last date to apply for the BEL Trainee Engineer posts?

The last date of receipt of application is 10 December 2026. The advertisement was released on 25 November 2026.

### Q3. What is the qualification required for BEL Trainee Engineer?

A B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Engineering degree (4 yr course) from an AICTE approved college/institute or a recognised university in the relevant discipline with pass class. Experience required is Nil.

### Q4. What is the age limit for BEL Trainee Engineer?

The age should not be more than 28 years for General/EWS candidates as on 01.11.2026. OBC (NCL) get 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, and PwBD an additional 10 years.

### Q5. What is the salary of a BEL Trainee Engineer?

Consolidated remuneration is Rs. 40,000 per month in the 1st year, Rs. 45,000 in the 2nd year and Rs. 50,000 in the 3rd year. Rs. 12,000 per year is also payable towards insurance premium, attire allowance, stitching charges, footwear allowance, etc.

### Q6. What is the application fee for BEL Trainee Engineer?

The fee is Rs. 150 + 18% GST, paid through SBI Collect. SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen are exempted from the fee.

### Q7. How do I apply for BEL Trainee Engineer 2026?

Apply online through the link provided against the advertisement. No manual, paper or hard copy application is accepted.

### Q8. What is the selection process for BEL Trainee Engineer?

Selection is through a written test of 100 marks with 1/4th negative marking for each incorrect response. Minimum qualifying marks are 35% for General/OBC-NCL and 30% for SC/ST/PwBD.

### Q9. Is the BEL Trainee Engineer job permanent?

The notice describes an engagement: Trainee Engineer-I is engaged initially for two years, extendable by one more year. After two years the engineer may be advanced to Project Engineer-I for 3 years, extendable by up to one year.

### Q10. Can current BEL Trainee Engineers apply?

No. Trainee Engineers/Officers presently working in BEL are not eligible, and such applications will be summarily rejected.
