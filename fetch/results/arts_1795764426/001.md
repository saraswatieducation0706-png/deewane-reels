## BPSC Quick Summary

2026 · 935 Posts

COMPANY NAME Bihar Public Service Commission

POST NAME Assistant Education Development Officer

QUALIFICATION Any Bachelors Degree

VACANCY 935 Posts

AGE LIMIT 21 - 37 Years

LAST DATE 30-12-2026

BPSC AEDO Recruitment 2026: The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released Advertisement No. 16/2026 dated 26 November 2026 for recruitment to 935 Assistant Education Development Officer (AEDO) posts under the Education Department, Government of Bihar. The new notification replaces the previously published Advertisement No. 87/2025. Candidates possessing a Bachelor's Degree or an equivalent qualification from a recognized university can apply. The minimum age is 21 years, while the maximum age is 37, 40 or 42 years depending on the candidate's category. Selected candidates will receive a starting basic salary of Rs. 29,200 under Pay Level 5. Online applications will be accepted from 1 December to 31 December 2026 through the official BPSC recruitment portal at [bpsconline.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsconline.bihar.gov.in/).

The Bihar AEDO Recruitment 2026 includes 374 Unreserved, 93 EWS, 150 SC, 10 ST, 168 EBC, 112 BC and 28 Backward Class Women vacancies. Of the total 935 posts, 319 are earmarked for women under the notified 35% horizontal reservation. The selection process consists of a Preliminary Examination followed by a Main Written Examination. The preliminary test carries 150 marks, while the Main Examination includes General Language, General Studies and General Aptitude papers. Candidates must pay the prescribed examination fee by 30 December 2026 and submit their completed application by 31 December 2026. Applicants should carefully verify eligibility, reservation certificates, examination requirements and online application instructions before applying.

## BPSC AEDO Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The Bihar Public Service Commission has prescribed a two-stage written examination for Assistant Education Development Officer recruitment.

  1. Online application and eligibility scrutiny.
  2. Preliminary Competitive Examination.
  3. Shortlisting for the Main Examination.
  4. Main Written Examination.
  5. Preparation of the merit list based on Main Examination marks.
  6. Original document verification.
  7. Final recommendation for appointment.



Important: The notification does not prescribe a personal interview for the AEDO recruitment. The Preliminary Examination is used for screening, while final merit is based on the Main Examination under the prescribed rules.
