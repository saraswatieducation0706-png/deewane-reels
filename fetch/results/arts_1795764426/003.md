BPSC Agriculture Department Recruitment 2026: The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released Advertisement Nos. 17/2026 to 21/2026, dated 26 November 2026, for recruitment to 147 Assistant Director posts under the Agriculture Department, Government of Bihar. The vacancies are available in Agricultural Engineering, Statistics, Measurement and Weights, Chemistry and Plant Protection specializations under the Bihar Agriculture Service. Candidates possessing the prescribed Bachelor's Degree in Agricultural Engineering, Agricultural Statistics, Science, Engineering, Chemistry, Agricultural Science or Plant Protection-related disciplines can apply according to the selected advertisement. The minimum age is 21 years, while the maximum age ranges from 37 to 42 years depending on category. All advertised posts carry Pay Level 9. Online applications will be accepted from 15 December 2026 to 12 January 2027 through [bpsconline.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsconline.bihar.gov.in/).

The BPSC Assistant Director Recruitment 2026 includes 50 Agricultural Engineering, 36 Statistics, 26 Measurement and Weights, 21 Chemistry and 14 Plant Protection vacancies. The selection process includes a written examination and interview, with weightage for relevant experience as prescribed in the notification. The written examination covers General Hindi, General Knowledge and specialization-specific subjects. Candidates must pay the prescribed examination fee by 11 January 2027 and complete final application submission by 12 January 2027. Applicants should check the qualification requirements, category-wise vacancies, reservation certificates and application instructions before submitting the form.
