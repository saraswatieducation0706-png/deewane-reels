## How to Apply

Here is the complete procedure to apply for DRDO LRDE Apprentice Recruitment 2026. The candidates should read the complete procedure to apply in the official notification from the links given below. 

The application procedure for is not fully online. Candidates are advised to follow the detailed instructions given below. 

  1. Candidates must be registered on NATS portal (https://nats.education.gov.in) for Graduate/Technician Apprentices or NAPS portal (https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in) for ITI Trade Apprentices.
  2. Appear for Walk-in Interview on the scheduled date at LRDE, C V Raman Nagar, Bengaluru – 560093, Karnataka.
  3. Report before 1000 hrs on the respective interview date: 15 December 2026 for Graduate Engineering and Technician (Diploma Engg); 16 December 2026 for Graduate General and Trade Technician (ITI).
  4. Bring self-attested copies and originals of: NATS/NAPS profile printout, 10th Mark sheet and Certificate, Mark sheets of qualifying examinations (all semesters/year-wise), Degree/Diploma/ITI Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Caste Certificate (if applicable), Two recent Passport size photographs, Bank Passbook (Aadhaar seeded account).
  5. Selection will be based on academic merit / written test / interview as required, subject to satisfactory document verification.
