Irrigation and Flood Control Department (I&FC;) has released the official notification for the recruitment of 28 Assistant Engineer /Assistant Surveyor of Works posts. The last date to submit the offline application is 27 December 2026. 

## I&FC Quick Summary

2026 · 28 Posts

COMPANY NAME Irrigation and Flood Control Department

POST NAME Assistant Engineer (Civil)/Assistant Surveyor of Works (Civil), Assistant Engineer (Mech.)/Assistant Surveyor of Works (Mech.)

QUALIFICATION Diploma, B.Tech/B.E

VACANCY 28 Posts

AGE LIMIT 56 Years (Max)

LAST DATE 27-12-2026

Irrigation and Flood Control Department (I&FC), Delhi, has invited applications for 28 posts in 2 roles. The posts include Assistant Engineer (Civil)/Assistant Surveyor of Works (Civil), Assistant Engineer (Mech.)/Assistant Surveyor of Works (Mech.). Applications are to be sent offline and must reach the organisation by 27 December 2026.

The posts are for Irrigation and Flood Control Department, Government of NCT of Delhi. Qualification required: Central/State Govt officers holding analogous posts, or serving in Pay Level-6 (Rs.35400-112400) with qualifying service: Civil 3 years (degree) / 7 years (diploma) as Junior Engineer or equivalent; Mech. 4/8 years (degree/diploma) plus a degree/diploma in Mechanical/Elect. Engg. The pay is Level-07 (7th CPC) for both posts. Post-wise eligibility, dates and the steps to apply are given below.

  * How to apply: Offline, by post or in person as the notification says
  * Pay: Pay Level-07 (7th CPC) for both posts
  * Fee: No application fee is mentioned in the notification
  * Age limit: Maximum 56 years (as on the closing date)
  * Place: Irrigation and Flood Control Department, Government of NCT of Delhi
