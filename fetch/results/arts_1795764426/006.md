## How Will GSSSB Select Candidates?

  * Single-stage competitive MCQ examination (CBRT/OMR) with Part-A and Part-B in one paper
  * Provisional result and merit list on total marks, with separate qualifying standard in Part-A and Part-B
  * About twice the category-wise posts called for original certificate verification
  * Selection list and waiting list on merit, category-wise, with candidates' order of preference of offices
  * Examination is one paper in two parts: Part-A (general subjects, 90 marks) and Part-B (subject-related, 120 marks)
  * Wrong answer carries negative marking of one-fourth of the marks for the question
  * Provisional answer key is published after the exam and objections are considered before the final answer key
  * If a question is cancelled in the final answer key, marks are calculated pro-rata on the remaining questions
  * Five percent marks are added for widow women candidates and for recognised sportspersons



## GSSSB Exam Pattern 2026

Part / Paper | Subject | Questions | Marks | Duration  
---|---|---|---|---  
Part-A | Reasoning and Data Interpretation (30), Mathematical Aptitude (30), Constitution of India, Current Affairs, Gujarati and English Comprehension (30) | 90 | 90 | 3 hours (180 minutes) for Part-A and Part-B together  
Part-B | Subject related to the prescribed qualification and its application | 120 | 120 | 3 hours (180 minutes) for Part-A and Part-B together  
  
  * Qualifying standard 40 percent separately in Part-A and in Part-B for all categories
  * Exam centres, dates, timings and admit card instructions will be put on https://gsssb.gujarat.gov.in

## How to Apply for GSSSB Recruitment 2026 Online

  2. Go to Online Application, click Apply, select GSSSB and click the post name under Advt. 472/202627.
  3. Click Apply now, enter Registration No. and date of birth and click Apply with OTR.
  4. Fill SSC details, bank account details (with first page of passbook uploaded as JPEG, JPG or PNG) and Educational Details.
  5. Accept the Declaration, save, and note the Application Number.
  6. Upload photograph and signature (JPG, up to 15 kb) using Application Number and date of birth.
  7. Confirm the application to get the Confirmation Number; no change is possible after confirmation.
  8. Pay the fee online by 18-12-2026 (23:59 hrs) and print the application form and fee receipt.
  9. Apply on or before 15 December 2026.

## GSSSB Recruitment 2026 - FAQs

### Q1. What is the last date to apply for GSSSB Recruitment 2026?

The last date is 15 December 2026. Applications opened on 1 December 2026.

### Q2. How many vacancies are there in GSSSB Recruitment 2026?

There are 227 vacancies: 169 Laboratory Technician, Class-3 (Additional Director, Public Health, Gandhinagar), 36 Laboratory Technician, Class-3 (Additional Director, Medical Services, Gandhinagar), 22 Laboratory Technician, Class-3 (Director, Employees' State Insurance Scheme, Ahmedabad).

### Q3. How do I apply for GSSSB Recruitment 2026?

Apply online through the link in the Important Links table, on or before 15 December 2026.

### Q4. What is the application fee for this recruitment?

Unreserved category: Rs. 500. Reserved category (all women, SEBC, SC, ST, EWS, disabled and ex-servicemen candidates): Rs. 400.

### Q5. What is the age limit for this recruitment?

The age limit is 18 to 36 years (as on 15 December 2026). Relaxation: General women 5 years (maximum 41 years), Reserved category men 5 years (maximum 41 years), Reserved category women 10 years (maximum 45 years), General category men with disability 10 years (maximum 45 years), General category women with disability 15 years (maximum 45 years), Reserved category men with disability 15 years (maximum 45 years), Reserved category women with disability 20 years (maximum 45 years), Ex-servicemen Period of service rendered plus up to 3 years (maximum 45 years).

### Q6. What is the salary for this recruitment?

It depends on the post: Rs. 40,800 per month fixed pay for first five years for Laboratory Technician, Class-3 (Additional Director, Public Health, Gandhinagar), Rs. 40,800 per month fixed pay for first five years for Laboratory Technician, Class-3 (Additional Director, Medical Services, Gandhinagar), Rs. 40,800 per month fixed pay for first five years for Laboratory Technician, Class-3 (Director, Employees' State Insurance Scheme, Ahmedabad).

### Q7. What qualification is needed for this recruitment?

Bachelor's degree in Chemistry / Microbiology / Bio-chemistry / Bio-technology, and a Diploma in Laboratory Technician or Medical Laboratory Technician (or equivalent course) from a recognised institution; basic computer knowledge; adequate knowledge of Gujarati or Hindi or both. The requirement for each post is in the eligibility table above.

### Q8. Are there other conditions for this recruitment besides qualification and age?

Yes. Citizen of India, or having nationality as per Rule 7 of the Gujarat Civil Services Classification and Recruitment (General) Rules, 1967. Reservation is only for reserved-category candidates belonging to Gujarat. Candidates without a computer certificate may apply but must produce a pass certificate in a basic computer knowledge test before appointment. Only one application should be made; the last confirmed application is valid and the others are cancelled.

### Q9. Who cannot apply for this recruitment?

Candidates presently debarred by GPSC, other recruitment boards or government bodies have their application cancelled automatically. SEBC or EWS candidates without the prescribed certificate get no age relaxation and are treated as general category candidates. Married women SEBC candidates submitting a non-creamy layer certificate based on husband's income have their application cancelled.

### Q10. How will candidates be selected for this recruitment?

Single-stage competitive MCQ examination (CBRT/OMR) with Part-A and Part-B in one paper. Provisional result and merit list on total marks, with separate qualifying standard in Part-A and Part-B. About twice the category-wise posts called for original certificate verification. Selection list and waiting list on merit, category-wise, with candidates' order of preference of offices.
