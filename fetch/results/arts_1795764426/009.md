DRDO LRDE Apprentice Recruitment 2026: The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), Electronics and Radar Development Establishment (LRDE), Bengaluru, has released Advertisement No. LRDE/APP-TRG/2026-27/W-1 for engagement of 105 Apprentice Trainees under the Apprentices Act, 1961, for the financial year 2026-27. The vacancies include 43 Graduate Engineering Apprentices, 26 Graduate General Apprentices, 16 Technician Diploma Apprentices and 20 ITI Trade Apprentices. Candidates possessing a relevant Engineering Degree, General Graduation, Engineering Diploma or NCVT ITI certificate can attend the walk-in selection process. The minimum age is 18 years as on 1 December 2026, and the monthly stipend ranges from Rs. 9,600 to Rs. 12,300 according to qualification. The walk-in selection will be held on 15 and 16 December 2026 at LRDE, CV Raman Nagar, Bengaluru.

The LRDE Bengaluru Apprentice Recruitment 2026 offers one year of apprenticeship training in Engineering, Science, Commerce, Computer Applications, Library Science and technical trades. Only candidates who passed their essential qualifying examination in 2024 or later through a regular course are eligible. Applicants must register on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) portal for Degree and Diploma apprenticeships or the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) portal for ITI trades. Selection may involve academic merit, a written test or an interview, depending on the vacancies and number of eligible candidates. Graduate Engineering and Diploma candidates must report on 15 December, while Graduate General and ITI candidates must report on 16 December 2026, before 10:00 AM.

### Can B.Tech Candidates Apply for DRDO LRDE Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

Yes. Candidates holding a recognized BE or B.Tech degree in one of the advertised Engineering disciplines can attend the Graduate Engineering Apprentice walk-in on 15 December 2026 if they meet the remaining eligibility conditions.

### Can Diploma Holders Apply for DRDO Apprentice Recruitment 2026?

### Can ITI Candidates Apply?

No. The official advertisement explicitly states that candidates possessing higher qualifications are not eligible for the advertised apprenticeship training.

No. Candidates who have already undergone apprenticeship training or are currently undergoing apprenticeship training under another organization are not eligible.
