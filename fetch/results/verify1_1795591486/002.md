## NHPC Recruitment Through GATE 2026 Notification

NHPC Limited, a Navratna Central Public Sector Enterprise operating in the hydropower sector, issued Advertisement No. NH/Talent Acquisition/09/2026 on 25 November 2026 for the recruitment of Trainee Engineers and Officers in five technical disciplines.

Unlike recruitment drives that conduct a separate written examination, NHPC will shortlist candidates using normalized marks obtained out of 100 in the relevant GATE 2026 paper. Shortlisted applicants will participate in a Group Discussion and Personal Interview for final selection.

## NHPC Trainee Engineer and Officer Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility for NHPC recruitment through GATE 2026 depends on the discipline selected by the candidate. Applicants must possess the prescribed full-time regular educational qualification with the required marks and a valid GATE 2026 score in the corresponding examination paper.

Only Indian nationals are eligible. Candidates appearing in the final year of their qualifying examination cannot apply for this recruitment.

## NHPC Trainee Engineer Selection Process Through GATE 2026

NHPC will prepare the final selection merit list using GATE 2026 marks, Group Discussion and Personal Interview performance. GATE marks carry the highest weightage of 75%, while Group Discussion and Personal Interview together account for the remaining 25%.

### Selection Weightage

Selection Component | Weightage  
---|---  
GATE 2026 Normalized Marks (Out of 100) | 75%  
Group Discussion | 5%  
Personal Interview | 20%  
Total | 100%  
  
### Shortlisting and Final Merit List

  1. GATE-based shortlisting: Candidates will be shortlisted according to normalized marks obtained in the corresponding GATE 2026 paper.
  2. Shortlisting ratio: Normally, candidates numbering up to five times the vacancies will be shortlisted. The ratio may be exceeded when candidates in the same discipline and category obtain equal marks at the shortlisting threshold.
  3. Consent form: Shortlisted applicants must submit their consent to participate in Group Discussion and Personal Interview within the notified schedule.
  4. Group Discussion and Personal Interview: Candidates must participate in both selection stages and satisfy the applicable combined qualifying marks.
  5. Psychometric assessment: Shortlisted candidates may undergo an online or offline psychometric assessment, the results of which will be used as an input during the interview.
  6. Final merit: Selection will be based on composite merit, reservation requirements, eligibility verification and availability of vacancies.
  7. Medical fitness: Selected candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical fitness standards of NHPC.



Failure to submit the consent form or attend the Group Discussion and Personal Interview will result in cancellation of candidature.

### Minimum Qualifying Marks for Group Discussion and Interview

Category | Minimum Combined GD and PI Marks  
---|---  
UR / OBC (NCL) / EWS | 40%  
SC / ST / PwBD | 30%  
  
NHPC has not prescribed a separate qualifying cutoff for Group Discussion. Candidates must secure the applicable combined qualifying marks in Group Discussion and Personal Interview to be considered for empanelment.

If candidates obtain identical final marks, preference in the merit list will be given to the older candidate. Meeting the minimum qualifying marks alone does not guarantee selection.

### Q1. How many vacancies are available in NHPC Recruitment Through GATE 2026?

NHPC has announced 47 Trainee Engineer and Officer vacancies across Electronics and Communication, Environment, Information Technology, Geology and Geophysics disciplines. The total includes seven backlog vacancies.

### Q2. What is the last date to apply for NHPC Trainee Engineer Recruitment 2026?

The last date to apply online is 26 December 2026 at 5:00 PM. Online registration begins on 27 November 2026 at 10:00 AM through nhpcindia.com.

### Q3. Is GATE 2026 mandatory for NHPC Trainee Engineer and Officer posts?

Yes. Candidates must possess a valid GATE 2026 score in the examination paper corresponding to their selected discipline. GATE scores from other years are not accepted.

### Q4. Can MCA graduates apply for NHPC Recruitment 2026?

Yes. Candidates with a recognized full-time regular MCA qualification and a valid GATE 2026 score in Computer Science and Information Technology (CS) can apply for Trainee Engineer/Officer (IT), subject to the prescribed marks and other eligibility requirements.

### Q5. Can M.Sc. Environmental Science graduates apply for NHPC recruitment?

Yes. Candidates possessing a full-time regular M.Sc. in Environmental Science can apply for Trainee Engineer/Officer (Environment), provided they have the required educational marks and a valid GATE 2026 score in the ES paper.

### Q6. What is the salary for this recruitment?

It depends on the post: Rs. 50,000-3%-1,60,000 (IDA) for Trainee Engineer (E&amp;C), Rs. 50,000-3%-1,60,000 (IDA) for Trainee Engineer/ Officer (Environment), Rs. 50,000-3%-1,60,000 (IDA) for Trainee Engineer/ Officer (IT), Rs. 50,000-3%-1,60,000 (IDA) for Trainee Officer (Geology), Rs. 50,000-3%-1,60,000 (IDA) for Trainee Officer (Geophysicist).

The maximum age limit is 30 years as of 26 December 2026. Eligible reserved category candidates can claim age relaxation as specified in the official recruitment advertisement.

### Q8. Are there other conditions for this recruitment besides qualification and age?

Yes. Only Indian Nationals can apply. Valid GATE-2026 scorecard in the corresponding paper is required. Selected candidates should be medically fit as per extant NHPC guidelines. SC/ST and PwBD candidates with 50% marks in qualifying examination are eligible to apply.

Final year appearing students. Candidates without a valid GATE-2026 score (GATE score of any other year is not valid). Candidates from disciplines other than those listed for each post.

Shortlisting on normalized GATE-2026 marks (out of 100) in the corresponding paper. Group Discussion and Personal Interview. Possible online/offline Psychometric Assessment. Empanelment on weighted score: GATE-2026 75%, Group Discussion 05%, Personal Interview 20%.
