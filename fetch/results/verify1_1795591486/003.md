## NHPC Trainee Officer Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must be Indian nationals, possess the prescribed full-time regular educational qualification and have a valid score in the relevant UGC NET June 2026 or CLAT PG 2026 examination. Final-year students are not eligible to apply.

### Educational Qualification

Post Name | Required Qualification | Minimum Marks  
---|---|---  
Trainee Officer (HR) | Two-year full-time regular postgraduate qualification in prescribed HR, personnel management or industrial relations disciplines; eligible MSW, MHROD, MBA HRD or MBA with HR as a major subject | 60%  
Trainee Officer (PR) | Two-year full-time regular PG Degree or PG Diploma in Communication, Mass Communication, Journalism or Public Relations | 60%  
Trainee Officer (Law) | Full-time regular three-year LLB or five-year integrated professional Law degree from a recognized university or institution recognized by the Bar Council of India | 60%  
  
SC, ST and PwBD candidates are eligible to apply with a minimum of 50% marks in the qualifying educational examination. Only the disciplines specifically mentioned in the official advertisement are accepted.

### Detailed Qualification for HR Trainee Officers

Candidates applying for the HR discipline must possess one of the following prescribed full-time regular two-year qualifications from a recognized Indian university or eligible AICTE-approved institution:

  * Postgraduate Degree, Postgraduate Diploma or Postgraduate Program in Management with specialization in Human Resource, Human Resource Management, Human Resource Management and Labor Relations, Industrial Relations, Personnel Management, Personnel Management and Industrial Relations, or Industrial Relations and Personnel Management.
  * Master of Social Work with specialization in Personnel Management and Industrial Relations.
  * Master of Human Resource and Organizational Developments (MHROD), now known as MBA HRD.
  * MBA with specialization in Human Resource as a major subject.



Candidates holding qualifications in disciplines not specifically listed in the notification are not eligible, even if those disciplines are considered equivalent.

## NHPC Trainee Officer Selection Process 2026

The selection process consists of shortlisting based on UGC NET June 2026 or CLAT PG 2026 marks, followed by a Group Discussion and Personal Interview. The qualifying examination score carries 75% weightage in the final selection process.

### Selection Weightage

Selection Component | Weightage  
---|---  
UGC NET June 2026 / CLAT PG 2026 Score | 75%  
Group Discussion | 5%  
Personal Interview | 20%  
Total | 100%  
  
### Shortlisting and Final Selection

  1. Candidates will be shortlisted based on their marks in the relevant UGC NET June 2026 or CLAT PG 2026 examination.
  2. Normally, candidates numbering up to five times the available vacancies will be shortlisted for Group Discussion and Personal Interview.
  3. Candidates securing equal marks in the same discipline and category at the shortlisting threshold may be called even if the prescribed ratio is exceeded.
  4. Shortlisted candidates must submit the required consent form and attend the Group Discussion and Personal Interview.
  5. Candidates may also be required to undergo an online or offline psychometric assessment, the results of which will be used as an input during the interview.
  6. Final selection will be based on composite merit, applicable reservation requirements, document verification and other prescribed eligibility conditions.
  7. Selected candidates must satisfy NHPC or NHDC medical fitness requirements.



### Minimum Qualifying Marks for GD and Interview

Category | Minimum Combined GD and PI Score  
---|---  
UR / OBC (NCL) / EWS | 40%  
SC / ST / PwBD | 30%  
  
There is no separate qualifying cutoff for Group Discussion. Candidates must secure the prescribed minimum combined score in Group Discussion and Personal Interview to be considered for empanelment.

If two or more candidates secure the same final marks, the older candidate will receive preference in determining the merit position.
