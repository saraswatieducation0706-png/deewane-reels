## Selection Procedure

The detailed selection procedure has been mentioned in the official notification of WBSETCL Recruitment 2026. But we are giving you here the short summary of the selection procedure. The candidates are advised to refer to official notification for selection procedure. 

Selection Procedure:

  1. Computer Based Test (CBT) / Online Test: All candidates will appear in a CBT of 90 minutes duration. For all posts except Office Executive: Domain Knowledge (50 marks), Bengali/Nepali Test (10 marks), General English (10 marks), General Aptitude (15 marks) — Total 85 marks. For Office Executive: Bengali/Nepali Test (10 marks), General English (10 marks), Arithmetic (20 marks), Reasoning (15 marks), General Knowledge (10 marks), Proficiency in MS-Office (20 marks) — Total 85 marks. Negative marking @ 0.33 per wrong answer. Minimum qualifying marks apply part-wise and aggregate-wise by category (UR/EWS: 40%, OBC/SC: 35%, ST/PwBD: 30%).
  2. Personal Interview: Candidates shortlisted in 1:3 ratio per category based on CBT performance. Total marks for interview: 15.
  3. Pre-Employment Medical Examination: Selected candidates must qualify in the pre-employment medical examination as per company rules.
  4. Police Verification & Certificate Verification: Medically fit candidates undergo Police Verification, Caste Certificate verification, and other relevant certificate verifications before issuance of Appointment Letter.
  5. Final Selection: Based on combined performance in CBT and Interview, fulfilment of eligibility criteria, medical fitness, available vacancies, and existing reservation rules of Govt. of West Bengal.
