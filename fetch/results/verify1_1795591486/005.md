West Bengal State Electricity Transmission Company Limited (WBSETCL), Kolkata, has invited applications for 202 posts in 5 roles under Advt No. REC/2026/04. The posts include Special Technical Assistant, Junior Engineer (Elect.) Gr.-II, Junior Surveyor, Office Executive and 1 more. The online application is open from 25 November 2026 to 15 December 2026.

The posts are for Different offices of WBSETCL; transferable anywhere in West Bengal, Linesman training in different EHV Sub-stations of WBSETCL. Qualification required: 3-year full-time Diploma in Electronics & Tele-Communication/Radio/Wireless, Electrical or Survey Engineering (technical posts); Graduation with 50% plus a computer course or BBA/BCA/B.Sc options (Office Executive); Madhyamik plus 2-year NCVT ITI in Wireman or Electrician (Linesman). The pay is Level-6 Rs. 36800-106700 (Special Technical Assistant, Junior Engineer, Junior Surveyor); Level-4 Rs. 29000-84500 (Office Executive); Level-3 Rs. 23400-68900 (Linesman). The application fee is Nil. Selection is by computer Based Test (CBT), personal Interview (15 marks) and pre-employment Medical Examination. Post-wise eligibility, dates and the steps to apply are given below.

## How Will WBSETCL Select Candidates?

  * Computer Based Test (CBT)
  * Personal Interview (15 marks)
  * Pre-employment Medical Examination
  * Police Verification and Caste/other certificate verification
  * Shortlisting for Interview in 1:3 ratio in each category based on CBT performance
  * Personal Interview carries 15 marks
  * Final selection on combined CBT and Interview performance, medical fitness, vacancies and reservation rules
  * CBT and Personal Interview to be held in Kolkata; CBT may be held in multiple cities of West Bengal

## WBSETCL Recruitment 2026 - FAQs

### Q1. What is the last date to apply for WBSETCL Recruitment 2026?

The last date is 15 December 2026. Applications opened on 25 November 2026.

### Q2. How many vacancies are there in WBSETCL Recruitment 2026?

There are 202 vacancies: 40 Special Technical Assistant, 100 Junior Engineer (Elect.) Gr.-II, 2 Junior Surveyor, 20 Office Executive, 40 Linesman.

### Q3. How do I apply for WBSETCL Recruitment 2026?

Apply online through the link in the Important Links table, on or before 15 December 2026.

### Q4. What is the application fee for this recruitment?

All category of candidates: Nil.

### Q5. What is the age limit for this recruitment?

The age limit is 18 to 37 years (as on 1 January 2026). Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3 years, PwBD Maximum age limit 45 years irrespective of category, Exempted Category (EC) Up to 45 years, Ex-Servicemen Service period (min. 6 months continuous) deducted from actual age; resultant age may exceed the maximum by up to 3 years, WBSETCL Employees Up to 45 years irrespective of caste category.

### Q6. What is the salary for this recruitment?

It depends on the post: LEVEL-6 Rs. 36800 – 106700 for Special Technical Assistant, LEVEL-6 Rs. 36800 – 106700 for Junior Engineer (Elect.) Gr.-II, LEVEL-6 Rs. 36800 - 106700 for Junior Surveyor, LEVEL-4 Rs. 29000 – 84500 for Office Executive, LEVEL-3 Rs. 23400 – 68900 (during the training period, they will be paid the Basic & DA only) for Linesman.

### Q7. What qualification is needed for this recruitment?

3-year full-time Diploma in Electronics & Tele-Communication/Radio/Wireless, Electrical or Survey Engineering (technical posts); Graduation with 50% plus a computer course or BBA/BCA/B.Sc options (Office Executive); Madhyamik plus 2-year NCVT ITI in Wireman or Electrician (Linesman). The requirement for each post is in the eligibility table above.

### Q8. Are there other conditions for this recruitment besides qualification and age?

Yes. Applicants must be Domicile of West Bengal; Domicile Certificate to be submitted at Personal Interview. Ability to read, speak and write in Bengali / Nepali language. Employees of Govt. Departments/PSUs/Autonomous Bodies must produce NOC at Personal Interview. Final semester/year students may appear in CBT but must submit final result on the date of interview.

### Q9. Who cannot apply for this recruitment?

Degree from Open University / Distance Learning / Part time / Sandwich Course, except existing WBSETCL employees. SC/ST/OBC with caste certificates issued outside West Bengal are treated as General with no relaxation.

### Q10. How will candidates be selected for this recruitment?

Computer Based Test (CBT). Personal Interview (15 marks). Pre-employment Medical Examination. Police Verification and Caste/other certificate verification.
