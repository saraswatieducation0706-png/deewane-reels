## NHPC Trainee Engineer Selection Process 2026

The NHPC Trainee Engineer Selection Process 2026 consists of shortlisting through GATE 2025 or GATE 2026 scores, followed by Group Discussion and Personal Interview. Candidates with a valid score in the corresponding GATE paper will be considered for shortlisting based on the normalised marks out of 100. The final merit will be prepared using the prescribed weightage for GATE, Group Discussion and Personal Interview.

### 1\. GATE Score-Based Shortlisting

NHPC will shortlist candidates based on the best valid normalised marks obtained in GATE 2025 or GATE 2026. Ordinarily, candidates up to five times the number of advertised vacancies in each category and discipline may be called for the next selection stages. If candidates have equal marks at the shortlisting boundary, all such candidates may be called according to the notification.

### 2\. Group Discussion

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for Group Discussion. Group Discussion carries 5% weightage in the final selection score. There is no separate qualifying cut-off for Group Discussion, but candidates must satisfy the combined minimum qualifying requirements for Group Discussion and Personal Interview.
