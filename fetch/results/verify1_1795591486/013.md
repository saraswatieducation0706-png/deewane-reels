Life at THDCIL

A promising organization THDC India Limited (THDCIL) - a joint venture between Government of India and Government of Uttar Pradesh- has marked its potential through it significant contribution to the area of hydro power generation, irrigation and supply of drinking water across the northern states of India. We claim the pride of construction and maintenance of the Tehri Dam- India’s highest dam as well as many others that add to the portfolio of THDCIL’s efficiency. THDCIL foresees a future through its diversification in the Thermal, Wind and Solar power generation. At this stage, the flourish calls for more hands to join in the venture and further the development.

THDCIL is committed to provide the best available work life for its employees. In addition to ensuring safe and aesthetically pleasing work places, THDCIL actively encourages a culture of mutual respect and trust amongst peers, superiors and subordinates. THDCIL harbours a boundary-less culture wherein one and all can participate in doing every bit for betterment of the organization through the Suggestion Mela.

Employees are sent for training and conferences in India and abroad to hone their skills in the field of their work. At THDC, enmeshed with work various in-house competitions and occasions as Hindi Pakhwada, Yoga Day, Independence Day, Foundation Day, Environment Day, Vigilance Awareness Week, Biodiversity Day and others are observed and participated with great enthusiasm. The festivals of Holi, Dusshera, DeepawaliMela are celebrated in the THDCIL family and strengthens the bond amongst all. Musical Nights, Kavi Sammelans, Drama, Dance Performances, organized at the THDCIL township hall- highlights moments of rejoice and recreation.

Far removed from the hustle and bustle of cities, our townships are the epitome of serenity, natural beauty and close community living. Provisions for sports opportunity to participation in various sports events is always encouraged by THDCIL. A range of welfare and recreation facilities including schools, hospitals and social clubs- Officers’ Club, Ladies Club- are provided at the township to enhance the quality of life and the well-being of employees and their families and also helps employees to maintain healthy work-life balance. Summer camps are organized for wards of the employees with special attractions as swimming, horse riding, rifle shooting, classical dance etc. workshops. There are provision for gymnasium, billiard, table tennis and badminton in the premises.

All said and done- THDCIL is a Miniratna Schedule ‘A’ Enterprise- which again in itself is a testament of its potential of Generating Power and Transmitting Prosperity- as our motto. THDCIL is one of the best pay masters in the Indian power sector with the best policies and provisions to support all the THDCIL-citizens.

However, in its endeavour for excellence, THDCIL also plays its part of giving back to the society through various awareness programs, rehabilitation schemes and sustainable development.

We, the THDCIL family invite you to be a part of us and contribute towards building a nation imbibed in values and a forerunner in the progress arena.
