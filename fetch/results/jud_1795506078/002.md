Kerala High Court Recruitment 2026 for 21 posts of Civil Judge (Junior Division). Candidates with LLB Can Apply Online. The online application opens on 23-11-2026, and closes on 16-12-2026. The candidate shall apply online through Kerala High Court official website highcourt.kerala.gov.in. 

Update 19 Nov 2026: Applications re-opened - apply online from 23 November 2026 to 16 December 2026. The new notification dated 19-11-2026 changes the vacancies to 21, removes the 3-year practice requirement and sets the preliminary exam for 07-02-2027 (tentative).

Kerala High Court (HCKL) Civil Judge (Junior Division) Notification 2026 has been released on the official website highcourt.kerala.gov.in. A total of 21 vacancies have been announced through the Kerala High Court Civil Judge Recruitment 2026.

Candidates up to 35 years of age (with applicable relaxations) who have a Degree in Law recognized by the Bar Council of India, obtained at least one year before the date of the notification, are eligible to apply for these Civil Judge (Junior Division) posts. The online application process for Kerala High Court Recruitment 2026 starts on 23 November 2026 and will remain open until 16 December 2026. Candidates who applied under the earlier notification dated 30-03-2026 must submit a fresh application, but need not pay the fee again if it was duly paid.

Candidates can download the official notification PDF and apply online through the direct links provided below in this article.

## Kerala High Court Recruitment 2026 - Selection Process

  * Preliminary Examination (Objective Type Screening Test) - 100 multiple choice questions, 2 marks each, 200 marks, 1 mark deducted for each wrong answer, 2.5 hours; on 07-02-2027 (tentative). It is for shortlisting only (ratio 1:10), and its marks do not count in the final merit.
  * Main Examination (Written Examination - 4 papers, 100 marks each, 3 hours each)
  * Viva-Voce - 50 marks; cut-off 40% for General and OBC, 35% for SC/ST and Persons with Benchmark Disabilities



Selected candidates become Trainee Judicial Officers. They complete one year of training at the Kerala Judicial Academy, then one year of Law Clerkship, and are paid half the remuneration of a Civil Judge (Junior Division) during this time. Probation is two years.
