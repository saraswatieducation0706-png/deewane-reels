## Selection Procedure

The detailed selection procedure has been mentioned in the official notification of Mumbai Port Graduate Apprentice Recruitment 2026. But we are giving you here the short summary of the selection procedure. The candidates are advised to refer to official notification for selection procedure. 

Selection Procedure:

  * Merit-Based Selection: Selection of Graduate Apprentices will be based on merit, determined by the percentage of total marks obtained in the Degree examination.
  * Tie-Breaking: If two candidates secure the same marks, the older candidate will be placed higher in the merit list.
  * Preference: Preference will be given to wards of MbPA employees. Separate merit lists will be prepared for wards of MbPA employees and other candidates.
  * Merit List Display: Merit list(s) will be displayed on the notice board at ATC office, Bhandar Bhavan, 3rd Floor, N.V. Nakhwa Marg, Mazgaon, Mumbai – 400010, and uploaded on MbPA’s website www.mumbaiport.gov.in.
  * Document Verification: Listed candidates will be called for verification of all original documents at ATC office. Date of reporting will be displayed on the notice board and MbPA website.
  * Absence: If a candidate on the merit list is absent on the specified date of reporting, their candidature will be rejected and the next candidate on the panel merit list will be considered.
  * Medical Examination: Selected candidates will have to undergo a Medical Examination and produce a fitness certificate from the Port Authority Hospital.

## How to Apply

Here is the complete procedure to apply for Mumbai Port Graduate Apprentice Recruitment 2026. The candidates should read the complete procedure to apply in the official notification from the links given below. 

The application procedure for is not fully online. Candidates are advised to follow the detailed instructions given below. 

  1. Register online through NATS 2.0 MIS web portal (https://nats.education.gov.in) and take a print of the e-mail copy mentioning apprentice enrollment/registration form.
  2. Download the application form from MbPA’s website (www.mumbaiport.gov.in) under ‘People & Career > Jobs > Advertisements’ menu.
  3. Fill the application form in all respects and submit the duly filled form by hand delivery or by post to: ATC, Bhandar Bhavan, 3rd Floor, N.V. Nakhwa Marg, Mazgaon (East), Mumbai – 400010, along with required documents and application fee receipt on or before 18.12.2026 up to 5:00 pm.
  4. Pay the application fee of Rs. 100/- via NEFT to: The Board of Mumbai Port Authority, Account No. 10996685430, SBI, Mumbai Main Branch, IFSC: SBIN0000300. Attach UTR receipt with the application.
  5. Physically handicapped candidates are exempted from paying the application fee.
