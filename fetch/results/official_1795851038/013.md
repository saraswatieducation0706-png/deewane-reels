         Advertisement for engagement of Graduate/Technician Apprentices

CSIR-National Metallurgical Laboratory, Jamshedpur is one of the global leaders in
metallurgy and materials research, backed-up by substantial share of research publications
in peer reviewed journals and intellectual property products generated in metallurgical and
materials science and technology.

The Director, CSIR-NML intends to engage Graduate Apprentice Trainees as well as
Technician Apprentice Trainees through Walk-in-Interview as per the scheme provided
under the Apprenticeship Act, 1961 and the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 of Government of
India, as per details mentioned below:

OTHER ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS:

   1. Only those candidates are eligible who have passed the Degree / Diploma during the
      years 2022/2023/2024/2025/2026;

   2. Each Candidate must have verified NATS ID and APAAR ID.

   3. In case the date of declaration of result is not mentioned in the Mark Sheet, the
      candidate must submit a certificate mentioning the date of publication of result
      issued by the Principal of the Polytechnic / College / Institute from where the
      candidate pursued his / her Diploma / Degree course, along with application form.

   4. One candidate can apply against any one of the discipline.

   5. The candidates should NOT have undergone any Apprenticeship training earlier or
      pursuing Apprenticeship Training as per the Apprentices Act, 1961/1971 as
      amended from time to time.

AGE LIMIT:

  •     For Technician Apprenticeship: Minimum 18 years and maximum 24 years as on the
        date of interview;

  •     For Graduate/Degree Apprenticeship: Minimum 21 years and maximum 26 years as
        on date of interview

  •     Relaxation in upper age limit: Relaxation by 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, for
        the posts reserved for them). Candidates belonging to PwBD Categories shall be given
        age relaxation up to 10 years (up to 15 years for SC/ST and up to 13 years for OBC
        (Non-Creamy Layer) Candidates.

RESERVATION OF VACANCIES:

  Guidelines under Apprentices Act on reservation for SC/ST/OBC (NCL)/PwBD will be
  followed. Those who are claiming reservation under SC/ST/OBC/PwBD shall produce
  certificate issued by appropriate authority in the format prescribed by Govt. of India,
  failing which they will be considered as ‘General’ category candidates only, if otherwise
  eligible.

Period of Apprenticeship Training: 12 months

GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS:

   1.     Candidates must have completed their diploma or graduation within the last five
          years.
   2.     Diploma/Engineering Graduate candidates are advised to register their name on
          NATS 2.0 Portal (https://nats.education.gov.in)
   3.     The candidates aspiring for Apprentice training must get enrolled themselves in
          the NATS (Diploma/Graduate candidates) portal within five years after passing
          their qualifying examination;
   4.     Candidates who have registered themselves in NATS/NAPS Portal and fulfil the
          eligibility criteria as indicated above will be considered for appearing in the walk-
          in-interview.
   5.     The duration of the training for all the above branches is one (01) year.
   6.     He/she must submit the original College Leaving Certificate (C.L.C.) at the time of
          joining which will be retained during the tenure of his/her apprenticeship;
   7.     Candidates should not have work experience of one year or more and not have
          undergone Apprenticeship training elsewhere & also student should not have
          completed five years after passing qualifying examination;
   8.     The original documents including educational qualification, Birth/SSLC
          certificate, caste, community and experience certificate, etc., in respect of selected
          candidate will be verified at the time of reporting. Joining at CSIR-NML as
          apprentice trainee is subject to verification of certificates of HSC, +2, ITI, Diploma
          and Degree and other relevant documents.

 9.      Candidates possessing higher professional qualifications such as B.E or
         equivalent (for technician apprentices), M. Tech or equivalent (for graduate
         apprentices) shall not be considered.
 10.     Based on the performance of the candidates in walk-in-interview, candidates will
         be selected for undergoing Apprenticeship Training. Engagement letter will be sent
         to the selected candidates through their email and they have to submit acceptance
         within 03 days and also join within 07 days after receipt of the engagement letters.
         If they fail to submit acceptance or joining, their selection shall be treated as
         cancelled and no further query will be entertained in the matter;
 11.     The candidates who are selected as Apprentice must possess an Aadhar linked
         Bank Account in his/her own name preferably a nationalized bank with internet
         banking facilities and PAN card to receive stipend from CSIR-NML;
 12.     No accommodation will be provided. Selected candidates will have to make their
         own arrangements for stay during their engagement;
 13.     Candidates are advised to ensure that they are medically fit as per medical
         standard required for the concerned discipline, failing which their candidature is
         liable to be cancelled.
 14.     No TA/DA will be paid to the candidates for attending the Walk-in-Interview/Test.
 15.     Any corrigendum/addendum/notification etc. or updates with regard to this
         advertisement shall be made available on our website: https://nml.res.in/
 16.     Candidates are advised to regularly visit our website, as all future correspondence
         and latest information with regard to final result shall be posted on our website.
 17.     Applicant’s candidature is provisional and subject to subsequent verification of
         certificates and testimonials. In case, it is detected at any stage of engagement or
         thereafter, that a candidate does not fulfil the eligibility norms and/or that he/she
         has furnished any incorrect/false information/certificate/documents or has
         suppressed any material fact(s), his/her candidature will stand cancelled. If any
         of these shortcomings is/are detected even after engagement, his/her engagement
         will liable to be terminated forthwith without giving any notice.
 18.     The decision of Competent Authority, CSIR-NML, Jamshedpur will be final and
         binding on all candidates and on all matters relating to eligibility, acceptance or
         rejection of the applications, mode of selection, cancellation of the selection
         process either in part or full, etc. No correspondence/ Interim queries will be
         entertained in this regard. Filling up of the seats is solely at the discretion of the
         Competent Authority CSIR-NML, Jamshedpur based on suitability of candidates
         and no claim will arise for engagement, if some of these seats are not filled due to
         unsuitability/ insufficient number of candidates.
 19.     The engagement of students as apprentice trainee is purely temporary for a period
         of one year only and not for regular employment in CSIR-NML. Upon completion
         of the Apprenticeship period, CSIR-NML shall neither have obligation to offer
         employment to such apprentices nor an Apprentice can claim for employment on
         the grounds of completion of Apprenticeship.
