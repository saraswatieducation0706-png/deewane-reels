Karnataka State Fire and Emergency Services (KSFES), Bengaluru, has invited applications for 16 posts of Fire Station Officer under Advt No. ಸಿಬ್ಬಂದಿ(7)18/ನೇಮಕಾತಿ/2026. The online application is open from 23 November 2026 to 22 December 2026.

The post is for Karnataka. Qualification required: Direct candidates: science degree with Chemistry as one of the subjects from a recognised university. In-service candidates (Leading Fireman, Driver Mechanic, Fireman Driver, Fireman, Fitter Class-II of the department only): degree in any subject from a recognised university, with not less than 8 years of service. The pay is scale Rs. 54175-99400. The application fee is Rs. 750 for General Merit and other categories (Category-2A / 2B / 3A / 3B). Selection is by physical Standard Test (PST), physical Fitness Test (PFT) and written competitive examination (Paper-1 and Paper-2, offline OMR). Eligibility, dates and the steps to apply are given below.

## Eligibility Criteria

  * Qualification: Direct candidates: science degree with Chemistry as one of the subjects from any recognised university; In-service candidates (Leading Fireman, Driver Mechanic, Fireman Driver, Fireman and Fitter Class-II of KSFES only): degree in any subject from any recognised university
  * Experience: In-service candidates: not less than 08 years of service in the cadres of Leading Fireman, Driver Mechanic, Fireman Driver, Fireman and Fitter Class-II; probation satisfactorily completed; no departmental enquiry or criminal case at the time of applying
  * Age limit: 21 to 33 years (on the last date fixed for submitting applications)
  * Age relaxation: General Merit (Direct) Maximum 31 years, SC/ST/Cat-1/2A/2B/3A/3B (Direct) Maximum 33 years, General Merit (In-service) Maximum 43 years, SC/ST/Cat-1/2A/2B/3A/3B (In-service) Maximum 45 years, Ex-servicemen Period of service rendered plus additional 03 years, All Limits include one-time 5-year relaxation under G.O. dated 29.01.2026



Other conditions:

  * Only male candidates may apply
  * Minimum height 168 cm, chest 81 cm with 5 cm expansion, minimum weight 50 kg
  * Must pass at least 3 of 5 physical fitness tests
  * Compulsory Kannada test unless Kannada studied as first or second language in SSLC or Kannada test already passed
  * Must be an Indian citizen



## Who Cannot Apply for KSFES Recruitment 2026

  * Candidates awaiting results of the qualifying examination on the last date
  * Candidates with knock knees, flat foot or bow legs
  * Persons permanently debarred by UPSC, KPSC, another State PSC or KEA
  * Persons convicted of an offence involving moral turpitude or temporarily debarred, until Government reconsiders
  * In-service candidates facing departmental enquiry or criminal case at the time of applying

  * 06 months practical training at fire stations
  * Candidates failing training examinations will be discharged from service
  * National Pension Scheme (NPS) applies

## How Will KSFES Select Candidates?

  * Physical Standard Test (PST)
  * Physical Fitness Test (PFT)
  * Written competitive examination (Paper-1 and Paper-2, offline OMR)
  * Compulsory Kannada Language Test (for non-exempted candidates)
  * Medical examination before the Medical Board
  * Verification of original documents
  * PST and PFT conducted by a committee chaired by the DGP and DG; only PST qualifiers take PFT
  * Only PFT qualifiers are admitted to the written examination
  * Paper-1 General Knowledge and Paper-2 Science and Aptitude, 100 marks each
  * Total marks of Paper-1 and Paper-2 count for merit
  * Candidates in the provisional selection list appear before the Medical Board (vision, hearing, chest X-ray)



## KSFES Exam Pattern 2026

Part / Paper| Subject| Questions| Marks| Duration  
---|---|---|---|---  
Paper-1| General Knowledge (Kannada or English)| 100| 100| 1 1/2 hours (90 minutes)  
Paper-2| Science and Aptitude (Kannada or English)| 100| 100| 1 1/2 hours (90 minutes)  
  
  * Offline OMR type examination
  * Absence in Paper-1 or Paper-2 leads to disqualification
  * Written examination at selected centres in Bengaluru city
  * Compulsory Kannada test: 150 marks, minimum 50 to qualify; marks not counted for selection
