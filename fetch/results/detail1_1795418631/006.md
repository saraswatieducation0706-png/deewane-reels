Mumbai Port Authority, Mechanical & Electrical Engineering Department, Apprentice Training Centre (MbPA) has published the Recruitment 2026 notification for 48 Graduate Apprentice posts. Offline applications will be accepted till 18 December 2026. For complete details, candidates can visit mumbaiport.gov.in. 

## MbPA Quick Summary

2026 · 48 Posts

COMPANY NAME Mumbai Port Authority, Mechanical & Electrical Engineering Department, Apprentice Training Centre

POST NAME Graduate Apprentice

QUALIFICATION B.A, BCA, B.Com, B.Sc

VACANCY 48 Posts

AGE LIMIT 18 - 25 Years

LAST DATE 18-12-2026

Mumbai Port Authority (MbPA) has 48 Graduate Apprentice seats for 2026-27, each with a 12-month training period and a stipend paid as Rs. 6150 Establishment Share plus Rs. 6150 Government Share (direct through DBT). Applicants must first register on the NATS 2.0 MIS portal (nats.education.gov.in), then send the application form offline to the Apprentice Training Centre in Mazgaon, Mumbai. The last date is 18 December 2026, up to 5.00 pm.

## About the Training Centre

The recruitment notice is issued by the Apprentice Training Centre (ATC) of the Mechanical & Electrical Engineering Department of Mumbai Port Authority. The ATC office is at Bhandar Bhavan, Mazgaon, Mumbai. Apprentices are engaged under the Apprentices Act, 1961 and receive practical training at various sections and departments of MbPA.

## Who Can Apply

### Qualification

Graduate in any stream (viz. BE, B.Com., BA, Bsc, BCA, etc.) from a recognized University or Institute as prescribed by All India Council for Technical Education or University Grants Commission. The applicant should NOT have completed three years after passing the minimum qualification examination as on 01/11/2026.

### Age

The minimum age is 18 years and the upper age limit is 25 years. The application form asks for age on 01/11/2026. Upper age limit relaxation is given as per Government Rules, on submission of the relevant certificate issued by the Competent Authority.

Category | Relaxation  
---|---  
Scheduled Caste / Scheduled Tribe | 5 years  
OBC | 3 years  
Divyang / PWD | 10 years  
  
### Who Cannot Apply

An applicant who has already undergone, or is undergoing, apprenticeship training in the same trade under the Apprentice Act, 1961 in any organization is NOT eligible to apply.

### Reservation

Reservation shall be as per prevailing practice in MbPA. Candidates claiming SC, ST or OBC reservation must produce the original Caste Certificate issued by the competent authority in the format prescribed by the Government of India. Candidates applying in the Physical Handicap category must produce the original certificate issued by the competent medical board. Reservation goes to Persons with Benchmark Disabilities under section 34 of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, with not less than 40% of the relevant disability, certified by a Medical Board appointed by the Central/State Govt. Without a copy of the certificate, the application is considered in the General Category.

Candidates should check the official notification for any further eligibility detail.

## Stipend and Training Period

The training period is 12 months. The stipend per month is Establishment Share Rs. 6150 and Government Share Rs. 6150 (direct through DBT). Each apprentice gets Casual Leave of 12 days and Medical Leave of 15 days during the training. An apprentice who cannot complete the course in the prescribed period because of illness or other circumstances beyond his/her control will have the period extended as per the Apprentices Act, 1961. After satisfactory completion of training, a Certificate of Proficiency is issued by the Board of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT).

## How to Apply for MbPA Graduate Apprentice

### Part 1: NATS registration

  1. Register online on the NATS 2.0 MIS web portal (<https://nats.education.gov.in>) and take a print of the e-mail copy mentioning the apprentice enrollment / registration form.



### Part 2: Application to MbPA

  1. With a valid enrollment / registration on the NATS portal, download the application form from MbPA's website ([www.mumbaiport.gov.in](https://www.mumbaiport.gov.in)) > People & Career > Jobs > Advertisements menu.
  2. Fill in the form in all respects. Paste a passport size photo, mention the NATS registration number and the UTR number of the fee payment.
  3. Pay the Rs. 100 application form cost by NEFT only (details below).
  4. Submit the filled form, with the required documents and the application fee receipt, by hand or by post to the Apprentice Training Centre, Bhandar Bhavan, 3rd floor, N. V. Nakhwa Marg, Mazgaon (East), Mumbai - 400010, on or before 18 December 2026, 5.00 pm.



Application forms are not sent by post and are not sold by MbPA at the Apprentice Training Centre office. Applications received after the due date, or incomplete, will be rejected with no further correspondence. MbPA is not responsible for postal delay or loss.

## Selection Process

Selection is on merit, followed by document verification and a medical examination.

### Merit list

Merit is based on the percentage of total marks obtained in the Degree examination. If two candidates have the same marks, the older candidate is placed above in the merit list. Preference is given to wards of MbPA employees, and a separate merit list is prepared for them and for other candidates. The merit lists are displayed on the notice board at the ATC office and uploaded on [www.mumbaiport.gov.in](https://www.mumbaiport.gov.in).

### Document verification and selection procedure

Candidates in the merit list are called for verification of all original documents and the selection procedure at the ATC office. The reporting date will be displayed on the ATC notice board and on [www.mumbaiport.gov.in](https://www.mumbaiport.gov.in). A listed candidate who is not present and duly prepared on that date is rejected, and the next candidate on the panel merit list is considered. No representation is entertained in that case.

### Medical examination

A selected candidate has to undergo a Medical Examination and produce a fitness certificate from the Port Authority Hospital.

## Important Instructions

  * After selection, the candidate enters a Contract of Apprenticeship in the prescribed form, to be registered with BOAT, Western Region, Mumbai. For a minor, the guardian / parents enter the contract.
  * Final selection is subject to registration of the Contract of Apprenticeship with BOAT (WR).
  * An apprentice who abandons the training before completion is liable to pay the cost of training (maximum three months' stipend) or an amount decided by the Regional Directorate of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (RDSDE); legal action is started if it is not paid.
  * MbPA has no obligation to offer employment during or after the apprenticeship, and the apprentice cannot claim employment on the ground of completing it.
  * Apprentices must observe the conduct rules applicable to workmen in the Port Authority; disputes are referred to BOAT (WR), Mumbai.
  * No daily allowance, conveyance allowance or travelling allowance is paid to candidates called for document verification and Medical Examination.
  * Candidature is cancelled if the required original documents are not produced or a discrepancy is found. A candidate found not to meet the eligibility criteria at any stage is summarily dismissed without notice.
  * MbPA does not undertake to send a reply or call to candidates who are not selected, and its decision on eligibility, acceptance or rejection of applications and mode of selection is final.
  * Help desk: 02266566637 / 6625, 10:00 a.m. to 17:00 p.m. (except Saturday, Sunday and holidays).

Register on the NATS 2.0 MIS portal, download the form from the MbPA website (People & Career > Jobs > Advertisements), then submit it with documents and the fee receipt by hand or post to the ATC office, Mazgaon (East), Mumbai – 400010.

### Q8. How are Graduate Apprentices selected by MbPA?

Selection is on merit, based on the percentage of total marks in the Degree examination, with preference to wards of MbPA employees. Listed candidates then go through document verification and a medical examination.

### Q9. Does the apprenticeship lead to a job at MbPA?

No. MbPA has no obligation to offer employment during or after the apprenticeship, and the apprentice cannot claim employment on that ground.

### Q10. Can someone who has done apprenticeship earlier apply?

An applicant who has already undergone, or is undergoing, apprenticeship training in the same trade under the Apprentice Act, 1961 in any organization is not eligible.
