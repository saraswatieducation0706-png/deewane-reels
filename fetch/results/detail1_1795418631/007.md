## BEML Executive Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Candidates applying for BEML Executive Recruitment 2026 must fulfil the educational qualification, post-qualification experience and age requirements specified for the position code. Eligibility will be determined as on 3 December 2026.

### Educational Qualification

Most technical positions require a full-time, first-class Engineering degree in the relevant discipline from a recognised university or institution. The accepted specialisations include Mechanical, Electrical, Electrical and Electronics, Automobile, Electronics and Communication, Computer Science, Information Technology, Thermal Engineering and other disciplines according to the post.

Certain positions require specialised qualifications, such as a postgraduate degree in Hindi or English with the specified language combination, a translation qualification, or recognised safety-related credentials.

The notification defines first class as at least 60% aggregate marks across all semesters or years. The qualifying-mark requirement is relaxed by five percentage points for eligible SC, ST and PwD candidates. Applicants with CGPA or credit-based grading must provide the university-approved conversion formula.

Important: A general Engineering degree does not make a candidate eligible for every advertised position. Applicants must possess the discipline-specific qualification and technical experience stated against their chosen position code.

## BEML Executive Recruitment 2026 Experience Requirements

BEML requires relevant post-qualification experience for most executive positions. Experience acquired before obtaining the essential qualification will not be counted towards the prescribed requirement.

Post / Grade | Minimum Post-Qualification Experience  
---|---  
Engineer - Grade II | 2 Years  
Assistant Manager - Grade III | 4 Years  
Assistant Manager - Medical, Position Code 123 | Nil  
Manager - Grade IV | 8 Years  
Senior Manager - Grade V | 11 Years  
Assistant General Manager - Grade VI | 13 Years  
Deputy General Manager - Grade VII | 16 Years

These are the grade-level minimum requirements. The detailed advertisement prescribes additional relevant-domain experience for individual positions, including rolling stock projects, engineering design, diesel engines, manufacturing, cybersecurity, testing, commissioning and other specialised functions.

### Important Experience Conditions

  * Only relevant experience gained after obtaining the prescribed qualification will be considered.
  * Private-sector experience must meet the employment conditions prescribed in the advertisement.
  * Freelance experience will not be counted as post-qualification experience.
  * Candidates applying for DGM and GM positions must provide their current reporting structure.
  * Private-sector applicants for General Manager must satisfy the company's specified annual turnover requirement of at least ₹1,000 crore.
  * Experienced candidates must provide a description of at least 150 words for each relevant experience entry in the online application.



The experience description should clearly explain the projects, assignments, responsibilities, position held and duration. BEML warns that unclear or irrelevant information may lead to rejection of the application.

## BEML Executive Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

BEML will shortlist eligible candidates for assessment based on the applications received and the relevant eligibility criteria. The company may restrict the number of candidates called for assessment and increase the qualifying percentage depending on the number of applicants.

The recruitment process includes the following applicable stages:

  1. Screening and shortlisting of eligible applications.
  2. Assessment of shortlisted candidates through the process notified by BEML.
  3. Verification of original documents and eligibility claims.
  4. Pre-employment medical examination.
  5. Final selection and appointment, subject to satisfactory verification and medical fitness.



The advertisement does not prescribe a single common written-examination pattern for all 83 executive vacancies. Candidates should follow subsequent official notices for assessment dates and methods.

## BEML Executive Recruitment 2026 NOC Requirements

Candidates employed in Central or State Government departments, autonomous bodies, quasi-government organisations and public sector undertakings must follow the proper-channel or No Objection Certificate requirements prescribed by BEML.

Such candidates may forward a printout of their online application through the proper channel within 10 days after the advertisement's closing date, addressed to the Assistant General Manager, Recruitment Cell, BEML Soudha, BEML Limited, 23/1, 4th Main, S.R. Nagar, Bengaluru - 560027.

Alternatively, they may produce a position-specific NOC from their present employer at the assessment stage. The notification also explains circumstances in which applicants without an NOC may attend the interview, subject to furnishing a proper relieving letter at joining and forgoing certain past-service benefits or pay protection.
