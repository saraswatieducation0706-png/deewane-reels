2026 · 127 Posts

COMPANY NAME Jammu and Kashmir Services Selection Board

Update: By its corrigendum dated 19.11.2026, JKSSB has set the new dates for online applications and online fee payment: 23.11.2026 (12:00 AM) to 22.12.2026 (11:59 PM). Update: JKSSB has postponed the start of online applications for Advertisement Notification No. 10 of 2026 by its notice dated 15.11.2026; the fresh dates have now been notified by the corrigendum dated 19.11.2026.

The Jammu & Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB) has released Advertisement Notification No. 10 of 2026, dated 21.10.2026, for JKSSB Junior Engineer Mechanical Recruitment 2026. The board is filling 127 vacancies of Junior Engineer (Mechanical) in the Public Works (R&B) Department of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir through direct recruitment.

Candidates who hold a 3-year Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from a Government recognized institute, an Indian University Degree in Mechanical Engineering, or AMIE Section (A&B) India (a Degree or Diploma in Aeronautical, Automobile or Production Engineering is considered equivalent), and who hold a valid J&K Domicile Certificate, can apply. Online applications can be submitted from 23.11.2026 (12:00 AM) to 22.12.2026 (11:59 PM). Applications are accepted in online mode only, through the JKSSB website, www.jkssb.nic.in.

## JKSSB Junior Engineer Mechanical Eligibility Criteria

To meet the JKSSB Junior Engineer Mechanical eligibility, candidates must satisfy the domicile, qualification and age conditions as on the cut-off date.

  * The candidate must be a Domicile of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir and must hold a valid Domicile Certificate issued by the Competent Authority in the prescribed format on or before the cut-off date, i.e. the last date for online applications (22.12.2026).
  * The candidate must hold the educational qualification given below, acquired on or before the cut-off date.
  * Candidates seeking reservation must hold a valid category certificate in the prescribed format as on the cut-off date and upload it with the online application.
  * Candidates claiming equivalent qualification, where applicable, must produce the relevant Equivalence Certificate. The final decision on such candidates rests with the Board.
  * A candidate with a qualification other than the one prescribed is not eligible, and the candidature will be summarily rejected at any stage.

## JKSSB Junior Engineer Mechanical Selection Process

The selection for the post of Junior Engineer (Mechanical) will be made on the basis of merit obtained in the written/OMR examination only. Shortlisted candidates are then called for Document Verification.

  * The examination consists of Objective Type, Multiple Choice Questions, set in English only.
  * There is negative marking of one-fourth of the marks assigned to each question for every wrong answer.
  * The syllabus will be notified separately on the official JKSSB website.
  * The date and centres of the examination will be notified separately on www.jkssb.nic.in. No request for a change of centre is entertained.
  * The number of questions, total marks and duration of the examination are not specified in the notification.
  * Candidates shortlisted for Document Verification must appear with original documents and a self-attested photostat copy of each document, the certificates and the online application form.
  * No TA/DA is paid for the written test or Document Verification.

## How to Apply for JKSSB Junior Engineer Mechanical Recruitment 2026

Candidates must apply online only. No other mode of application is accepted, and the instructions for filling the online application will be made available separately on the Board's website.

### JKSSB Junior Engineer Mechanical Apply Online

  1. Check the eligibility conditions for domicile, qualification and age as on the cut-off dates.
  2. Visit the official website www.jkssb.nic.in and follow the online application instructions published for this notification.
  3. Fill in the application form carefully. Write the name, date of birth, father's name and mother's name exactly as in the matriculation certificate.
  4. Upload the live photograph and signature (size 10 KB to 20 KB), the category certificate (maximum 200 KB, PDF) and the self-attested qualification certificates (maximum 200 KB, PDF), as required.
  5. Pay the application fee online through Net Banking, Credit Card or Debit Card.
  6. Review the form, submit it, note the application number and take a printout to produce at Document Verification. Do not send the hard copy to the JKSSB office.

## JKSSB Junior Engineer Mechanical Recruitment 2026 FAQs

### Q1. What is JKSSB Junior Engineer Mechanical Recruitment 2026?

It is the direct recruitment of Junior Engineer (Mechanical) in the Public Works (R&B) Department of Jammu & Kashmir, advertised by JKSSB through Advertisement Notification No. 10 of 2026.

### Q2. How many vacancies are available in JKSSB Junior Engineer Mechanical Recruitment 2026?

There are 127 vacancies (OM-51, SC-10, ST1-13, ST2-13, OBC-10, ALC/IB-5, RBA-13, EWS-12).

### Q3. What is the starting date to apply online for JKSSB JE Mechanical 2026?

Online applications start on 23.11.2026 (12:00 AM), as per the JKSSB corrigendum dated 19.11.2026.

### Q4. What is the last date to submit the online application form?

The last date to submit the online application form and pay the fee online is 22.12.2026 (11:59 PM), as per the JKSSB corrigendum dated 19.11.2026.

### Q5. What is the required educational qualification for JKSSB JE Mechanical?

Applicants must possess a 3-year Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from a recognized institute or university, or a Degree in Mechanical Engineering / AMIE Section (A&B) India. A Degree or three-year Diploma in Aeronautical, Automobile or Production Engineering is considered equivalent to a Degree or Diploma in Mechanical Engineering.

### Q6. What is the age limit for Open Merit candidates?

The maximum age limit for Open Merit candidates is 40 years as on 1st January 2026, with admissible age relaxations provided for reserved categories.

### Q7. What is the application fee for General category candidates?

General and other unreserved category candidates are required to pay an application fee of Rs. 600/- online.

### Q8. What is the salary structure for the Junior Engineer post?

Selected candidates for the Junior Engineer (Mechanical) post will be placed in Level-6 with a salary range of Rs. 35,400 to Rs. 1,12,400.

### Q9. What is the selection procedure for this recruitment?

The selection of candidates will be strictly based on the merit obtained in the objective type Written or OMR-based examination.

### Q10. Are non-domiciles of Jammu and Kashmir eligible to apply?

No, candidates must be a Domicile of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir and possess a valid Domicile Certificate issued by the competent authority.
