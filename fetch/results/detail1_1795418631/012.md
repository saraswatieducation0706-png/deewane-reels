## Selection Procedure

The detailed selection procedure has been mentioned in the official notification of KEA Industrial Expansion Officer Recruitment 2026. But we are giving you here the short summary of the selection procedure. The candidates are advised to refer to official notification for selection procedure. 

Selection Procedure:

  * Paper-1 (General Knowledge): 100 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours duration.
  * Paper-2 (Specific Paper): 100 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours duration; syllabus published separately.
  * Kannada Language Test: 150 marks, minimum 50 marks required to qualify (qualifying in nature; marks not counted for selection or seniority). Compulsory unless exempted.
  * Verification of Original Documents.
  * Minimum 35% marks in the competitive exam is compulsory.
  * Negative marking: 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer and for unattempted questions (no option shaded on OMR).
  * Exam mode: Online (CBT) or Offline (OMR).
  * Competitive examination to be held in February 2027.
