Northern Coalfields Limited (NCL) has invited online applications for 52 vacancies, including Clerk, Office Assistant/ Graduate Assistant, and Lab Technician posts. Applications can be submitted at nclcil.in from 21 November 2026 to 06 December 2026. 

## NCL Quick Summary

2026 · 52 Posts

COMPANY NAME Northern Coalfields Limited

QUALIFICATION B.Sc, B.Com, B.A

AGE LIMIT 18 - 25 Years

LAST DATE 06-12-2026

Northern Coalfields Limited (NCL), a Miniratna company and a subsidiary of Coal India Limited under the Ministry of Coal, Government of India, will engage 52 interns under Round 03 of the 2nd phase of the Prime Minister Internship Scheme (PMIS): 30 Clerk, 17 Office Assistant/ Graduate Assistant and 5 Lab Technician, each at a monthly stipend of ₹9,000/-. Selection is by a merit list of aggregate scores in the qualifying examination, and candidates must be between 18 and 25 years of age as on 1 December 2026. Applications are accepted only online on the PMIS portal (www.pminternship.mca.gov.in) from 21 November 2026 to 6 December 2026, and the 9-month internship gives no claim to regular employment in NCL or CIL.

## NCL Selection Process 2026

  1. Merit list: prepared by arranging the aggregate scores of the qualifying examinations (Degree/Diploma) in descending order for each discipline.
  2. Tie-breaking: a candidate older in age (earlier date of birth) is preferred; if a tie persists, the candidate whose name appears first alphabetically in English (Roman script) is preferred.
  3. Document verification: shortlisted candidates will be notified via the PMIS portal and the NCL official website.



The notice mentions no written examination or interview for this engagement. Before joining, selected candidates must submit a Medical Fitness Certificate in the prescribed format from a Government Medical Officer.
