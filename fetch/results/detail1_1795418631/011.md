#  NICL Administrative Officer Vacancy Recruitment 2026

##  Recruitment of Administrative Officers in NICL 2026

  
The National Insurance Company Limited (NICL), a leading Public Sector General [Insurance](https://www.SarkariNaukriBlog.com/search/label/Insurance) Company, invites [online applications](https://www.sarkarinaukriblog.com/2019/04/filling-sarkari-naukri-application-form.html) in the prescribed format for the recruitment of 321 Administrative Officers (Generalists and Specialists) in Scale-I cadre from the open market for the year 2026. (Advertisement No. NICL/AO/2026) :   
  
  
The recruitment of Administrative Officer Scale-I (Generalists and Specialists) 2026 is being conducted after 1 year at National Insurance Company Limited (NICL). This is a good chance for job seekers to become an officer in a prestigious PSU like NICL.   
  


[](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhs9Y9bRtDLYDdFDxAW6XqYdGZp6p8uBrkK86jB2iWjVh1yCP3sz17RjR2JAjSaPBfwAv82fVXbyYpmWwSUGIvuUcGLI8SLkeIV4duJdlZRuT2JT0adG5Zfcdk00yignQoGPECx7ZvEz1ymGOp1nZPu7dVb17NT0AHPVb5XVQRAYTfbZF_r0bKI/s1600/National-Insurance-Administrative-Officers-Recruitment-2026.webp)

  


### NICL Administrative Officers Recruitment 2026 Vacancies

  


  * Administrative Officer (AO): 321 vacancies (Specialist-121, Generalist-200), Pay Scale: Scale-I ₹82485-156745, Age: 21-30 years as of 01/10/2026, relaxation as per Govt. rules.
    1. Generalist: 200 vacancies (UR-80, EWS-20, OBC-55, SC-30, ST-15) (PWD-9)
    2. Specialists: 121 vacancies (UR-58, EWS-12, OBC-31, SC-13, ST-7) (PWD-5)
       1. Finance (CA & CMA): 20 (UR-10, EWS-2, OBC-5, SC-2, ST-1)
       2. Legal: 20 (UR-10, EWS-2, OBC-5, SC-2, ST-1)
       3. Information Technology: 11 (UR-5, EWS-1, OBC-3, SC-1, ST-1)
       4. Automobile Engineers: 10 (UR-5, EWS-1, OBC-2, SC-1, ST-1)
       5. Risk Engineers: 28 (UR-13, EWS-3, OBC-7, SC-3, ST-2)
       6. Company Secretary: 02 (UR-1, OBC-1)
       7. Agriculture: 20 (UR-09, EWS-2, OBC-5, SC-3, ST-1)

  
  


### Selection Procedure

  
Selection for Administrative Officers (Generalists and Specialists) will be through an online written exam, which will be conducted in two phases, i.e. Phase - I: Preliminary Examination online Phase - II: Main Examination, followed by a personal interview for selected candidates.  The date of the online Examination - Phase I is 17th November 2026  (tentatively), and for the Phase-II exam is 22nd December 2026 (tentatively).   
  
Interview: Candidates who have been shortlisted at the end of Phase-II will subsequently be called for an Interview to be conducted by the Company.   
  
View - [All open Officer Government Jobs](https://www.SarkariNaukriBlog.com/search/label/Officer)   
  


### Application Fee

  
An application fee of ₹1000/- (₹250/- for SC/ST/PWD) to be paid online using Debit Cards (RuPay/Visa/MasterCard/Maestro), Credit Cards, Internet Banking, IMPS, Cash Cards/ Mobile Wallets.   
  


### How to Apply for NICL Administrative Officers Recruitment 2026?

  
Suitable and desirable job seeker candidates should [apply online](https://www.sarkarinaukriblog.com/2019/04/filling-sarkari-naukri-application-form.html) in the prescribed format on the National Insurance Company Limited website from 08/10/2026 to 26/10/2026 only for [National Insurance Company Administrative Officer Recruitment 2026](https://www.sarkarinaukriblog.com/2026/10/nicl-administrative-officer-vacancy.html).
