SSC Grade C Stenographer LDCE 2026: The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the notification for the Grade 'C' Stenographers Limited Departmental Competitive Examination (LDCE) 2026 to fill 320 tentative vacancies across six participating services and cadres. The online application process started on 17 November 2026, and eligible departmental candidates can apply through the official website at [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in/) until 8 December 2026 at 11:00 PM. The Computer Based Examination is tentatively scheduled for February-March 2027 in Delhi-NCR.

This examination is intended for eligible serving Stenographer Grade 'D' personnel of the participating government services and cadres. It is not an open recruitment examination for fresh candidates. Applicants must meet the prescribed service eligibility conditions and submit their applications through the proper departmental channel. Check the vacancy details, eligibility, examination pattern, selection process and application instructions below.

## SSC Grade C Stenographer LDCE Exam Pattern 2026

The examination includes a Computer Based Examination, Stenography Skill Test and Evaluation of Service Records.

### Part A - Computer Based Examination

Subject | Questions | Maximum Marks  
---|---|---  
General Awareness | 100 | 100  
Comprehension and Knowledge of English Language | 100 | 100  
Total | 200 | 200  
  
The Computer Based Examination will have a duration of two hours. Eligible candidates entitled to compensatory time will receive two hours and forty minutes. The questions will be objective-type multiple-choice questions, with a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer.

### Part B - Stenography Skill Test

Candidates shortlisted in the Computer Based Examination will be required to appear for the Stenography Skill Test, subject to the conditions prescribed in the notice.

Particulars | Details  
---|---  
Dictation Duration | 10 Minutes  
Dictation Speed | 100 Words Per Minute  
English Transcription Time | 40 Minutes  
Hindi Transcription Time | 55 Minutes  
Skill Test Marks | 200  
  
Eligible candidates entitled to scribe assistance will receive the applicable extended transcription time. Candidates must take the Skill Test in the language selected in their application. Hindi transcription must be completed using Mangal font.

### Part C - Evaluation of Service Records

Evaluation of Service Records carries a maximum of 100 marks. The Commission will assess the service records or APARs of candidates shortlisted according to the examination rules.

## SSC Grade C Stenographer LDCE Selection Process 2026

The selection process consists of the following stages:

  1. Computer Based Examination (Part A)
  2. Stenography Skill Test (Part B)
  3. Evaluation of Service Records / APARs (Part C)
  4. Preparation of the final merit list and recommendation for inclusion in the applicable cadre select list



Candidates must secure the required qualifying standards at the relevant stages. Final selection will be based on aggregate performance and the applicable cadre-wise vacancy and reservation provisions.
