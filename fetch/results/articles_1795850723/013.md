## Kerala PSC Quick Summary

2026 · 39 Posts

COMPANY NAME Kerala Public Service Commission

POST NAME Field Supervisor, LD Clerk and More

QUALIFICATION Any Graduate, Diploma, ITI, 12TH, 10TH, Any Post Graduate

VACANCY 39 Posts

AGE LIMIT 18 - 50 Years

LAST DATE 16-12-2026

Kerala PSC Recruitment 2026: The Kerala Public Service Commission (KPSC) has released recruitment notifications under Category Nos. 175/2026 to 192/2026 for a total of 39 notified vacancies in various Kerala Government departments, boards and public sector organizations. The posts include Systems Manager, Divisional Accounts Officer, Marketing Executive, Revenue Surveyor, ECG Technician, Field Supervisor, LD Clerk, Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector, Computer Assistant, Assistant Professor, Police Constable Driver, Electrician and other positions. Candidates with the prescribed SSLC, Plus Two, ITI, Diploma, Graduation, Engineering, MBA, medical postgraduate or other relevant qualifications can apply according to the selected post. The age limits and pay scales differ across categories. Eligible candidates must submit their applications through the Kerala PSC One Time Registration (OTR) system at [www.keralapsc.gov.in](https://www.keralapsc.gov.in/) by 16 December 2026, up to midnight.

The Kerala PSC Notification 2026 covers 18 category numbers across 17 official PDF documents, including general recruitment, special recruitment for Scheduled Tribe candidates, Non-Creamy Layer/community-specific NCA vacancies, Ex-Servicemen-only recruitment and an in-service quota. The largest vacancy group is 12 Divisional Accounts Officer posts in Kerala State Electricity Board Limited, exclusively for eligible KSEB departmental employees. Other vacancies include five Field Supervisor Grade II posts and three LD Clerk posts. Applicants should carefully verify the recruitment category, educational qualification, age limit, reservation conditions and certificates before applying. The Commission will announce examination, practical test or interview arrangements separately, wherever applicable.
