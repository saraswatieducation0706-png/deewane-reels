The recruitment drive includes Stenographer, DEO, Staff Car Driver, MTS, and other positions. For complete details, candidates can visit aft.gov.in.

The Armed Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench, Chandigarh (Ministry of Defence, Government of India) is filling 57 posts in 11 categories, from Principal Private Secretary to Multi Tasking Staff, on deputation/absorption for a period of three years. The posts are open to serving officials of the Central Government, State Governments, courts, tribunals, commissions and statutory or autonomous bodies who meet each post's conditions; for five posts, Armed Forces personnel due to retire within one year are also considered. Applications in the Annexure-1 pro-forma must be forwarded by the applicant's Department to the Registrar, Armed Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench, Chandigarh at Chandimandir, latest by 22 January 2027.

### Age Limit

The maximum age limit for appointment by deputation shall not exceed 56 years with a residual service of four years on the closing date of receipt of applications. The rows for Principal Private Secretary, Tribunal Officer/Section Officer, Tribunal Master (Stenographer Grade-1) and Assistant also repeat the 56-year limit (for Tribunal Officer/Section Officer, "by deputation or absorption"). The notice prints no age relaxation.

### Rules That Apply to All Posts

  * The departmental officers/officials in the feeder grade who are in direct line of promotion shall not be eligible for consideration for appointment on deputation.
  * A deputationist in the Armed Forces Tribunal shall not be eligible for consideration for appointments by promotion channel.
  * The period of deputation, including the period of deputation in another ex-cadre post held immediately preceding the appointment in the same or some other organization or department of the Central Government, shall ordinarily not exceed three years. For Principal Private Secretary, Tribunal Master (Stenographer Grade-1) and Assistant, this includes Short Term Contract.



### Armed Forces Personnel (Stenographer Grade-II, Lower Division Clerk, Data Entry Operator, Staff Car Driver, Multi Tasking Staff)

For these five posts, Armed Forces personnel due to retire or who are to be transferred to reserve within a period of one year and having the requisite experience and qualifications prescribed shall also be considered. If selected, they will be given deputation terms up to the date on which they are due for release from the Armed Forces, and thereafter they may be continued on re-employment.

Stenographers of the Central Government or Armed Forces or State Government or Supreme Court or High Court or District Courts or Statutory/Autonomous bodies having pensionary benefits holding:

Officials from Central Government or State Governments or Tribunals or Commissions or Statutory Bodies or Courts having pensionary benefits:

## AFT Chandigarh Recruitment 2027 - FAQs

### Q1. How many vacancies are there in AFT Chandigarh Recruitment 2026?

The circular lists 57 posts across 11 categories, including 09 Stenographer Grade-II and 07 Data Entry Operator posts. The number of vacancies may vary.

### Q2. What is the last date for AFT Chandigarh Recruitment 2026?

The application must be forwarded by the Department to the Registrar, Armed Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench, Chandigarh latest by 22.01.2027.

### Q3. Who can apply for the AFT Chandigarh deputation posts?

Officials of the Central Government, State Governments, courts, tribunals, commissions and statutory or autonomous bodies holding analogous posts or the required regular service in the feeder grade can apply. For Stenographer Grade-II, LDC, DEO, Staff Car Driver and MTS, Armed Forces personnel due to retire or be transferred to reserve within one year are also considered.

### Q4. What is the educational qualification for AFT Chandigarh posts?

It varies by post: 10th standard pass for Staff Car Driver and Multi Tasking Staff, 12th pass for Stenographer Grade-II, LDC and DEO, and a degree for Tribunal Officer/Section Officer, Assistant and Junior Accountant, in addition to the service conditions.

### Q5. What is the age limit for AFT Chandigarh Recruitment 2026?

The maximum age for appointment by deputation shall not exceed 56 years with a residual service of four years on the closing date of receipt of applications.

### Q6. What is the salary in AFT Chandigarh Recruitment 2026?

Pay ranges from Pay Matrix Level 1 (Rs 18000-56900) for Multi Tasking Staff to Level 11 (Rs 67700-208700) for Principal Private Secretary. Deputation pay is governed by DoP&T O.M No 6/8/2009-Estt. (Pay-II) dated 17.06.2010.

### Q7. How do I apply for AFT Chandigarh Recruitment 2026?

Fill the Annexure-1 pro-forma and have your Department forward it, with photocopies of the ACRs for the last five years and the Vigilance Clearance Certificate, to the Registrar, Armed Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench, Chandigarh near Tank TCP, Chandimandir, Haryana-134107.

### Q8. Is there a test for the Staff Car Driver post?

Yes. Staff Car Drivers are selected on the results of a test in driving, and Data Entry Operators' speed of 8000 Key Depressions per hour is judged by a speed test on EDP machine(s).

### Q9. For how long is the deputation at AFT Chandigarh?

The posts are filled on deputation/absorption for a period of three years, and total deputation including an immediately preceding ex-cadre deputation shall ordinarily not exceed three years.

### Q10. Will government accommodation be provided at AFT Chandigarh?

The circular states that this Office is not yet enlisted in the Directorate of Estate for allotment of GPRA (General Pool Residential Accommodation).
