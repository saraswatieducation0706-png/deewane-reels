## VVCMC Fire Services Recruitment 2026 Eligibility

The municipal corporation has announced 16 vacancies in its fire services categories, comprising Fire Station Officer, Deputy Fire Station Officer and Leading Fireman posts. These positions have specialized educational, professional, experience and physical fitness requirements.

### Fire Station Officer Qualification and Experience

  * Graduate degree in any discipline from a recognized university.
  * Station Officer and Instructor course in Fire Engineering from the National Fire Service College, Nagpur; or the prescribed one-year Sub Station Officer and Fire Prevention Officer course from the Maharashtra Fire Service Academy; or the specified Grade-I qualification from the Institution of Fire Engineers (UK or India).
  * MS-CIT qualification.
  * Ability to read, write and speak Marathi.
  * At least 2 years of service as Assistant Fire Station Officer in an A+ class municipal corporation; or 3 years of prescribed equivalent service in the other institutions specified in the notification.
  * Compliance with the prescribed physical standards.



### Deputy Fire Station Officer Qualification and Experience

  * Graduate degree from a recognized university.
  * B.E. in Fire Engineering from the National Fire Service College, Nagpur; or its Divisional Fire Officer advanced diploma; or the prescribed professional membership of the Institution of Fire Engineers.
  * MS-CIT qualification.
  * Knowledge of Marathi for administrative work.
  * Prescribed officer-level fire service experience of 2 or 3 years, depending on the institution and position held.
  * Sound physical fitness for administrative and operational fire service duties.



### Leading Fireman Qualification and Experience

  * SSC or equivalent qualification as prescribed.
  * Six-month Fireman training course from the Maharashtra Government State Fire Training Centre, Mumbai.
  * MS-CIT qualification.
  * Knowledge of Marathi.
  * At least 5 years of experience as Fireman or Driver-cum-Machine Operator in the fire service of an eligible Government, semi-Government, municipal or special planning authority institution.
  * Compliance with the prescribed physical fitness standards.

The selection process for VVCMC Group C posts consists of an online competitive examination followed by document verification. The official notification explicitly states that no oral examination or interview will be conducted for the advertised Group C positions.

  1. Application Scrutiny: Applications will be reviewed based on the information submitted by candidates. Additional shortlisting criteria may be applied if the number of applications is excessively high.
  2. Online Examination: Eligible candidates will appear for an objective online competitive examination.
  3. Publication of Marks: The examination marks of eligible candidates will be published through the official municipal corporation website.
  4. Document Verification: Candidates falling within the probable selection range will be called for verification of original certificates and supporting documents.
  5. Final Selection: Category-wise selection and waiting lists will be published after document verification.



Meeting the minimum educational qualifications or passing the examination does not automatically guarantee selection. Candidates must satisfy all post-specific eligibility and appointment conditions.
