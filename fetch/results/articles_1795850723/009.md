NPC Recruitment 2026: The National Productivity Council (NPC), an autonomous organization under the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India, has released Advertisement No. NPC/TA/1/2026 dated 28 November 2026 for recruitment to 15 Technical Assistant and Technical Assistant (Accounts) Group B posts. The vacancies include 10 Technical Assistant (Accounts) and five Technical Assistant positions. Candidates possessing a recognized Degree in Commerce or a Degree in Arts, Science, Commerce or Management, along with five years of relevant post-qualification experience in Government, Public Sector Undertakings or autonomous bodies, can apply according to the selected post. The general upper age limit is 30 years as on 28 December 2026, with applicable relaxation. Selected candidates will receive a salary under Pay Level 6, Rs. 35,400 to Rs. 1,12,400. Online applications can be submitted from 28 November to 28 December 2026 through [npcrecruitments.in](https://npcrecruitments.in/).

The National Productivity Council Technical Assistant Recruitment 2026 selection process includes a Phase-I Computer-Based Multiple Choice Examination, Phase-II Descriptive Examination and a qualifying Digital Competency Test covering MS Word, MS Excel and MS PowerPoint. The preliminary examination is tentatively scheduled for 24 January 2027, while the Phase-II examinations are expected in March 2027. Eligible candidates may apply for one or both posts through a single online application. Male candidates belonging to UR, OBC-NCL and EWS categories must pay Rs. 1,000 for one post or Rs. 2,000 for both posts, while women of all categories and SC, ST and PwBD candidates are exempted from paying the application fee. The last date for online registration and fee payment is 28 December 2026 at 5:00 PM.

## NPC Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Only Indian nationals possessing the prescribed qualification and relevant professional experience are eligible to apply.

### NPC Technical Assistant Educational Qualification and Experience

---|---|---

### NPC Technical Assistant (Accounts) Qualification 2026

Candidates must possess a recognized Degree in Commerce and at least five years of relevant experience in accounts, finance, auditing, taxation or budget-related work.

The experience must have been gained in Central Government, State Government, a Public Sector Undertaking or an autonomous organization.

A postgraduate qualification in Commerce or passing the Intermediate Examination conducted by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India or the Institute of Cost Accountants of India is desirable but not mandatory.

### NPC Technical Assistant Qualification 2026

Applicants must possess a recognized Bachelor's Degree in Arts, Science, Commerce or Management, along with five years of experience in administrative or office-related work.

Relevant experience includes noting and drafting, establishment work, procurement, record management and file administration in an eligible Government, PSU or autonomous organization.

A postgraduate degree in any discipline is considered desirable.

### Can B.Com. Graduates Apply for NPC Recruitment 2026?

Yes. Candidates holding a recognized B.Com. degree may apply for both posts if they separately satisfy the relevant five-year experience requirements.

### Can BA and B.Sc. Graduates Apply?

Yes. Candidates holding recognized Arts or Science degrees can apply for Technical Assistant positions, provided they have the required administrative or office work experience.

BA or B.Sc. qualifications alone do not meet the essential degree requirement for Technical Assistant (Accounts).

### Can Fresh Graduates Apply for NPC Recruitment 2026?

No. Both posts require five years of relevant full-time paid experience after acquiring the essential qualification.

Internships, articleships, apprenticeships, part-time work and honorary service are not counted as qualifying experience.

### Can Private Sector Employees Apply?

The notification specifically requires qualifying experience in Central/State Government, a Public Sector Undertaking or an autonomous body. Experience solely in ordinary private-sector employment does not satisfy the stated essential experience condition.

Service through outsourcing agencies, manpower suppliers, internships or apprenticeships is excluded.

Candidates claiming this relaxation must upload the prescribed service certificate issued by the Head of the concerned NPC office.

## NPC Recruitment 2026 Selection Process

The National Productivity Council will select candidates through a two-phase examination system and subsequent verification.

  1. Online application submission and fee payment, where applicable.
  2. Phase-I Computer-Based Preliminary Examination.
  3. Shortlisting for Phase-II in the prescribed ratio.
  4. Phase-II Descriptive Examination.
  5. Digital Competency IT Practical Test.
  6. Preparation of post-wise merit lists.
  7. Original document verification.
  8. Medical fitness and character verification.
  9. Final appointment.

### NPC Descriptive Examination

The descriptive examination will be conducted in pen-and-paper mode. Separate papers will be held for Technical Assistant (Accounts) and Technical Assistant.

The examination will assess post-specific professional knowledge, office or financial procedures, communication skills and the ability to prepare notes, drafts and written answers.

The marks obtained in the descriptive examination will form the basis of final merit for candidates who qualify in the IT Practical Test.

### NPC Digital Competency Test Pattern

The Digital Competency Test is common to both posts and will assess practical computer skills using MS Office 2019 or later.

Candidates shortlisted for both posts will take the common Digital Competency Test only once, but must appear separately for both descriptive papers.
