## APPSC Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector Selection Process 2026

The recruitment process consists of a single-level written examination conducted in online Computer Based Recruitment Test (CBRT) mode, followed by a Computer Proficiency Test (CPT).

  * Single-level Written Examination (Objective Type in CBRT mode)
  * Computer Proficiency Test (Practical Type)



## APPSC Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector Exam Pattern 2026

The written examination structure varies by post:

  * Paper-I (All Posts): General Studies & Mental Ability - 150 Questions, 150 Marks, 150 Minutes (Degree Standard).
  * Paper-II (AMVI): Automobile Engineering - 150 Questions, 300 Marks, 150 Minutes (Diploma Standard).
  * Paper-II (Assistant Director Mines / Royalty Inspector / Technical Assistant): Geology / Mining Engineering - 150 Questions, 300 Marks (Mining) or 150 Marks (Geology) / 150 Minutes.
  * Paper-II (Assistant Chemical Examiner / Lab Assistant): Chemistry or Pharmacy - 150 Questions, 150 Marks, 150 Minutes (Degree Standard).



Negative Marking: Each wrong answer is penalized with 1/3rd of the marks prescribed for the question, as per G.O.Ms. No.235, Finance Dept., dated 06/12/2016.
