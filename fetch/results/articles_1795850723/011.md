Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL), Thirumayam, has published the Recruitment 2026 notification for 30 Graduate Apprentice posts. The online application process will be conducted from 26 November 2026 to 10 December 2026. Eligible candidates can apply through the official website, trichy.bhel.com. 

## BHEL Quick Summary

2026 · 30 Posts

COMPANY NAME Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited

POST NAME Graduate Apprentice

QUALIFICATION 12TH, B.A, B.Com, B.Sc, B.Tech/B.E

VACANCY 30 Posts

AGE LIMIT 18 - 27 Years

LAST DATE 10-12-2026

Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL), Thirumayam, a Government of India undertaking, is engaging 30 Graduate Apprentices for one year of apprenticeship training in 2026-27, with a consolidated stipend of Rs 12,300 per month. The seats are for graduates of 2022 to 2026 in seven engineering branches, B.Com, and B.Sc or BA. Only candidates who passed their qualifying examination from Govt. recognized institutions in the State of Tamil Nadu will be considered first. Candidates must register on the NATS 2.0 portal (nats.education.gov.in) and then apply online on the BHEL Trichy portal (trichy.bhel.com) between 26 November 2026 and 10 December 2026.

## About BHEL Thirumayam

BHEL Thirumayam is a unit of Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited, which describes itself on the circular as a Government of India undertaking. Under a notification of the Board of Apprenticeship Training (Southern Region), BHEL provides one-year apprenticeship training to fresh degree holders under the Apprentices (Amendment) Act, 1973. Applications for the Thirumayam unit are taken through the BHEL Trichy website.

## Vacancy Details

The circular lists 30 training vacancies across nine disciplines. BHEL states that the number is tentative and may increase or decrease in all or any of the branches depending on requirement, at the absolute discretion of the management.

### Reservation

Out of the training vacancies, 19%, 1%, 27%, 10% and 4% are reserved for SC, ST, OBC (NCL), EWS and Physically Challenged (PwD) candidates respectively. The circular gives these percentages only and does not print a category-wise number of seats for each discipline. Reservation for dependents of regular employees (Serving/Retired/Deceased) is as per company rules.

## Who Can Apply

### Educational Qualification

Discipline| Essential Qualification  
---|---  
Mechanical, Production, Electrical & Electronics, Electronics & Communication, Civil, Computer Science, Information Technology| Passed in 10+2 and Graduation in Engineering or Technology as a regular full time candidate.  
Accountant - B.Com| Passed in 10+2 and Graduation in Commerce (B.Com) as a regular full time candidate.  
Assistant (HR) - B.Sc or B.A| Passed in 10+2 and Graduation in Science B.Sc or BA as a regular full time candidate.  
  
  * Minimum marks: 60% for UR/OBC(NCL)/EWS candidates and 50% for SC/ST candidates, from a Government recognized Institution/University.
  * Year of passing: only candidates who completed the full-time degree in 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 or 2026 are eligible.
  * Degrees completed through Distance Learning, Part Time, Correspondence or Sandwich courses will not be considered.
  * Only candidates who passed their qualifying examination from Govt. recognized academic institutions located in the State of Tamil Nadu will be considered for selection. If candidates from Tamil Nadu are not available for any branch, those vacancies will be filled with other candidates as per procedure for that branch.



### Who Is Not Eligible

  * Candidates who have already undergone, or are currently undergoing, apprenticeship training in any other establishment under the Apprentices (Amendment) Act, 1973.
  * Candidates who have completed a Post Graduate qualification (even an integrated PG course), as per the NATS process manual.
  * Candidates with work experience of one year or more.



### NATS Registration

Candidates must be registered on the MHRD NATS 2.0 New Portal (<https://nats.education.gov.in>) for Graduate Apprentices. Only candidates with a BOAT/NATS 2.0 Student Enrolment Number will be considered for the training.

### Age Limit

The age limit is minimum 18 years and maximum 27 years as on 01.11.2026 for General candidates. The upper age limit is relaxed as follows:

Category| Relaxation in Upper Age Limit  
---|---  
SC/ST| 5 years  
OBC (NCL)| 3 years  
PwD (with minimum 40% disability)| 10 years  
  
### Physical Standards

Candidates selected as apprentices will have to fulfil the minimum physical fitness standard / parameters as specified in the Apprentices Act, Clause 4 of Apprenticeship Rule 1992 and amendments thereof, if any.

Candidates should check the official notification for the discipline-specific eligibility of each post.

## Pay and Tenure

  * Stipend: Rs 12,300/- per month (Consolidated), paid for the entire training duration. The same stipend applies to all nine disciplines.
  * Duration: one year from the date of joining, as per the Apprentices (Amendment) Act, 1973.
  * No TA/DA will be paid for certificate verification or for joining the apprenticeship training on selection.
  * After the apprenticeship at BHEL Thirumayam, BHEL has no obligation to offer employment, and an apprentice cannot claim employment on the ground of completing the apprenticeship.



## How to Apply for BHEL Thirumayam Recruitment 2026

Only online applications are accepted. The circular and its Annexure-1 give these steps:

  1. Register on the Ministry of Education/GOI portal (<https://nats.education.gov.in>) and note down your 12/16-digit Student Enrolment Number; it is mandatory for the BHEL application.
  2. Go to <https://trichy.bhel.com> and click the link "Apprenticeship Application Portal (THIRUMAYAM)".
  3. Click "Register" from the menu, read the instructions and accept them by clicking "I agree".
  4. Fill in the registration form and click "REGISTER"; an OTP will be sent to your registered email ID and mobile number.
  5. Create a new password using the OTP, then log in to the portal with it.
  6. Fill in the application form and upload the scanned documents listed below, in the sizes shown on the portal.
  7. Submit the application and save a copy; it must be produced at the time of document verification.



### Documents to Upload

  * Latest colour passport size photograph
  * Candidate's signature
  * 10th Standard mark sheet
  * Consolidated mark sheet of the prescribed educational qualification
  * Community Certificate (SC/ST/OBC (NCL), EWS, PwD certificate); the OBC (NCL) and EWS certificate must follow the latest government guideline and must not be more than one year old
  * Aadhaar card
  * Screenshot of the candidate's profile home page on the NATS portal (<https://nats.education.gov.in>), showing the 12/16-digit portal registration number
  * Bank passbook front page as uploaded on the NATS portal; the account should be Aadhaar seeded and DBT enabled



Blurred, edited or incorrect documents will be summarily rejected. Candidates must have an active email ID and mobile number that stay valid for at least the next one year.

## Selection and Interview

Selection will be based on fulfilling the conditions of the notification. A merit list will be drawn up on the marks secured in the qualifying examination, and in case of a tie in marks, the candidate older in age will be preferred. The list of eligible candidates for document verification and apprentice training will be made available on <https://trichy.bhel.com> (Our services → Apprenticeship Application Portal (THIRUMAYAM)) or through email/SMS alerts. At document verification, candidates must produce the copy of their online application.

## BHEL Recruitment 2026 - FAQs

### Q1. How many vacancies are there in BHEL Thirumayam Recruitment 2026?

There are 30 Graduate Apprentice training vacancies (tentative): Mechanical 9, Production 1, Electrical & Electronics 4, Electronics & Communication 4, Civil 2, Computer Science 4, Information Technology 1, Accountant - B.Com 3 and Assistant (HR) - B.Sc or B.A 2.

### Q2. What is the last date to apply for the BHEL Thirumayam Graduate Apprentice training?

The online application closes on 10 December 2026. It opened on 26 November 2026.

### Q3. What qualification is needed for the BHEL Thirumayam apprenticeship?

Candidates need 10+2 and a regular full-time degree in Engineering or Technology, Commerce (B.Com), or Science B.Sc or BA, depending on the discipline, completed in 2022 to 2026 with at least 60% marks (50% for SC/ST).

### Q4. What is the age limit for BHEL Thirumayam Graduate Apprentices?

The age limit is minimum 18 years and maximum 27 years as on 01.11.2026 for General candidates, with upper age relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL) and 10 years for PwD (minimum 40% disability).

### Q5. What stipend does a BHEL Thirumayam Graduate Apprentice get?

The stipend is Rs 12,300/- per month (Consolidated) for the entire one-year training. No TA/DA is paid for certificate verification or joining.

### Q6. How do I apply for BHEL Thirumayam Recruitment 2026?

First register on the NATS portal (nats.education.gov.in) to get a 12/16-digit Student Enrolment Number, then apply online through trichy.bhel.com under "Apprenticeship Application Portal (THIRUMAYAM)" by 10 December 2026.

### Q7. How will BHEL Thirumayam select Graduate Apprentices?

A merit list will be drawn up on the marks secured in the qualifying examination, followed by document verification. In case of a tie, the older candidate will be preferred.

### Q8. Can candidates from outside Tamil Nadu apply?

Only candidates who passed their qualifying examination from Govt. recognized institutions in Tamil Nadu will be considered for selection. Other candidates will be considered for a branch only if Tamil Nadu candidates are not available for it.

### Q9. Can post graduates or experienced candidates apply for the BHEL apprenticeship?

No. Candidates with a Post Graduate qualification (even an integrated PG course), work experience of one year or more, or earlier or ongoing apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act are not eligible.

### Q10. Is the BHEL Thirumayam apprenticeship a permanent job?

No. It is one year of apprenticeship training, and BHEL has no obligation to offer employment after it, nor can an apprentice claim employment on completing it.
