Update 27 Nov 2026: Notification out with age limits and full qualification details. It also gives category-wise vacancies, says selected candidates may be posted anywhere in India or outside India, and says the online recruitment portal will be launched shortly.

Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), New Delhi, Across India, has invited applications for 20 posts in 2 roles under Advt No. MeitY/2026/1. The posts include Electronics & Communication Engineering, Computer Science & Information Technology. Applications are accepted only through MeitY's online recruitment portal, which is to be launched shortly; the notification does not give the application dates yet.

Selected candidates may be posted anywhere in India or outside India. Qualification required: Valid Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) Score (2024/2025/2026). The pay is Level 10 of the Pay Matrix (Rs. 56,100 - Rs. 1,77,500). Selection is by valid Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) Score (2024/2025/2026) and personal Interview. Post-wise eligibility, dates and the steps to apply are given below.
