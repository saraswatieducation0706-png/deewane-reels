The walk-in interview will be held on 16 December 2026. Candidates should report at 9:30 AM. Further details are available at nml.res.in.

The walk-in interview of CSIR-National Metallurgical Laboratory (Council of Scientific & Industrial Research) will be held on 16 December 2026 (Wednesday) from 09:30 AM onwards, and no candidate will be allowed entry in the venue after 11:00 AM. The venue is CSIR-National Metallurgical Laboratory, Burmamines, Jamshedpur (Jharkhand) – 831007. Under Advertisement No. 05/2026, the laboratory will engage 18 apprentices: 7 Engineering Graduate (B.E/ B. Tech.) apprentices at a stipend of Rs.12,300/- per month and 11 Diploma Engineering (Technician) apprentices at Rs.10,900/- per month, for 12 months of apprenticeship training.

## About CSIR-NML

CSIR-National Metallurgical Laboratory is a laboratory of the Council of Scientific & Industrial Research located at Burmamines, Jamshedpur. The notice describes it as one of the global leaders in metallurgy and materials research, with research publications in peer reviewed journals and intellectual property in metallurgical and materials science and technology. The Director, CSIR-NML is engaging these apprentices under the scheme provided by the Apprenticeship Act, 1961 and the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 of the Government of India.

### Engineering Graduate (B.E/ B. Tech.) – Graduate Apprentice

  * Degree in Engineering/Technology (regular full time) in relevant area granted by a recognized/approved University/Institutions in relevant disciplines.
  * Graduation in Engineering must have been completed within the last five years.
  * Age: minimum 21 years and maximum 26 years as on date of interview.
  * Candidates holding M. Tech or equivalent shall not be considered.



### Diploma Engineering – Technician Apprentice

  * Three years Diploma in Engineering/Technology (regular-full time) in relevant area granted by a recognized/approved University/Institutions in relevant disciplines.
  * The diploma must have been completed within the last five years.
  * Age: minimum 18 years and maximum 24 years as on the date of interview.
  * Candidates holding B.E or equivalent shall not be considered.



### Other Eligibility Conditions

  * Only those who passed the Degree / Diploma during the years 2022/2023/2024/2025/2026 are eligible.
  * Each candidate must have a verified NATS ID and APAAR ID.
  * If the date of declaration of result is not mentioned in the mark sheet, a certificate giving the date of publication of the result, issued by the Principal of the Polytechnic / College / Institute, must be submitted along with the application form.
  * One candidate can apply against any one of the disciplines.
  * Candidates should not have undergone any apprenticeship training earlier, or be pursuing apprenticeship training, under the Apprentices Act, 1961/1971 as amended from time to time.
  * Candidates should not have work experience of one year or more, and should not have completed five years after passing the qualifying examination.
  * Candidates must have enrolled on the NATS portal within five years after passing their qualifying examination.



Candidates should check the official notification for the discipline-specific eligibility of each branch.

### Stipend

The stipend is Rs.12,300/- per month for Engineering Graduate apprentices and Rs.10,900/- per month for Diploma Engineering apprentices. 50% of this amount will be paid by the Establishment on a monthly basis and the rest 50% will be paid by the Government through DBT, that is Rs. 6,150/- for Graduate and Rs. 5,450/- for Diploma candidates, as per BOPT Letter No. BT/AA/Revised Stipend/2026/78 dated 14.05.2026. No other allowance or benefit will be admissible. Selected apprentices must have an Aadhar linked bank account in their own name, preferably in a nationalized bank with internet banking facilities, and a PAN card to receive the stipend from CSIR-NML.

### NATS Registration

Candidates are advised to register as Graduate (Degree)/ Technician (Diploma) Apprentices on [https://nats.education.gov.in](https://nats.education.gov.in/) / [www.bopter.gov.in](https://www.bopter.gov.in) under their respective state, as per their trade. Only candidates registered on the NATS/NAPS portal who meet the eligibility criteria will be considered for the walk-in interview. Those not yet enrolled can follow these steps given in the notice:

  1. Go to <https://nats.education.gov.in/>.
  2. Click on Student – Student Register.
  3. Complete the application form.
  4. Note the Unique Student Enrolment ID generated after successful registration; it is mandatory before applying and must be mentioned in the application form.



The application form for engagement as Apprentice Trainee is enclosed with the notice as Annexure-I. It asks for the area/discipline, branch, NAPS/NATS registration number, personal details, Aadhar number, category, PwBD status, addresses, educational qualifications and a passport size colour photograph, followed by a signed declaration.

## What to Bring

Candidates must bring the following documents in original along with one set of self-attested photocopies:

  * 10th / Matriculation certificate / marks sheet, as proof of date of birth
  * Certificate and marks sheet of BE / B. Tech. or Diploma, as the case may be
  * Original College Leaving Certificate (C.L.C.)
  * Caste certificate for SC/ST/OBC (Non Creamy Layer) candidates, in the prescribed format issued by the Competent Authority as prescribed by the Government of India
  * PwBD certificate, if any
  * Two recent passport size colour photographs
  * AADHAR Card / PAN Card or any other Government issued photo ID proof



The enclosure list in the Annexure-I application form names self-attested photocopies of the 10th mark sheet and certificate; the 10+2/B. Tech/Diploma mark sheet and certificate; the Aadhaar card; the caste/disability certificate (if applicable); and the bank passbook.

## What Happens on the Day

Reporting starts at 09:30 AM on 16 December 2026, and entry closes at 11:00 AM. Candidates will be selected for apprenticeship training based on their performance in the walk-in interview. The engagement letter will be sent to selected candidates by email; they must submit acceptance within 03 days and join within 07 days of receiving it, failing which the selection will be treated as cancelled. Original documents of selected candidates will be verified at the time of reporting, and the original College Leaving Certificate is submitted at joining and retained during the apprenticeship. No TA/DA will be paid for attending the walk-in interview.

## Important Instructions

  * The training period is 12 months (one year) for all branches, at CSIR-NML, Jamshedpur.
  * The engagement is purely temporary for one year and not for regular employment in CSIR-NML; there is no obligation to offer employment after the apprenticeship, and no claim to employment arises from it.
  * No accommodation will be provided; selected candidates must arrange their own stay.
  * Candidates should be medically fit as per the standard required for the concerned discipline, failing which candidature is liable to be cancelled.
  * Candidature is provisional; false information or suppression of facts will cancel candidature, and engagement is liable to be terminated without notice if detected later.
  * Filling of seats is at the discretion of the Competent Authority, CSIR-NML, Jamshedpur, and some seats may remain unfilled due to unsuitability or insufficient candidates.
  * Any corrigendum, addendum, update and the final result will be posted on <https://nml.res.in/>.

The walk-in interview is on 16 December 2026 (Wednesday) from 09:30 AM at CSIR-National Metallurgical Laboratory, Burmamines, Jamshedpur (Jharkhand) – 831007, with no entry after 11:00 AM.

### Q2. How many apprentices will CSIR-NML engage?

CSIR-NML will engage 18 apprentices: 7 Engineering Graduate (B.E/ B. Tech.) and 11 Diploma Engineering apprentices; the number of seats is tentative.

### Q3. What qualification is required?

Graduate apprentices need a regular full-time Degree in Engineering/Technology and Technician apprentices a three-year regular full-time Diploma in Engineering/Technology in the relevant area, passed during 2022 to 2026.

### Q4. What is the age limit for CSIR-NML apprentices?

The age limit is minimum 21 and maximum 26 years for Graduate apprentices and minimum 18 and maximum 24 years for Technician (Diploma) apprentices, as on the date of interview.

### Q5. What is the stipend?

The stipend is Rs.12,300/- per month for Graduate apprentices and Rs.10,900/- per month for Diploma apprentices, half paid by CSIR-NML and half by the Government through DBT; no other allowance is admissible.

### Q6. Who gets age relaxation?

SC/ST candidates get 5 years and OBC candidates 3 years for the posts reserved for them; PwBD candidates get up to 10 years (up to 15 years for SC/ST and up to 13 years for OBC Non-Creamy Layer).

### Q7. Which documents must be brought to the walk-in?

Originals with one set of self-attested photocopies of the 10th certificate, degree or diploma certificate and marks sheet, original College Leaving Certificate, caste and PwBD certificates if applicable, two passport size colour photographs and a Government photo ID.

### Q8. How will candidates be selected?

Candidates will be selected based on their performance in the walk-in interview, and the engagement letter will be sent by email.

### Q9. Is NATS registration compulsory?

Yes, each candidate must have a verified NATS ID and APAAR ID, and only candidates registered on the NATS/NAPS portal will be considered for the walk-in interview.

### Q10. Is this a permanent job at CSIR-NML?

No, the engagement is purely temporary for one year and not for regular employment, and CSIR-NML has no obligation to offer employment after the apprenticeship.
