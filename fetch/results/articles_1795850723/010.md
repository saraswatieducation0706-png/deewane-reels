## MPSC Quick Summary

2026 · 18 Posts

Meghalaya Public Service Commission (MPSC), Shillong, has invited applications for 18 posts in 8 roles under Advt No. 02/2026-2027. The posts include Stenographer Grade III - Office of the Director General of Police Meghalaya, Shillong, Stenographer Grade III - Directorate of Sports and Youth Affairs, Meghalaya, Shillong, Stenographer Grade III - Directorate of Soil and Water Conservation Department, Meghalaya, Shillong, Stenographer Grade III - Meghalaya Administrative Training Institute and 4 more. Applications are accepted online until 26 December 2026.

The posts are for Office of the Director General of Police Meghalaya, Shillong, Directorate of Sports and Youth Affairs, Shillong, Directorate of Soil and Water Conservation Department, Shillong, Meghalaya Administrative Training Institute, Directorate of Housing, Labour Commissioner and Directorate of Social Welfare, Meghalaya Secretariat. Qualification required: Graduate in any discipline from any recognized university with minimum speed of 80 words per minute (W.P.M) in Short Hand. The pay is Level 8 of Revised Pay Structure. The application fee is Rs. 320 for For post at Sl. no.01. Selection is by screening Test and personal Interview. Post-wise eligibility, dates and the steps to apply are given below.

## How Will MPSC Select Candidates?

  * Screening Test
  * Personal Interview
  * Screening Test as per syllabus uploaded on the MPSC website
  * Ratio from Screening Test to Personal Interview as per Commission's decision dated 13 April 2023
  * Only candidates who pass the Screening Test / Main examination are eligible for Personal Interview
  * Only candidates who produce all required documents in original are called for Personal Interview



## MPSC Exam Pattern 2026

  * Examination centres: Shillong, Tura, Jowai, Williamnagar and Nongstoin
  * Change of examination centre will not be entertained



## Documents to Enclose

  * Original documents of educational qualification for Personal Interview
  * Original date of birth certificate (Matriculation/SSLC or equivalent)
  * Caste and community certificates where required
  * Disability Certificate for PwD fee exemption



## How to Apply for MPSC Recruitment 2026 Online

  1. Apply only online by clicking the Online Application icon on the MPSC website.
  2. First-time applicants must complete a one-time registration.
  3. Already registered candidates may log in and apply directly.
  4. Pay the fee by e-Challan or online through the GRAS gateway.
  5. Applications by any other mode will be summarily rejected.
  6. Apply before 17:00 hrs on 26 December 2026.



## Other Points to Note

  * Candidates must check and update their profile before applying
  * Category once indicated in the online form cannot be changed later
  * 2% reservation for sports persons as per OM No. PER (AR) 150/88/534 Dt. 07/08/2017
  * There will be no Ineligibility List; all applicants are called for the Screening Test
  * Candidates appear for Screening Test / Personal Interview at their own expenses
  * Commission may direct applicants to the nearest centre if Jowai, Nongstoin or Williamnagar has inadequate candidates
