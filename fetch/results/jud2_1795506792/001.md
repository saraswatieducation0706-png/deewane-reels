The Karnataka High Court Civil Judge Selection Process 2026 consists of three main competitive examination stages: Preliminary Examination, Main Written Examination and Viva-voce. ### 1\. Preliminary Examination

The Preliminary Examination will consist of objective-type questions carrying 100 marks. It tests candidates' knowledge of Civil Law, Criminal Law, the Constitution and General Knowledge, including reasoning and mental ability.

### 2\. Main Written Examination

Candidates shortlisted from the Preliminary Examination will appear for the Main Written Examination, which consists of four papers carrying 100 marks each. The papers cover translation, legal knowledge, civil judgment writing and criminal judgment writing.

### 3\. Viva-voce

Candidates qualifying in the Main Written Examination will be called for Viva-voce carrying 100 marks. The interview will assess general knowledge, understanding of legal principles and suitability for appointment as a Civil Judge.

### 4\. Computer Test

A separate Computer Test carrying 25 marks will assess candidates' computer knowledge and suitability. Marks obtained in the Computer Test will not be added to the total marks secured in the Main Written Examination and Viva-voce.

### 5\. Document Verification and Final Selection

Shortlisted candidates must produce the prescribed original certificates and supporting documents for verification. Final selection will be based on the applicable examination results, qualifying standards, eligibility verification and recruitment rules.
