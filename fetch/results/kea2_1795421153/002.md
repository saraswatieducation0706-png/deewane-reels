## KEA Selection Process 2026

Under the Karnataka Prosecutions and Government Litigations (Recruitment) Rules, 2012, selection has three stages:

  1. Preliminary examination – multiple-choice questions on an Offline-OMR sheet. In each paper (Paper-1 / Paper-2), SC and ST candidates need 35% and General and other categories 40%; qualifying candidates are called to the main examination in the ratio 1:10.
  2. Main examination – descriptive questions answered in writing. In each paper (Paper-1 / Paper-2 / Paper-3), SC and ST candidates need 35% and General and other categories 40%; qualifying candidates are called to the interview in the ratio 1:3.
  3. Interview – maximum 25 marks, conducted by the Selection Committee constituted under Government Order No. ಒಇ 95 ಪಿಪಿಇ 2026 dated 22.09.2026. It tests general knowledge, the ability to grasp points of law, understanding and suitability for the post. Questions may be answered in Kannada or English. A candidate absent at the interview will not be considered for selection.



### Merit and final selection

  * After the interview, a merit list is prepared on the total marks of the main examination and the interview.
  * The Selection Committee publishes the provisional and final selection lists in the ratio 1:1 against the notified vacancies, as per the reservation rules in force.
  * Candidates are selected following the reservation rules in the Government's orders, notifications and circulars.
  * If two or more candidates get equal marks, the older candidate is placed higher in the merit order.
  * Before appointment, the Department of Prosecutions and Government Litigations will verify the marks cards and certificates with the issuing departments, universities and boards, and the genuineness of the enrolment obtained from the Bar Council.

## KEA Assistant Public Prosecutor cum Assistant Government Pleader Recruitment 2026 - FAQs

### Q1. How many vacancies are there in KEA Recruitment 2026?

There are 268 Assistant Public Prosecutor-cum-Assistant Government Pleader (Group-B) posts: 233 posts, 02 posts under Article 371(J) and 33 backlog posts.

### Q2. When does the KEA APP-cum-AGP online application start?

The online application starts on 18 November 2026 on the KEA website.

### Q3. What is the last date to apply for KEA Recruitment 2026?

The last date to apply online is 15 December 2026, and the last date for online fee payment is 16 December 2026.

### Q4. What is the application fee for KEA Recruitment 2026?

The fee is Rs. 750 for General Merit and Category 2A/2B/3A/3B, Rs. 500 for SC/ST/Category-1/Ex-servicemen/Third gender and Rs. 250 for persons with disabilities, payable online only and not refundable.

### Q5. What is the age limit for KEA Assistant Public Prosecutor posts?

The minimum age is 25 years as on the last date; the maximum is 42 years for General Merit, 45 years for Category 2A, 2B, 3A and 3B, and 47 years for SC/ST/Category-1, including a one-time 5-year relaxation.

### Q6. What qualification is required for KEA APP-cum-AGP Recruitment 2026?

An LLB degree and continuous practice as an advocate in civil and criminal courts in India under the Advocates Act, 1961, for not less than 03 years as on the last date for applying.

### Q7. What is the salary of a KEA Assistant Public Prosecutor-cum-Assistant Government Pleader?

The pay scale is Rs. 69250-1650-74200-1900-85600-2300-99400-2700-115600-3100-134200 under the revised State pay scale 2024, with NPS and KGID facility.

### Q8. What is the selection process for KEA Recruitment 2026?

Selection is through a preliminary examination (Offline-OMR), a descriptive main examination and an interview of 25 marks, with final selection on the total marks of the main examination and the interview.

### Q9. Is there negative marking in the KEA APP preliminary exam?

Yes. If no option, including the fifth option, is shaded for a question on the OMR sheet, 0.25 marks are deducted for that question.

### Q10. When is the KEA APP preliminary exam 2026?

The preliminary examination will be held in February 2027; the detailed schedule will be published later.
