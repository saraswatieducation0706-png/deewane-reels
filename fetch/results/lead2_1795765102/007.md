## Selection Procedure

The detailed selection procedure has been mentioned in the official notification of BPSC Assistant Director Recruitment 2026-27. But we are giving you here the short summary of the selection procedure. The candidates are advised to refer to official notification for selection procedure. 

Selection Procedure:

  * Written Examination: Objective type written exam consisting of three subjects: 
    * General Hindi: 100 marks, 100 questions, 2 hours (qualifying, minimum 30 marks required; not counted in merit)
    * General Knowledge: 100 marks, 100 questions, 2 hours
    * Subject-specific Papers (Two Papers): Paper I – 200 marks, 100 questions, 2 hours; Paper II – 200 marks, 100 questions, 2 hours
  * Minimum Qualifying Marks in Written Exam: General Category: 40%; EBC: 34%; BC: 36.5%; SC/ST/Women/Divyang: 32%
  * Negative Marking: 1/3 mark deducted for unanswered questions (if option E not selected). Each question has 5 options (A, B, C, D, E); option E must be selected if not attempting.
  * Interview (Viva-Voce): 50 marks for candidates who qualify the written exam. Candidates called for interview = 2.5 times the number of vacancies (category-wise).
  * Merit List Preparation: Based on total marks = Written Exam + Interview (75 marks weightage) + Contract Service Preference (25 marks max, at 5 marks per year of contractual service on same post under Bihar Government).
  * Tie-breaking: Written exam marks → Interview marks → Age (older preferred) → Name in Devanagari alphabetical order.
  * No re-evaluation provision for written examination.
