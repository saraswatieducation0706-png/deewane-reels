## Selection Procedure

The detailed selection procedure has been mentioned in the official notification of GSSSB Laboratory Technician Class-3 Recruitment 2026. But we are giving you here the short summary of the selection procedure. The candidates are advised to refer to official notification for selection procedure. 

Selection Procedure:

  * Written Examination (MCQ – CBRT/OMR): A single-stage competitive examination consisting of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ) in Computer Based Response Test (CBRT) or OMR mode.
  * Exam Structure:  
Part-A (90 Marks):

    * Logical Reasoning & Data Interpretation: 30 Marks
    * Mathematical Aptitude: 30 Marks
    * Indian Constitution, Current Affairs, Gujarati & English Comprehension: 30 Marks

Part-B (120 Marks):

    * Subject-related questions (as per educational qualification): 120 Marks

Total: 210 Questions, 210 Marks, Duration: 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
  * Negative Marking: 1/4 mark deducted for each wrong answer.
  * Minimum Qualifying Standard: 40% in both Part-A and Part-B separately (for all categories).
  * Provisional Answer Key will be published after the exam; objections will be considered before publishing the Final Answer Key.
  * Document Verification: Approximately 2x the number of vacancies (category-wise) will be called for document verification based on merit.
  * Merit List & Selection List: Prepared based on marks/Normalized marks obtained in the competitive exam, considering category-wise vacancies. Widows get 5% bonus marks; Sports persons (National/International/Inter-University/SGFI level) get 5% bonus marks.
  * Appointment: Final appointment is subject to the appointing authority’s satisfaction regarding the candidate’s suitability.

## How to Apply

Here is the complete procedure to apply for GSSSB Laboratory Technician Class-3 Recruitment 2026. The candidates should read the complete procedure to apply in the official notification from the links given below. 

The application procedure for is not fully online. Candidates are advised to follow the detailed instructions given below. 

  1. Visit the official website: https://ojas.gujarat.gov.in
  2. Click on ‘Online Application’ and select GSSSB.
  3. Click on the advertisement for Laboratory Technician, Class-3 (Advertisement No. 472/202627) and click ‘Apply Now’.
  4. Enter your OTR (One Time Registration) Registration Number and Date of Birth, then click ‘Apply with OTR’. (OTR registration is mandatory before filling the application form.)
  5. Fill in Personal Details, SSC details, Bank Account details (for fee refund), and Educational Details carefully.
  6. Upload passport-size photograph (5 cm height x 3.6 cm width, JPG/JPEG/PNG, max 15 KB) and signature (2.5 cm height x 7.5 cm width, JPG, max 15 KB).
  7. Accept the Declaration and click ‘Save’. Note the Application Number generated.
  8. Click ‘Confirm Application’, verify all details, and confirm. A Confirmation Number will be generated — save it for future reference.
  9. Pay the examination fee online (Debit Card, Internet Banking, UPI, or Wallets) by 18/12/2026 (23:59 hrs) through the OJAS website under ‘Print Application Form / Pay Fees’.
  10. Download and print the application form for your records before the last date.
