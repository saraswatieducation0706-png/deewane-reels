# BHEL Engineer and Supervisor Trainee Recruitment 2026 Notification OUT: Apply Online for 415 Posts from 25 November

By DK Malik | Nov 23, 2026 |  Updated: Nov 23, 11:14 am 

[ Telegram ](https://t.me/quick_sarkari) [ WhatsApp ](https://whatsapp.com/channel/0029VaxxbrREquiPoXTWM229) [ YouTube ](https://www.youtube.com/@Quick_Sarkari) [ Instagram ](https://www.instagram.com/quicksarkari_com/) [ Facebook ](https://www.facebook.com/quicksarkari) [ X ](https://x.com/quick__sarkari)

BHEL Engineer and Supervisor Trainee Recruitment 2026: Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL), India's leading engineering enterprise, has released a short notice for the recruitment of Engineer/Executive Trainees and Supervisor Trainees for 2027. A total of 415 posts are to be filled: 200 Engineer/Executive Trainee posts in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Civil, IT/CS, HR and Finance, and 215 Supervisor Trainee posts in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Civil, HR and Finance. The BHEL Engineer and Supervisor Trainee Recruitment 2026 online application will start on 25 November 2026 (10:00 AM), and the last date to apply online is 28 December 2026 (11:45 PM).

Engineering graduates, CA/CMA qualified candidates and HR postgraduates can apply for the Engineer/Executive Trainee posts, while engineering diploma holders, BBA/BBS/BMS graduates and B.Com graduates can apply for the Supervisor Trainee posts. Candidates have to apply online at careers.bhel.in and appear for a Computer Based Examination (CBE). The detailed notification with age limit, application fee, pay scale and selection process will be available on careers.bhel.in from 25 November 2026.

### How to Apply for BHEL Engineer and Supervisor Trainee Recruitment 2026

  1. Visit the BHEL recruitment website careers.bhel.in on or after 25 November 2026.
  2. Read the detailed notification for Engineer/Executive Trainee or Supervisor Trainee and check your eligibility.
  3. Click on the online application link for the post and register with your basic details.
  4. Fill in the application form with your educational details and choose your discipline.
  5. Upload your photo, signature and documents, and pay the application fee online (if applicable).
  6. Submit the form on or before 28 December 2026 (11:45 PM) and take a printout for future reference.
