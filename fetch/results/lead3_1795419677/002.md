## AFCAT 01/2027 Apply Online: Flying, Ground Duty Technical & Non-Technical, NCC Special Entry and GATE Score Entry Explained

If you dream of wearing the Air Force blue, this is your chance. The Indian Air Force (IAF) has invited men and women to join as commissioned officers in the Flying Branch and the Ground Duty (Technical and Non-Technical) Branches through AFCAT 01/2027. Two more doors are open too: the NCC Special Entry (Flying) and the GATE Score Entry (Ground Duty Technical). Courses begin in January 2028, and officers start at Pay Level 10.

Online registration opens on 12 November 2026 at 11:00 AM and closes on 11 December 2026 at 11:30 PM. ~~This is a short newspaper notice, so the number of vacancies, the educational qualification and the selection details are in the detailed notification on the official website.~~ The detailed 66-page notification is now out: it gives 284 vacancies, the qualifications, the exam pattern and the exam date, 31 January 2027. ### What is the Salary for AFCAT 01/2027 Officers?

On commissioning, a Flying Officer gets pay as per the Defence Matrix at Level 10, with Military Service Pay (MSP) on top.

Component| Amount  
---|---  
Pay (Defence Matrix, Level 10)| ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500  
Military Service Pay (MSP)| ₹15,500  
Flight Cadet stipend during training| ₹56,100 per month (fixed)  
  
Allowances are paid on commissioning, depending on branch, duty and place of posting. The notice names Flying, Transport, Technical, Test Pilot and Flight Test Engineer, and Risk and Hardship allowances. Officers also get furnished accommodation, medical cover for self and dependents, canteen and Officers' Mess, loans at subsidised rates, LTC, and leave of 60 days annual and 20 days casual, subject to service needs. The IAF also offers sports and adventure activities such as sky diving, micro light flying and mountaineering.

Insurance is mandatory through the Air Force Group Insurance Society, presently ₹1.25 crore (on contribution) for death in service. PC officers who retire after 20 or more years, and SSC officers who complete 14 years, get Air Veterans Insurance Cover of ₹24 lakh for 15 years, then ₹12 lakh till age 75.
