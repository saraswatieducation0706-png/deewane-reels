Essential Qualification :-

     1.   Candidates applying for Trade (ITI) Apprentices mentioned in the above table must possess ITI Trade Qualification
          Certificate (National Trade Certificate / State trade Certificate) from an institution which is recognized by
          National Council of Vocational Training (NCVT) / State Council of Vocational Training (SCVT) at the time of
          application.
     2.   Candidates applying for Graduate Apprenticeship, must possess Degree in Engg. / Technology from Govt.
          recognized university/ Institute and must produce degree certificate at the time of interview.
     3.   Candidates applying for Technician (Diploma) Apprenticeship must possess Diploma Certificate in Engg.
          /Technology from Govt. recognized university/ Institute and must produce diploma certificate at the time of
          interview.


Eligibility Criteria: -

     1.   Candidates, interested to appear for walk-in interview of (A) Trade Apprenticeship, are required to register
          themselves and get it approved in advance in National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) web portal
          (https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in). Apprenticeship Registration No. (Ex.-A0XXX111223) is must for
          contract generation.
     2.   For Graduate Apprenticeship and Technician (Diploma) Apprenticeship, it is required to register themselves
          and get it approved in advance in National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) web portal
          (https://nats.education.gov.in). Apprentice Registration No. (Ex.- ACTXXX123456) is must for contract generation.
     3.   Engineering graduate/other graduates or Diploma Holder or Trade Certificate holder who have undergone /
          undergoing / already contract has been created in apprenticeship portal or having job experience for more than one
          year, after attainment of these qualifications shall not be eligible for apprenticeship under apprentice act 1961.
     4.   Candidate are not eligible to apply in case of awaiting the results of their final examination and do not have
          Trade/Diploma/Degree Certificate in respective trade/branch.
     5.   Please ensure that gap shall not be more than 5 years from date of passing examination to date of contract creation
          on the respective portal. If this gap is more than 5 years on date of contract creation, contract won’t be created
          by portal and engagement as apprentice won’t be possible. So, candidate having gap of more than 5 years need
          not apply.
     6.   The age of candidates should not be less than 18 years as on the date of notification as per the Apprentices Act,
          1961.
     7.   The selection of SC/ST/OBC will be as per Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 (as amended from time to time) and as per
          the quota fixed by the Govt.
     8.   The caste certificate of SC/ ST is valid only, if issued by Officer mentioned in the Proforma attached as Annexure-
          II published in NMDC website (www.nmdc.co.in.). If not produced in the required format as mentioned above at the
          time of interview, your candidature will not be considered for reserved seats meant for respective category. Please
          visit NMDC web site for required format of caste certificate which is attached with this notification also published
          in NMDC website.
     9.   Caste Certificate for OBC Category (Non creamy layer) is valid only, if issued within 06 (Six) months of date of
          notification in the Proforma issued by the officer mentioned in the attached Annexure-III published in NMDC
          website (www.nmdc.co.in.). If not produced in the required format as mentioned above at the time of interview, your
          candidature will not be considered for OBC reserved seats. Please visit NMDC web site for required format of caste
          certificate which is attached with this notification also published in NMDC website.
     10. Only Indian National are eligible to apply.
11. All the above documents shall be checked by Interview help Desk members and Interview Board members. Interview

12. Suppression of facts will lead to disqualification at any stage. The apprenticeship training scheme does not guarantee
    any employment on completion of apprenticeship training. The period of apprenticeship training will be for one year.
13. B.I.O.M., Kirandul Complex of M/s NMDC LIMITED reserves all the rights to withdraw/cancel the
     advertisement/selection process if circumstances so warrant without assigning any reason thereof.
14. The number of vacancies mentioned above in the table are only indicative and may change at a later stage depending
     on the requirement and final assessment of the eligible candidates.
15. Selection will be made on the basis of an interview performance and satisfactory verification of the documents. If
     documents don’t establish your eligibility, then candidature is liable to cancel at any stage.
16. Candidates must ensure that their name and their father’s name and mother’s name are identical across all documents,
     such as the 10th Mark-sheet, ITI/Diploma/Degree Certificate, Aadhaar card, PAN card, and Bank Passbook. In the
     event of any discrepancy in the name or father’s name or mother’s name across these documents, the candidate must
     get it corrected. This correctness is must for contract creation and stipend payment. In the event of any mismatch the
     contract is not created by portal itself & vendor code is not generated for stipend/DBT payment.

Before interview, candidates shall ensure to bring required documents: -

1.   Approved copy of Profile pages of Registration proof in respective web portal (NAPS / NATS) and 100%
     completed/updated profile in the portal.
2.   Bio-data form (provided at Help Desk) along with one Passport Size Photograph.
3.   Self-attested photocopy of 10th class certificate/ birth certificate for age proof.
4.   Qualifications Certificate and Caste Certificate is must. Original certificates are required for verification purpose.
     Non-production of these certificates will respectively disqualify from appearing in the interview and for reserved
     seats.
5.   Candidates shall fill-up online Google Form (provided at Help Desk) on the date of interview.
The walk-in interview for the above-mentioned disciplines will be conducted from 9:00 AM to 5.30 PM on the
dates mentioned against each category at Training Institute, B.I.O.M, Kirandul Complex, Kirandul, Dist. -
Dantewada (C.G.)- 494556. Candidate must reach the interview site before 01:00 PM to register himself / herself
at interview help desk for appearing in the walk-in interview. Reporting after 1.00 PM will not be entertained.
The candidates appearing for interview shall not be paid or reimbursed any TA/DA.
