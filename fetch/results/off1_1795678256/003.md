   1. About the National Testing Agency

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has been established as a premier, specialist, autonomous and
self-sustained testing organization under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, to conduct
entrance examinations for admission and fellowship in higher educational institutions. NTA conducts
some of the country's largest and most high-stakes examinations, including JEE (Main), NEET (UG),
UGC-NET, CUET (UG & PG), CMAT, GPAT and several others, serving candidates across India and at
select international centers.
NTA invites applications from eligible candidates for engagement to the post of Office Assistant
(OA) on a contractual basis for a period of five (5) years from the date of joining, extendable based
on performance and organizational requirement, subject to the terms and conditions stated in this
advertisement.

   3. Role Overview

The Office Assistant will provide administrative, office support services to facilitate the smooth
functioning of NTA's offices and assigned sections. The incumbent will assist in day-to-day office
administration, record maintenance, movement of files and documents, data entry, correspondence,
coordination and other routine tasks assigned by the Reporting Officer.

•   The engagement shall be purely on a contractual basis for a period of five (5) years from the
