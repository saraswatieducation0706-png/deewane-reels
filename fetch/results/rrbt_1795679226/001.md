### RRB Technician Grade I & III Recruitment 2026 : Eligibility Details

| Post Name | Eligibility   
---|---  
Technician Grade I | 

  * Candidates who have Bachelor Degree of Science in Physics / Electronics / Computer Science / Information Technology / Instrumentation OR
  * B.SC in a Combination of Any Sub Stream of Physics / Electronics / Computer Science / IT / Instrumentation OR
  * BE / B.Tech / 3 Year Engineering Polytechnic Diploma in Above Basic Stream.

  
Technician Grade III (S&T Trade) | 

  * Candidates who have passed 10+2 with Physics and Math OR Class 10th with ITI Certificate from NCVT / SCVT will be eligible for this recruitment.

  
Technician Grade III (Other Trades) | 

  * Candidates who have passed Class 10th with ITI Certificate from NCVT / SCVT in Related Trade / Branch will be eligible for this recruitment.
