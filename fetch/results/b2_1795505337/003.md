The National Divyangjan Finance and Development Corporation (NDFDC), a wholly owned CPSE under the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan), Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, is filling five posts, one each: Chief Manager (Finance), Asst. Manager (Finance), Sr. Executive, Sr. Executive (Accounts) and Jr. Executive. Applications are accepted by post only, addressed to the NDFDC office in New Delhi, and must reach on or before 31 December 2026.

## About NDFDC

NDFDC was set up with the objective of providing financial assistance to Persons with Disability for self-employment. The notice describes it as a wholly owned CPSE, and its office is at DLF Prime Tower, Okhla Phase-I, New Delhi. It says the Corporation is looking for suitable result oriented persons to fill the posts.

## Where and How to Send

Applications must be sent through post only. The notice asks candidates to write in capital letters on top of the envelope: "APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF" followed by the post name. The address is:

National Divyangjan Finance and Development Corporation (Dept. of Empowerment of PwDs (Divyangjan), Ministry of SJ&E, Government of India), Unit No.11&12, Ground Floor, DLF Prime Tower, F-79-80, Okhla Phase-I, New Delhi - 110020.

The application is typed neatly on plain paper in the format below, with a recent passport size photograph and the demand draft where the fee applies. It must be complete in all respects, strictly in the prescribed format, and reach on or before 31 December 2026.

  1. Post applied for.
  2. Name, and Father's/Husband's name.
  3. Date of birth and age as on 30-11-2026 (attach attested copy of birth certificate).
  4. Category (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwDs/XSM/Gen.), with an attested copy of the certificate.
  5. Disability category and percentage of disability, with an attested copy of the disability certificate issued by the competent authority for a Person with Disability.
  6. Present address for communication, permanent home address, contact numbers and e-mail.
  7. Academic/professional qualification in reverse chronological order, starting from the highest: course, discipline, main subjects, university/institution with complete address, year of passing and percentage of marks/division (attach attested copies).
  8. Details of present and past employment: employer, posts held with pay scale, duration, whether revised or pre-revised IDA/CDA pattern, and nature of duties against each post (attach attested copies of certificates).
  9. Demand draft of Rs.500 (non refundable) in favour of National Divyangjan Finance and Development Corporation, with place, date and signature of the candidate.

  * Essential qualification: Degree in Commerce/CA/ICWA/ MBA (Finance) from recognised institute/ university.

### Asst. Manager (Finance)

  * Essential qualification: Qualified Chartered Accountant from ICAI/Qualified Company Secretary from ICSI/Qualified Cost Accountant from ICWAI/02 years MBA (Finance) from recognised University/Institute of repute.

  * Age limit: 37 Years.

  * Essential qualification: Degree in Arts/Science/Commerce from a recognised University.
  * Experience: Minimum 3 years experience in the establishment and general administration.
  * Knowledge of using computer.
  * Desirable: Diploma/Certificate in Personnel Management/Industrial relation; knowledge of typing (Hindi/English).
  * Age limit: 32 Years.



### Sr. Executive (Accounts)

  * Essential qualification: CA (Inter)/ICWA (Inter)/B. Com from recognised Institute/University.
  * Experience: Minimum 2 Years experience in relevant field.
  * Desirable: Knowledge of Tally accounting software.

### Jr. Executive

  * Essential qualification: Degree in Arts/Science/Commerce.
  * A certificate course in computer application of not less than six months duration from a reputed institution.
  * Knowledge of standard software packages (MS-Office, Word, FOXPRO etc.).
  * Data entry speed of not less than 6000 key depressions per hour.
  * Age limit: 30 Years.
