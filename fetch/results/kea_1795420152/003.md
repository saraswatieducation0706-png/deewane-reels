## KEA Selection Process 2026

  1. Preliminary Examination – objective (multiple choice), Offline-OMR type. In each Preliminary paper (Paper-1 and Paper-2), SC and ST candidates must score at least 35% and General and other category candidates at least 40%; those who do so are called to the Main Examination in the ratio of 1:10 to the vacancies.
  2. Main Examination – descriptive, answers written. In each Main paper (Paper-1, Paper-2 and Paper-3), SC and ST candidates must score at least 35% and General and other category candidates at least 40%; those who do so are called to the viva-voce in the ratio of 1:3.
  3. Viva-voce – maximum 25 marks, held by the Selection Committee constituted under Government Order No. ಒಇ 95 ಪಿಪಿಇ 2026 dated 22.09.2026. It tests general knowledge, the ability to grasp points of law, understanding and suitability for the post. Answers may be given in Kannada or English. A candidate absent from the viva-voce is not considered for selection.



### Merit list and final selection

  * After the viva-voce, a merit list is published on the total marks secured in the Main Examination and the interview.
  * The Selection Committee publishes a provisional / final selection list in the ratio of 1:1 to the notified vacancies, following the reservation rules in force.
  * When two or more candidates have equal marks, the older candidate is placed higher.
  * Before appointment, the Department of Prosecutions and Government Litigations verifies marks cards and certificates with the issuing bodies, and also the genuineness of the sannad issued by the Bar Council.

### Reservation certificates

  * Caste / reservation certificates must be obtained from the Tahsildar (signature and seal, or barcode, hologram and watermark, with RD number) by the last date, in the prescribed form: Form D for SC (Category-A, B, C) and ST; Form E for Category-1; Form F for Category 2A, 2B, 3A and 3B.
  * SC, ST and Category-1 certificates are valid until cancelled; 2A, 2B, 3A and 3B certificates are valid for 5 years and must be valid on the last date. Certificates in any other format are rejected.
  * Balija and its sub-castes must claim reservation under Category 3(A) for employment; a 2(A) certificate obtained for education will not be considered.
  * Married women of Category 2A, 2B, 3A and 3B must obtain the income certificate in the husband's name.
  * Rural reservation: only those who studied Class 1 to 10 in rural areas; Form-1 (with RD number) from the Tahsildar and Form-2 from the school headmaster countersigned by the Field Education Officer.
  * Kannada medium reservation: only those who studied Class 1 to 10 in Kannada medium, with a certificate from the school headmaster.
  * Persons with disabilities: 4% reservation in Group-B posts, for disability of not less than 40%, with a medical certificate / UDID; disability must be proved before the medical board set up by the appointing authority.
  * Third gender: 1% of posts in each category, with a certificate from the District Magistrate under the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act, 2019.
  * Sports achievers: 2% of posts in each category, for those living in Karnataka for at least 10 years who represented Karnataka in a recognised National Championship or National Games; weightage marks are given as notified.
  * Kalyana-Karnataka local candidates (Article 371(J)): eligibility certificate in Annexure-A (with RD number) from the Assistant Commissioner of the sub-division; government servants whose service book recorded a Hyderabad-Karnataka native place before 01.01.2013 obtain the native place certificate from their head of office.



### Ex-servicemen

  * Serving armed forces personnel may apply one year before completing their term, with a No Objection Certificate or release certificate obtained before applying.
  * Dependants of ex-servicemen who died or were permanently disabled in service must obtain the certificate before applying.



### Examination day and communication

  * Examination centre details and admit card download will be announced later through a press note and the KEA website.
  * Candidates must bring the admit card and one original photo identity card (PAN card, driving licence, Aadhaar card, passport, voter ID or government employee ID); without these, entry to the centre is not allowed.
  * The centre for the Preliminary and Main Examinations cannot be changed for any reason.
  * KEA does not correspond with candidates. Any letter to KEA must give the application number and post name, full name in capital letters and the postal address entered in the application.
  * Recruitment updates are posted on the KEA website and its official X account @KEA_KARNATAKA. Helpline: 080-23460460 (10.00 AM to 6.00 PM).

## KEA Assistant Public Prosecutor cum Assistant Government Pleader Recruitment 2026 - FAQs

### Q1. How many vacancies are there in KEA Recruitment 2026?

There are 52 Assistant Public Prosecutor-cum-Assistant Government Pleader (Group-B) posts: 38 posts, 04 left-out posts under Article 371(J) and 10 backlog posts.

### Q2. When does the KEA APP-cum-AGP online application start?

Online applications start on 18 November 2026 on the KEA website.

### Q3. What is the last date to apply for KEA Recruitment 2026?

The last date to apply online is 15 December 2026, and the last date to pay the fee online is 16 December 2026.

### Q4. What is the application fee?

The fee is Rs. 750 for General Merit and Category 2A/2B/3A/3B, Rs. 500 for SC/ST/Category-1/ex-servicemen/third gender candidates and Rs. 250 for persons with disabilities. It is paid online and not refunded.

### Q5. What is the age limit for the KEA APP-cum-AGP posts?

Candidates must be at least 25 years old on the last date; the maximum age is 42 years for General Merit, 45 years for Category 2A, 2B, 3A and 3B, and 47 years for SC/ST/Category-1, including the one-time 5-year relaxation.

### Q6. What qualification is required?

An LLB degree, practice as an advocate in civil and criminal courts in India under the Advocates Act, 1961, and not less than 3 years of practice as an advocate as on the last date for applying.

### Q7. What is the salary of an Assistant Public Prosecutor-cum-Assistant Government Pleader?

The pay scale is Rs. 69250-1650-74200-1900-85600-2300-99400-2700-115600-3100-134200 under the Revised State Pay Scale 2024, with NPS and the KGID facility.

### Q8. What is the selection process?

Selection is through an objective Preliminary Examination, a descriptive Main Examination of three law papers and a viva-voce of 25 marks; the final list is based on Main Examination and interview marks.

### Q9. When is the KEA APP Preliminary Examination?

The Preliminary Examination will be held in February 2027; the detailed schedule will be published later.
