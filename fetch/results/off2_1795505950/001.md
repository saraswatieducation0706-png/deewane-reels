Electronics Corporation of India Limited is a Miniratna (Category-I) Public
Sector Enterprise (under Department of Atomic Energy, Govt. of India) engaged
in the area of Strategic Electronics with thrust on innovation & indigenization.
ECIL operates in strategic sectors like Nuclear, Defence, Aerospace, Information
Technology, Telecom, Network & Homeland Security, CBRN and e-Governance.
ECIL pioneered a number of products and technologies include Solid State
Television, Digital Computer, Cockpit Voice Recorders, Electronic Voting
Machines, Programmable Logic Controllers, Earth Station and Deep Space
Network Antennas. It has close collaboration with national R&D laboratories as
well as Academic Institutes and has been involved in the projects of national
importance.

2. AGE LIMIT
Maximum age of 25 years or less as on 31-12-2026. Upper age limit is relaxed
by 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL) & 10 years for PwD (40% and
above disability) candidates.


3. APPRENTICESHIP PERIOD
The tenure of the Apprenticeship would be One Year only, which will commence
from 01-01-2027.

4. ESSENTIAL QUALIFICATION & ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
 a. Candidates who have passed B.E./ B.Tech. course in the above-mentioned
    engineering branches on or after 1st April 2024, from AICTE approved
    colleges / recognized Indian Universities for GEA.
 b. In case of Diploma Apprentices, the candidates who have passed 3 Years
    Diploma course in above mentioned branches on or after 1st April, 2024.
 c. Candidates residing All Over India and only Indian residents are eligible to
     apply.

5. HOW TO APPLY

   STEP 1: Interested candidates who possess the required criteria/educational
   qualification MUST register themselves on the website of the National
   Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) i.e.www.nats.education.gov.in.

   STEP 2: Subsequent to registration in the portal mentioned above, the
   interested eligible candidates shall submit the online application through
   ECIL website: www.ecil.co.in>Careers>Current Job Openings.


   IMPORTANT NOTE: Applications submitted on the ECIL website shall
   only be accepted and considered.

  a. After applying online, the candidate is required to take the printout of the
     registered online application form with system-generated application serial
     number. Candidate is required to note down application serial number,
     for all future references. The candidate can take a re-print of his/her
     registered online application form before the last date for Online
     registration.
  b. The candidate has to quote his/her system-generated                  online
     application number for all future correspondence.
  c. No separate communication will be sent to any candidate on their non-
     selection at any stage of the engagement process.

6.METHOD OF SELECTION

 a. Based on the online applications data, candidates will be called for original
    documents verification at CLDC-ECIL Hyderabad.
 b. The candidates called for document verification shall require to
   present the following original documents mandatorily with a set of
   photocopies at the time of certificate verification:
     i.    Online registered application form duly signed by the candidate and
           with recent color passport size photo affixed.
     ii.   All original certificates in support of NATS Registration, his / her date
           of birth, identity (Govt. issued only like Aadhaar, Driving License,
           Passport etc.), Educational Qualification, Latest Caste & Disability
           (PwD) certificates (wherever applicable).
    iii.   Category Certificate in support of SC/ST/OBC (Non-Creamy
           Layer)/EWS, in the prescribed proforma as hosted on www.ecil.co.in
           portal.
 c. After successful document verification, Apprentices selections will be done
    based on the qualifying examination marks merit (i.e. B.E./B.Tech.
    consolidated marks for GEAs and Diploma consolidated marks for Diploma
    Apprentices). In case of CGPA, relevant CGPA to percentage conversion
    certificate from the college/university should be produced by the
    candidate.
