#  [Gujarat GPRB Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 3000 Constable & MT Supervisor Posts](https://www.indgovtjobs.in/2026/11/gujarat-gprb-recruitment.html)

Last Updated: November 20, 2026 | Author: Admin 

Gujarat GPRB Recruitment 2026-27 of Constable & Other Posts: Gujarat Police Recruitment Board (GPRB) has released the recruitment notification for direct recruitment to various Class-III (Grade-R) posts, including Unarmed Police Constable, Armed Police Constable, Armed Police Constable (SRPF), Head Constable Driver Mechanic and Motor Transport Supervisor. A total of 3,000 vacancies are available under Advertisement Nos. GPRB/202627/1 to GPRB/202627/3. The online application process will commence on 26 November 2026, and eligible candidates can submit their applications up to 16 December 2026. Candidates can check the post-wise vacancies, educational qualifications, selection process, application fee and other details below.

## ✅ Gujarat GPRB Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria:

Post-wise Educational Qualification:

  * Unarmed Police Constable (UPC): Matric + 12th Class Passed. 
  * Armed Police Constable (APC): 12th Class Passed. 
  * Armed Police Constable (SRPF): 12th Class Passed. 
  * Head Constable Driver Mechanic (Grade-2): 12th Passed and Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade Certificate with 01 Year Apprenticeship from a Government Recognized ITI, OR 03 Years Apprenticeship Certificate in Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade (NATS). 
  * Motor Transport Supervisor (Grade-2): 12th Passed and Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade Certificate with 01 Year Apprenticeship from a Government Recognized ITI, OR 03 Years Apprenticeship Certificate in Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade (NATS). 



## ✅ Gujarat GPRB Recruitment 2026 Selection Process:

  * Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST) 
  * Written Examination 
  * Medical Examination 
  * Document Verification 



## ✅ Gujarat GPRB Recruitment 2026 Application Fee:

  * Unreserved Category: ₹100/- 
  * SC / ST / EWS / EBC / ESM: No Fee 
  * Payment Mode: Online Mode
