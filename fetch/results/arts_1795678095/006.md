## SBI SO Selection Process 2026

The selection process consists of an online written examination followed by a personal interview. The final merit calculation gives 70% weightage to Professional Knowledge and 30% to the interview.

  1. Online Written Test: Candidates appear in the objective examination.
  2. Shortlisting: SBI shortlists candidates based on the examination results and applicable requirements.
  3. Personal Interview: Shortlisted candidates attend an interview carrying 100 marks.
  4. Document Verification: Educational qualifications, service experience, medical fitness and category certificates are verified.
  5. Final Merit List: Prepared using normalized Professional Knowledge and interview marks.



SBI reserves the right to modify the selection process, including considering shortlisting followed by interview, if required.

### Important SBI SO Exam Rules

  * Marks obtained in these three qualifying sections are not counted toward final merit.
  * Candidates must obtain the minimum qualifying marks fixed by SBI in the applicable sections.
  * Professional Knowledge carries 100 marks and contributes to final merit.
  * There is no negative marking for incorrect answers.

### Q1. How many vacancies are there in SBI Recruitment 2026 for security posts?

SBI has advertised 60 provisional vacancies for Manager (Security) and Deputy Manager (Security) together: 9 SC, 4 ST, 16 OBC, 6 EWS and 25 UR.

### Q2. When does online registration start for SBI Manager (Security) recruitment?

Online registration and fee payment start on 4 November 2026.

### Q3. What is the last date to apply for SBI Recruitment 2026 for security posts?

The last date for online registration, including editing of the application, and for payment of the fee is 02 December 2026.

### Q4. What is the application fee for SBI Manager and Deputy Manager (Security)?

The fee is ₹750 for UR/EWS/OBC candidates, and SC/ST candidates pay no fee. It is paid online and is non-refundable.

### Q5. What is the age limit for SBI Security Officer posts?

The age limit is minimum 25 years and maximum 40 years as on 31.10.2026, with relaxation in the upper age limit for reserved categories as per Govt. of India guidelines.

### Q6. What qualification and experience are required for Manager (Security)?

A graduate in any discipline from a Government recognized University or Institution who is an officer not below the rank of Major (or equivalent in Navy/Airforce) with minimum 10 years of commissioned service, or not below Deputy Commandant in Para-military forces with minimum 10 years of service as an officer.

### Q7. What experience is required for Deputy Manager (Security)?

An officer not below the rank of Captain in the Indian Army (or equivalent in Navy/Airforce) with minimum 5 years of commissioned service, or not below Assistant Commandant in Para-military forces with minimum 5 years of service as an officer, besides graduation.

### Q8. What is the pay scale for SBI Manager (Security) and Deputy Manager (Security)?

Manager (Security) in MMGS-III gets Rs (85920-2680/5-99320-2980/2-105820) and Deputy Manager (Security) in MMGS-II gets Rs (64820-2340/1-67160-2680/10-93960), plus DA, HRA, CCA, PF, NPS, LFC and medical facility as per rules.

### Q9. Is there negative marking in the SBI Security Officer online test?

No. The notice states there will be no penalty for wrong answers in the objective tests; the test has 200 questions for 200 marks in two hours.

### Q10. What medical category is needed to apply for SBI security posts?

Applicants must have medical fitness in SHAPE-1 or an equivalent medical category as on the date of release of the advertisement, and must upload the supporting document with the online application.
