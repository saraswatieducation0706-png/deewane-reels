Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), New Delhi, Across India, has invited applications for 20 posts in 2 roles under Advt No. MeitY/2026/1. The posts include Electronics & Communication Engineering, Computer Science & Information Technology. Applications are accepted online; the last date is given in the notification.

The posts are for New Delhi. Qualification required: Valid Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) Score (2024/2025/2026). The pay is Level 10 of the Pay Matrix (Rs. 56,100 - Rs. 1,77,500). Selection is by valid Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) Score (2024/2025/2026) and personal Interview. Post-wise eligibility, dates and the steps to apply are given below.
