## UKSSSC Sub Inspector Selection Process 2026

UKSSSC will conduct the recruitment in two principal competitive stages. The first stage consists of physical standards and physical efficiency examinations, while the second stage consists of a 200-mark objective written examination for candidates who qualify in the physical tests.

  1. Physical Standards Test (PST): Candidates must satisfy the prescribed physical measurement requirements applicable to their selected posts.
  2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET): Candidates must qualify in the prescribed physical activities.
  3. Written Examination: Candidates who qualify in the physical examinations will be eligible to appear for the objective competitive written examination.
  4. Document Verification: Educational qualifications, age, reservation claims and other eligibility documents will be verified.
  5. Medical Examination: Candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical and physical fitness requirements before appointment.



The final merit list will be prepared on the basis of performance in the written examination, subject to the applicable eligibility, reservation and appointment conditions.
