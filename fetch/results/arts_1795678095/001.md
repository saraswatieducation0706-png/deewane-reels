Engineers India Limited (EIL) has published the Recruitment 2026 notification for 87 Associate Engineer posts. The online application process will be conducted from 25 November 2026 to 09 December 2026. Eligible candidates can apply through the official website, engineersindia.com. 

## EIL Quick Summary

2026 · 87 Posts

COMPANY NAME Engineers India Limited

POST NAME Associate Engineer

QUALIFICATION B.Tech/B.E, B.Sc

VACANCY 87 Posts

AGE LIMIT 37 Years (Max)

LAST DATE 09-12-2026

EIL Recruitment 2026: Engineers India Limited (EIL), a Navratna Government of India undertaking, has released Advertisement No. HRD/Rectt./Advt./2026-27/12 for recruitment to 87 Associate Engineer Grade I and Grade II posts on a fixed-term basis. The vacancies are available in Electrical, Instrumentation, Piping, Civil, Structural, Safety, Environment, Mechanical, Process and other engineering disciplines. Candidates possessing a BE, B.Tech or B.Sc. Engineering degree with a minimum of 60% marks and two or five years of relevant post-qualification experience can apply, depending on the position. The maximum age is 32 years for Grade I and 37 years for Grade II, before applicable relaxation. Selected candidates will receive consolidated monthly remuneration ranging from Rs. 57,600 to Rs. 80,000, depending on their grade and posting city. Online applications open on 25 November 2026 at 2:00 PM and close on 9 December 2026 at 2:00 PM through the official EIL recruitment portal.

The Engineers India Limited Associate Engineer Recruitment 2026 will be conducted through application screening and a personal interview. Walk-in interviews will be organized at EIL offices in Gurugram, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru and Vadodara. Candidates must complete online registration before attending the interview and carry six printed copies of their submitted application along with original educational, experience and other required certificates. The appointment is initially for two years and may be extended up to a maximum total period of three years, subject to organizational requirements and satisfactory performance. The exact interview date, reporting instructions and venue will be communicated separately to registered candidates by email.
