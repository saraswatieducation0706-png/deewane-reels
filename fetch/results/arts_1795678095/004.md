NMDC Limited, Bailadila Iron Ore Mine Kirandul Complex, is conducting a Walk-in Interview for 247 vacancies, including Trade Apprentice, Graduate Apprentice, and Technician Apprentice posts. Candidates meeting the prescribed eligibility criteria can attend the interview on 14 December 2026. Further details are available at nmdc.co.in. 

## NMDC Quick Summary

2026 · 247 Posts

COMPANY NAME NMDC Limited

POST NAME Trade Apprentice, Graduate Apprentice and More

QUALIFICATION 10TH, 12TH, B.Tech/B.E, Diploma, ITI

VACANCY 247 Posts

AGE LIMIT 18 Years (Min)

LAST DATE 22-12-2026

NMDC Limited, Kirandul, Chhattisgarh, has invited applications for 247 posts in 20 roles under Advt No. KC/T & S/ 2026. The posts include Trade Apprentice - Machinist, Trade Apprentice - Fitter, Trade Apprentice - Welder, Trade Apprentice - Mechanic Diesel and 16 more. Selection is through a walk-in interview on 14 December 2026.

The posts are for Bailadila Iron Ore Mine, Kirandul Complex, Dantewada (Chhattisgarh). Qualification required: Trade Apprentices: 10th pass with ITI/Trade Certificate (NCVT/SCVT) in relevant trade. Graduate Apprentices: full time Bachelor's Degree in the respective discipline. Technician (Diploma) Apprentices: full-time Diploma in the respective discipline. The pay is Stipend as per the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 (as amended from time to time). Selection is by interview and verification of documents. Post-wise eligibility, dates and the steps to apply are given below.

## Key Points

  * Walk-in date: 14 December 2026 to 22 December 2026
  * Vacancies: 247, in 20 roles
  * How to apply: Attend the walk-in interview
  * Pay: Stipend as per the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 (as amended from time to time)
  * Fee: No application fee is mentioned in the notification
  * Age limit: Minimum 18 years (as on the date of notification)
  * Place: Bailadila Iron Ore Mine, Kirandul Complex, Dantewada (Chhattisgarh)

  * Reporting after 1:00 PM will not be entertained.



## Documents to Enclose

  * Approved copy of profile pages of registration proof on NAPS / NATS portal with 100% completed/updated profile
  * Bio-data form (provided at Help Desk) with one passport size photograph
  * Self-attested photocopy of 10th class certificate / birth certificate for age proof
  * Original qualification certificates
  * Caste certificate (SC/ST per Annexure-II, OBC Non creamy layer per Annexure-III issued within 06 months of notification date)



## How to Apply for NMDC Recruitment 2026

  1. Trade Apprentice candidates register and get approval in advance on the NAPS portal (https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in).
  2. Graduate and Technician (Diploma) Apprentice candidates register and get approval in advance on the NATS portal (https://nats.education.gov.in).
  3. Reach Training Institute, B.I.O.M, Kirandul Complex, Kirandul, Dantewada on the walk-in date for your category before 01:00 PM.
  4. Register at the interview help desk and fill the Bio-data form provided there with one passport size photograph.
  5. Fill the online Google Form provided at the Help Desk on the date of interview.
  6. Bring portal profile proof, age proof copy, original qualification and caste certificates.
  7. Attend on 14 December 2026.

## NMDC Recruitment 2026 - FAQs

### Q1. When is the walk-in interview for NMDC Recruitment 2026?

The walk-in interview is on 14 December 2026.

### Q2. How many vacancies are there in NMDC Recruitment 2026?

There are 247 vacancies: 4 Trade Apprentice - Machinist, 16 Trade Apprentice - Fitter, 27 Trade Apprentice - Welder, 32 Trade Apprentice - Mechanic Diesel, 12 Trade Apprentice - Mechanic Motor Vehicle, 34 Trade Apprentice - Electrician and 14 more posts.

### Q3. How do I apply for NMDC Recruitment 2026?

Attend the walk-in interview on 14 December 2026. The steps and the documents to bring are under How to Apply above.

### Q4. What is the age limit for this recruitment?

The minimum age is 18 years (as on the date of notification).

### Q5. What is the salary for this recruitment?

It depends on the post: Stipend as per the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 (as amended from time to time) for Trade Apprentice - Machinist, Stipend as per the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 (as amended from time to time) for Trade Apprentice - Fitter, Stipend as per the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 (as amended from time to time) for Trade Apprentice - Welder, Stipend as per the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 (as amended from time to time) for Trade Apprentice - Mechanic Diesel, Stipend as per the Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 (as amended from time to time) for Trade Apprentice - Mechanic Motor Vehicle.

### Q6. What qualification is needed for this recruitment?

Trade Apprentices: 10th pass with ITI/Trade Certificate (NCVT/SCVT) in relevant trade. Graduate Apprentices: full time Bachelor's Degree in the respective discipline. Technician (Diploma) Apprentices: full-time Diploma in the respective discipline. The requirement for each post is in the eligibility table above.

### Q7. Are there other conditions for this recruitment besides qualification and age?

Yes. Trade apprentices must register and get approval in advance on NAPS portal; Registration No. is must for contract generation. Graduate and Diploma apprentices must register and get approval in advance on NATS portal. Gap from passing examination to contract creation must not be more than 5 years. Name, father's name and mother's name must be identical across 10th mark-sheet, certificates, Aadhaar, PAN and bank passbook.

### Q8. Who cannot apply for this recruitment?

Graduates, Diploma or Trade Certificate holders who have undergone / are undergoing apprenticeship or whose contract is already created in the apprenticeship portal. Those having job experience of more than one year after attaining these qualifications. Candidates awaiting results of their final examination and without Trade/Diploma/Degree Certificate. Candidates having a gap of more than 5 years need not apply.

### Q9. How will candidates be selected for this recruitment?

Interview. Verification of documents. Selection on the basis of interview performance and satisfactory verification of documents. Documents checked by Interview help desk members and Interview Board members before interview.

### Q10. What are the terms of engagement for this recruitment?

Apprenticeship training period is one year. Apprenticeship training does not guarantee any employment on completion.
