The National Testing Agency, an autonomous organization under the Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education, Government of India, has published an advertisement for the contractual engagement of Office Assistants.

The recruitment aims to strengthen administrative and office support services at NTA. Selected candidates will assist with record maintenance, clerical activities, official correspondence, data entry, file management and coordination of examination-related activities.

The official advertisement contains details regarding vacancies, qualifications, desirable experience, indicative remuneration, contractual conditions, selection procedures and the prescribed application form.

## NTA Office Assistant Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates applying for NTA Office Assistant posts must possess a recognized graduate degree, working knowledge of computer applications and good communication skills in English and Hindi. Relevant administrative or clerical work experience is desirable rather than an explicitly mandatory requirement.

### Educational Qualification

  * Graduate degree in any discipline from a recognized university or institution.
  * Working knowledge of computers, including MS Word, MS Excel, PowerPoint, email and internet-based applications.
  * Good written and verbal communication skills in English and Hindi.



### Desirable Experience and Skills

NTA has identified the following experience and professional skills as desirable for Office Assistant positions:

  * Experience in office administration or clerical work.
  * Experience in data entry, file management or secretarial assistance.
  * Familiarity with e-Office systems and digital record management.
  * Knowledge of routine administrative and office procedures.
  * Ability to organize work and meet deadlines.
  * Ability to coordinate with multiple stakeholders.



### Can Fresh Graduates Apply for NTA Office Assistant Recruitment 2026?

Fresh graduates who satisfy the prescribed educational, computer proficiency, communication and age requirements may apply because the advertisement lists relevant work experience as desirable. NTA has not specified a mandatory minimum number of years of work experience.

However, NTA reserves the right to determine its shortlisting parameters and selection criteria. Meeting the minimum eligibility requirements does not guarantee shortlisting or selection.

Office Assistants will provide administrative and clerical support to facilitate the smooth functioning of NTA offices and assigned sections. The role involves maintaining records, handling official correspondence and assisting with routine administrative activities.

Confidentiality | Ensure safe custody and confidentiality of official records and information
