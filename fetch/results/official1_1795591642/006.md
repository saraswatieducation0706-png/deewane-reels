Company Profile:
 Incorporation & Ownership
 THDC India Limited is a leading profit making Public Sector Enterprise, which was registered as a Public
 Limited Company in July 1988 under the Companies Act, 1956 with equity participation in the ratio of 75:25
 between the Government of India and the Government of Uttar Pradesh. THDCIL was granted 'Mini Ratna –
 Category-I' status in October 2009 by the Government of India and upgraded to a 'Schedule 'A' PSU in July
 2010. Post the strategic sale on March 25, 2020, the equity in THDC India Limited is now shared between
 NTPC Limited and Government of Uttar Pradesh in the ratio of 74.496:25.504.


 THDCIL has been making profit since its first commercial year (2006-07), which started with its first project
 i.e. Tehri HPP (1000 MW). Since then, THDCIL has expanded its scope and diversified across all
 conventional and non-conventional sources of energy.
 Business Activities
 THDCIL has grown from a water-intensive utility to a diversified energy sector undertaking. The company
 is engaged in the development, operation and maintenance of projects in the hydro, thermal, wind, solar and
 coal mining sectors with its commitment to sustainable energy. THDCIL has been actively participating in
 various measures to facilitate transition in the energy sector.
 THDCIL’s Projects:
 Power Plants under Operation:
 At present, THDCIL has a total installed capacity of 3,918 MW, which includes 2,424 MW from hydropower
 (Tehri Hydro Power Project 1,000 MW, Tehri PSP 1000 MW, Koteshwar HEP 400 MW and Dhukwan SHP
 24 MW), 113 MW from Wind Power (Patan 50 MW and Devbhoomi Dwarka 63 MW), 61 MW Solar Power
 (Kasaragod Solar Power Plant at Kerala – 50 MW and Khurja Floating Solar power plant, Uttar Pradesh – 11
 MW) and 1,320 MW thermal power (Khurja Super Thermal Power Project in Uttar Pradesh) are included. In
 addition, THDCIL operates Amelia coal mine in Madhya Pradesh with a production capacity of 5.6 million
 tonnes per annum.
 Projects Under Construction:
 THDCIL's Vishnugad Pipalkoti Hydroelectric Project (444 MW) is currently under construction in the state
 of Uttarakhand.


 Financial Performance:
 The authorized share capital of the company as of 31.03.2026 is ₹6,000 crore, including a paid-up capital of
 ₹4358.53 crore. THDCIL started making profit from the first year of commercial operation of its flagship
 Tehri Hydro Electric Plant (1000 MW) (2006-07) and since then it has maintained a consistent track record
 of profitability.


 Projects Pipeline & Expansion
 THDCIL is pursuing an ambitious expansion strategy with a diverse portfolio of projects at various stages of
 development in several states of the country, including Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh,
 Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Chhattisgarh. The company's growth roadmap primarily focuses on hydropower
 projects, pump storage, solar energy, and other projects, reflecting its commitment to sustainable and clean
 energy.
 Additionally, THDCIL is actively pursuing the development of Ultra Mega Renewable Energy Power Parks
 (UMREPPs) through strategic joint ventures with States like Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan. These partnerships
 aim to set up large-scale solar and hybrid projects to accelerate the expansion towards clean energy.
 With a robust project pipeline and strategic collaboration, THDCIL is poised to significantly increase its
 installed capacity over the coming decade, further strengthening its role as a major contributor to India's
 sustainable energy infrastructure.

         Sectionà New Job Opening. No other means/ mode of Application Form shall be accepted. Candidates
         shall only apply through online registration link. Before filling the Online Application Form please read
         eligibility criteria and essential qualification required carefully


 II.     Candidates must fill in their Basic Information.
         Candidates must enter the same name in the same configuration as mentioned in their Xth
         Marksheet certificate.


III.     Must carefully fill the fields asked in the Basic Information. Such as State Domicile, Category, Religion
         and other information asked.


IV.      Fill in the details of Academics & Professional Qualification as per your marksheet and certificate only.
         Provide marks as per your marksheet. In case of CGPA provide correct marks in percentage after
         conversion.


 V.      While filling in the Languages Known remember to tick the boxes provided in that section as well. Next
         fill in Address details and other information.


VI.      Fill in the details of your experience with proper mention of period of service.


VII.     Remember to upload all the documents as per mentioned format and size. Please see that all documents
         uploaded are clear and readable. (Candidate’s online application form is liable to be rejected if they
         have uploaded supporting documents that are not clear and unreadable)
VIII.      Upon completing the filling of the details in Registration form, candidate has to click the Save button
           and then proceed further:
               Ø For the candidates belonging to General, OBC(NCL) and EWS, Payment Now option will
                   appear before final submission of Registration Form, the candidate has to click on Make
                   Payment and complete the payment process. After completion of Payment process, the
                   candidates shall complete the application form by checking Payment details(auto fetched)
                   and then shall click on the Submit Button at the bottom of the page.
               Ø For ST/SC/PwBDs / Ex-Servicemen /Doob Kshetra (Project Affected)/ Department
                   Candidates (THDCIL Employees only) need not pay the Registration Fee and no Make
                   Payment option will appear, for such candidates, they have to directly click on Submit button
                   at the bottom for Final Submission.


               IMPORTANT NOTE: After clicking of Submit button (subsequent to successful payment of
               application fees and filling of payment details(auto fetched) in the online form in case of
               General/OBC/EWS candidates) the Registration Form will be considered complete and submitted
               for Final Submission.


 IX.       Candidate should take a print out of completed application form and keep it with him/ her safely for future
           reference. (This print out of application should be kept in safe custody of the candidate. Candidates
           must not send this printout to any office of THDCIL).


  X.       Other Points:
        Ø All Information regarding this engagement process shall be made available in the career section of our
           website http://www.thdc.co.in and no separate communication shall be made.
        Ø Applicants should keep sufficient copies of same photographs in reserve for future use, which they are
           using in the Online Application Form.
