       Recruitment of Trainee Engineer/Officer in E&C, Environment, IT, Geology, Geophysicist discipline through GATE-2026 Score
NHPC Limited, established in 1975, is a Schedule–A Enterprise of the Government of India and proudly holds the distinguished 'Navratna' status. Renowned for its engineering excellence, NHPC
is a leading organization in hydropower development across India and neighboring countries. From its modest beginning with just three hydro projects, NHPC has evolved into a leading organization
in hydropower sector.

Currently, NHPC's total installed capacity is 9582.90 MW including 1681.70 MW in Joint Venture, comprising 9021.20 MW from 24 Hydro Power Stations, 511.70 MW from Six Solar Power Projects
and 50 MW from a Wind Power Project. NHPC's hydro share of 9021.20 MW is approximately 17.24% of the country's total installed hydro capacity of 52314.66 MW (as on 04.11.2026, as per
CEA website). NHPC has been earning profit for the last many years with Profit after Tax of Rs.3618 Crore during FY 2025-26.For more information, please visit the website of NHPC
(www.nhpcindia.com)

The Company offers dynamic career opportunities, a stimulating work environment, and an attractive compensation package. To support its robust growth trajectory, the Company is looking for
