 AGE RELAXATION
   •    Age relaxation for SC/ST & OBC candidates will be 5 years & 3 years respectively and for PwBD candidates the maximum age limit
        will be 45 years irrespective of his/her actual category.
   •    SC, ST, OBC candidates, in respect of whom the caste certificates are issued from the authorities of states other than West Bengal,
        shall be treated as General Candidates and no relaxation will be allowed to them.
   •    Candidates applying against Exempted Category (EC) shall be entitled to age relaxation up to 45 years.
   •    Upper age limit is also relaxable for Ex-Servicemen who have put in not less than 6 (six) months’ continuous service in any rank
        (whether as a Combatant or as a Non-Combatant) in the Armed Forces of the Union. Such Ex-Servicemen shall be allowed to deduct
        the period of such service from his actual age and if the resultant age does not exceed the maximum age limit prescribed for the post
        for which he/she seeks appointment by more than 3 (three) years, he/she shall be deemed to have satisfied the condition regarding
        age limit.
  •     Eligible Employees of WBSETCL will get age relaxation up to the age of 45 years irrespective of their caste category.

 MODE OF SELECTION
        The applicants will be allowed to appear for Computer Based Test (CBT) based on the self-declaration submitted by them at the
time of online registration The Computer Based Test and Personal Interview for the notified posts will be held in Kolkata. However,
WBSETCL, at its discretion, may conduct the CBT in multiple cities within the State of West Bengal depending on the number of applicants.
If large numbers of applications are received for a particular post, the CBT may be conducted in multiple sessions/shifts with different sets
of question papers and in such cases, normalization formula as approved by the Competent Authority of WBSETCL.
                                                                                                                                     Page 7 of 14
       The candidates will be shortlisted for Interview in 1:3 ratio in each category based on their performance in the Computer Based Test.
Final selection will be made on the basis of fulfilment of prescribed eligibility criteria, combined performance in the Computer Based Test and
Interview, fitness in pre-employment medical examination, available vacancies and existing reservation rules of Govt. of West Bengal.
       In case of tie in scores of two or more candidates in the stages of the selection process, for determining merit position under this
Notification, the following methods will be adopted by WBSETCL:
       A. Tie-breaking rule for the posts under Sl. No. 1, 2, 3 & 5:
          i. If the aggregate/combined marks in the online examination (CBT) be same, the candidate scoring higher marks in Domain
             Knowledge (Part-A) in the online examination (CBT) will be placed higher in the merit list.
         ii. If the aggregate/combined marks and also the marks in Domain Knowledge (Part-A) in the online examination (CBT) be same,
             the candidate scoring higher in Bengali/Nepali Test (Part- B) in the online examination (CBT) will be placed higher in the merit
             list.
        iii. If the aggregate/combined marks, the marks in Domain Knowledge (Part-A) & the marks in Bengali/Nepali Test (Part- B) in
             the online examination (CBT) be same, the candidate scoring higher in General English (Part- C), in the online examination (CBT)
             will be placed higher in the merit list.
        iv. If the aggregate/combined marks, the marks in Domain Knowledge (Part-A), the marks in Bengali/Nepali Test (Part- B) & the
             marks in General English (Part- C) in the online examination (CBT) be same, the candidate scoring higher in General Aptitude
             (Part- D) in the online examination (CBT) will be placed higher in the merit list.
         v. If the aggregate/combined marks, the marks in Domain Knowledge (Part-A), the marks in Bengali/Nepali Test (Part- B), the
             marks in General English (Part- C) & the marks in General Aptitude (Part- D) in the online examination (CBT) be same, the
             candidate senior in age will be placed higher in the merit list.
      B. Tie-breaking rule for the post under Sl. No. 4:
        i.   If the aggregate/combined marks in the online examination (CBT) be same, the candidate scoring higher marks in Bengali/Nepali
             Test (Part-A) in the online examination (CBT) will be placed higher in the merit list.
       ii.   If the aggregate/combined marks and also the marks in Bengali/Nepali Test (Part-A) in the online examination (CBT) be same,
             the candidate scoring higher in General English (Part- B) in the online examination (CBT) will be placed higher in the merit list.
      iii.   If the aggregate/combined marks, the marks in Bengali/Nepali Test (Part-A) & the marks in General English (Part- B) in the
             online examination (CBT) be same, the candidate scoring higher in Arithmetic (Part- C) in the online examination (CBT) will be
             placed higher in the merit list.
      iv.    If the aggregate/combined marks, the marks in Bengali/Nepali Test (Part-A), the marks in General English (Part- B) & the marks
             in Arithmetic (Part- C) in the online examination (CBT) be same, the candidate scoring higher in Reasoning (Part- D) in the online
             examination (CBT) will be placed higher in the merit list.

 PROBATION PERIOD

       The candidates will be appointed provisionally after being found medically fit and after completion of Police Verification/Caste
Certificate verification/ Meritorious Sportsperson Certificate verification/ PwBD Certificate verification/ EWS Certificate verification
process, where applicable. Selected Candidates will be appointed in the regular Pay Band initially on probation for a period of one year,
except the post of Linesman. During the period of probation, they will have to undergo extensive orientation / on-the-job and class room
training in the Company’s training centre / establishments. On successful completion of probation, they may be confirmed under the
Company as per rules. For the post of Linesman, the selected candidates have to undergo a 3 (three) months’ training period and after
successful completion of the said training period and subsequently being successful in the written examination as conducted by WBSETCL,
they will be absorbed in the regular Pay Band. They will remain on probation for a period of one year (one year will be calculated after 3
months’ training period and from the date of their absorption in regular establishment).
