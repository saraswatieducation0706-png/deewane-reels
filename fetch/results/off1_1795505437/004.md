## Frequently Asked Questions

How many vacancies are announced for Coconut Development Board Recruitment 2026?

A total of 35 Group A, B and C vacancies have been announced across 13 designations, including Lower Division Clerk, Field Officer, Deputy Director and other posts.

What is the last date to apply for Coconut Development Board Recruitment 2026?

Candidates must apply within 30 days from the advertisement's appearance in Employment News dated 21–27 November 2026. The exact closing date and time should be confirmed on the official application portal.

How many LDC vacancies are available in Coconut Development Board Recruitment 2026?

There are 13 Lower Division Clerk vacancies, including eight UR, three OBC and two SC positions. Two of the 13 vacancies are reserved for Ex-Servicemen.

What is the qualification for Coconut Development Board LDC Recruitment 2026?

Candidates must have passed Higher Secondary or Class 12 from a recognised board or institute and possess computer typing speed of 35 words per minute in English or 30 words per minute in Hindi. Graduation is a desirable qualification.

What is the age limit for Coconut Development Board Recruitment 2026?

The upper age limit varies from 27 to 40 years depending on the post. Age relaxation is available for eligible candidates according to the official notification.

What is the salary for Coconut Development Board LDC?

The Lower Division Clerk post carries Pay Level 2, with a basic pay scale of Rs.19,900 to Rs.63,200.

What is the application fee for Coconut Development Board Recruitment 2026?

The application fee is Rs.300 through SBI Collect for non-exempt candidates. SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and women candidates are exempted from paying the fee.

Can 12th-pass candidates apply for Coconut Development Board Recruitment 2026?

Yes. Candidates who have passed Class 12 may be eligible for Lower Division Clerk. Field Officer applicants must also have studied Science and possess the prescribed Agriculture or Horticulture diploma or certificate qualification.
