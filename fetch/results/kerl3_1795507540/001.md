## Selection Procedure

The detailed selection procedure has been mentioned in the official notification of Kerala High Court Civil Judge Junior Division Recruitment 2026. But we are giving you here the short summary of the selection procedure. The candidates are advised to refer to official notification for selection procedure. 

Selection Procedure:

  1. Stage 1 – Preliminary Examination (Tentative Date: 07.02.2027): Objective type screening test with 100 MCQs, each carrying 2 marks (Total: 200 marks). Duration: 2.5 hours. Negative marking: 1 mark deducted per wrong answer. Ratio of notified vacancies to qualified candidates: 1:10. Marks in Preliminary Examination are NOT counted in final merit.
  2. Stage 2 – Main (Written) Examination: Consists of 4 papers, each carrying 100 marks (Total: 400 marks). Duration: 3 hours per paper. Candidates shortlisted at ratio of up to 3 times the notified vacancies are called for Viva-Voce.
  3. Viva-Voce: Maximum marks: 50. Cut-off: 40% for General/OBC candidates; 35% for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities, SC and ST candidates. Viva-voce marks are added to Main Written Examination marks for final merit.
  4. Training: Selected candidates are designated as ‘Trainee Judicial Officers’ and undergo 1 year of intensive training at Kerala Judicial Academy (paid at 50% of Civil Judge pay), followed by 1 year of structured Law Clerkship (6 months under Principal District/Sessions Judges or Higher Judicial Services members, and 6 months under a Hon’ble Judge of the High Court of Kerala).
  5. Regular Appointment: Upon satisfactory evaluation after Law Clerkship, the Trainee Judicial Officer is appointed to the regular post with full pay scale and service benefits.
