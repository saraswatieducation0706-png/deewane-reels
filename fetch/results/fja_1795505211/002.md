## KPESRB Quick Summary

2026 · 33 Posts

COMPANY NAME Kerala Public Enterprises (Selection & Recruitment) Board

POST NAME Engineer, Supervisor and More

QUALIFICATION Any Graduate, B.Tech/B.E, LLB, CA, ICWA, MBA/PGDM, MSW

VACANCY 33 Posts

LAST DATE 19-12-2026

Kerala Public Enterprises Recruitment 2026: The Kerala Public Enterprises (Selection & Recruitment) Board (KPESRB) has released 17 recruitment notifications for 33 vacancies in various Kerala Government public sector undertakings. The recruitment covers Commercial Manager, Business Development Executive, Legal Officer, Advisor, Technical Advisor, MEP Engineer, Chief Financial Officer, Deputy Manager, Supervisor, Junior Manager, General Manager, Assistant Manager and Company Secretary posts. Candidates possessing the prescribed Engineering Degree, Diploma, Graduation, MBA, LLB, CA, CMA, Company Secretary or other professional qualifications, along with relevant experience, can apply according to the selected position. The age limits range from 35 to 65 years for different posts. Online applications opened on 20 November 2026 for most categories and on 21 November 2026 for Commercial Manager. The last date to apply is 19 December 2026 through the official KPESRB recruitment portal.

The KPESRB Recruitment 2026 covers Category Nos. 020/2026 to 036/2026, with vacancies in Kerala State Industrial Development Corporation Limited (KSIDC), Kerala State Textile Corporation Limited (KSTC), Kerala State Cashew Development Corporation Limited (KSCDC), Kerala Transport Development Finance Corporation Limited (KTDFC), Steel Industrials Kerala Limited (SILK) and Kerala Lifesciences Industries Parks Private Limited (KLIP). The notifications include both permanent and contractual positions. Monthly consolidated remuneration for contractual posts ranges from Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 3,00,000, while permanent posts have separate pay scales. Candidates must submit their essential qualification and experience certificates while applying online. Applications without the required documents will be rejected.
