Candidates need a B.E/B.Tech/BSc Engg, MCA or MSc in the listed disciplines with 2 to 8 years of experience. Online registration on bel-india.in opens on 24 November 2026, and the last date is 9 December 2026.

## BEL Recruitment 2026 Job Description

### Database Administrator (Sr FOE)

  * To provide highly specialized technical expertise towards administration of databases.
  * Installation of database, creation of schemas, table spaces, defining users, user profiles, memory utilization, caching etc.
  * Monitoring and maintenance of databases, installation of database software patches, monitoring of backups, standardization and implementation of databases.
  * Improving management of production and test environments and resolving problems with application databases.
  * Day-to-day tasks such as allocating volumes, managing zones, LUN etc., fabric security, utilization analysis, performance tuning and system updates or fixes.



Skills asked for: good understanding of the underlying operating system; knowledge of physical database design; database and operating system performance tuning and monitoring; proficiency with MS SQL, Oracle Database and Hadoop; programming with experience in handling standby databases preferred.

### Sr System Administrator (Sr FOE)

  * Maintaining the optimum performance of the Emergency Response system.
  * Handling System Administration challenges for complex and large-scale systems.
  * Monitoring system activities, utilization and resources, capacity control, performance tuning, system updates or fixes, adding or deleting users and generating reports.
  * Ensuring systems security through usage policies, backup and restore, etc.
  * Granting access and permission to various users.



Skills asked for: sys admin of RDMB data (MS-SQL, Oracle etc.), sysadmin of windows-2008/2012 etc. servers, programming in .NET, SQL, PL/SQL, etc.

### Sr Network Administrator (Sr FOE)

  * Installation, setup or configuration, troubleshooting, tuning, diagnostics and maintenance of networking and related equipment.
  * Coordinating with other vendors or agencies to resolve network issues.
  * Managing deployment and maintenance of IT Security infrastructure and compliance with IT security policy directives and regulations of GoUP.
  * Monitoring content filtering and blocking, virus protection and vulnerability protection tools.
  * Escalating security breaches and installing patches for threats related to OEM products.
  * Maintaining an updated knowledge base of published security vulnerabilities and virus threats.



Skills asked for: Routing and Switching devices and technologies like ATM, Frame Relay, MPLS, Wireless, Broadband and Protocol Analysis Tools; intermediate skills in Anti-virus, Firewalls, 2 & 3 factor Authentication, IDS, IPS, Content Filtering, Encryption, VPN and Threat Management; familiarity with Information Security Audit parameters.

### Sr Application Developer (Sr FOE)

  * Working closely with analysts, solution architects, database designers and UP112 to customize the ER applications.
  * Gathering business requirements and developing specifications for the ER applications.
  * Producing detailed specifications and writing program code.
  * Unit-testing software in controlled, real situations before going live.
  * Preparing training manuals and assisting System administrators in maintaining the systems.



Skills asked for: cloud message APIs and push notifications; Google's Android design principles and interface guidelines; ability to design, develop and support apps and perform unit and integration testing.

### SOC Expert (Security Operations Centre Expert, Sr FOE)

  * 24 X 7 monitoring with onsite personnel, reporting security alerts and incidents, and capturing data from all devices on a real time basis.
  * Investigating all suspicious activities and maintaining secure monitoring tools.
  * Reviewing and reporting on cybersecurity processes and keeping security programs and resources up to date.
  * Detecting internal and external attacks on UP112 infrastructure.
  * Providing security reports to UP112 or any other agency daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly (when required), and assisting in security audits.
  * Compliance with security policy and any policy defined by cert-in.



Skills asked for: knowledge of networking and security.

### Sr VAPT Expert (Senior Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing Expert, Sr FOE)

  * Prior experience of working with standards such as ISO 27001, ISO 20000, COBIT framework and ITIL.
  * Understanding of applicable laws, regulatory requirements and frameworks, with good IT auditing skills.
  * Comprehensive knowledge of network scanning, firewall logging, penetration testing and related domains.
  * Analysing network scans, pen test results, firewall logs or vulnerability scan results to find anomalies suggesting a malware attack or malicious event.
  * Rigorous analysis to identify vulnerabilities, risks and threats, and testing customized patching solutions.
  * Managing the compliance efforts of all internal and outsourced functions with information security responsibilities.



### System Admin (FOE)

  * Maintaining the optimum performance of the Emergency Response system.
  * Handling System Administration challenges for complex and large-scale systems.
  * Monitoring system activities, utilization and resources, capacity control, performance tuning, updates or fixes, adding or deleting users and generating reports.
  * Ensuring systems security through usage policies, backup and restore, etc.
  * Granting access and permission to various users.



Skills asked for: sys admin of RDMB data (MS-SQL, Oracle etc.), sysadmin of windows-2008/2012 etc. servers, programming in .NET, SQL, PL/SQL, etc.

### IT Support staff (FOE)

  * First level diagnosis and troubleshooting of hardware and software installed at the office locations.
  * Troubleshooting of MDT, Radio devices, Desktop, Network, Telephony and other software deployed.



### IT Helpdesk Staff (Project Engineer-I)

  * Dedicated personnel for the Project, handling all IT related problems.
  * Creating issue tickets and providing immediate solutions physically or remotely (if possible).
  * First level diagnosis and troubleshooting of network, IP phones, applications, OS, Internet Explorer, Open Office, messaging solutions, Anti-virus, etc.
  * Monitoring and responding quickly to requests received through the IT helpdesk.

## Selection and Interview

Selection for Sr FOE, FOE and Project Engineers is through a written test followed by an interview for shortlisted candidates. All candidates who meet the eligibility criteria will be called for the written test. From those who pass, a category-wise list is drawn and normally candidates in the ratio of 1:10 in each category are called for the interview; where a category has fewer candidates, all who passed the written test are called.

Category | Written Test (minimum) | Interview (minimum)  
---|---|---  
General / EWS / OBC-NCL | 35% | 35%  
SC / ST / PwBD | 30% | 30%  
  
The assessment weightage is 85% for the written test and 15% for the interview. Candidates are considered for final selection only if they score more than the qualifying marks in the personal interview. Shortlists for the written test and interview and the final selections will be notified on the BEL website, and call letters will be sent by email only.

## BEL Recruitment 2027 - FAQs

### Q1. How many vacancies are there in BEL Recruitment 2026?

There are 23 vacancies: 8 Senior Field Operation Engineers, 7 Field Operation Engineers and 8 Project Engineer-I, all for the NexGen UP112 project.

### Q2. What is the last date to apply for BEL Recruitment 2026?

Online registration opens on 24 November 2026, and the last date for online registration and submission of the application is 9 December 2026.

### Q3. What qualification is needed for these BEL posts?

A full time B.E/B.Tech/BSc Engg (4 years) in the listed electronics, computer, IT or electrical disciplines is needed; MCA is also accepted for most Sr FOE roles and System Admin, MSc (Computer Science/Information Technology) for Sr Application Developer, and Mech for IT Support staff and IT Helpdesk Staff.

### Q4. What is the age limit for BEL Recruitment 2026?

As on 01.11.2026, the upper age limit is 45 years for Sr FOE, 40 years for FOE and 32 years for Project Engineer-I (General and EWS). OBC (NCL) get 3 years, SC/ST 5 years and PwBD 10 years of relaxation.

### Q5. What is the salary of a BEL Field Operation Engineer and Project Engineer-I?

The first-year consolidated pay is Rs. 90,000 per month for Sr FOE, Rs. 70,000 for FOE and Rs. 50,000 for Project Engineer-I, with an increase of Rs. 5,000 for every year of extension.

### Q6. What is the application fee?

The fee is Rs.450/- + 18% GST for Sr FOE and FOE and Rs.400/- + 18% GST for Project Engineer-I, paid through SBI Collect. PwBD, SC and ST candidates are exempted.

### Q7. How is the selection done?

Selection is through a written test followed by an interview for shortlisted candidates, with 85% weightage for the written test and 15% for the interview.

### Q8. Is this a permanent job?

No, the posts are temporary contract posts for an initial period of two years, which may be extended by up to two more years, to a maximum tenure of 4 years.

### Q9. Can I apply for more than one post?

Yes, candidates can apply for more than one post, but a separate fee payment must be made for each post.

### Q10. Do allowances come on top of the pay?

Yes, a consolidated ₹20,000 per year (Sr FOE and FOE) or ₹12,000 per year (Project Engineer-I) is paid towards medical insurance premium, attire, stitching and footwear allowance.
