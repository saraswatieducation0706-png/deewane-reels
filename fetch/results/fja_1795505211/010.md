Kerala Public Enterprises (Selection & Recruitment) Board (KPESRB) has released Notification No. 025/2026 for the post of Junior Manager (Personnel). Eligible candidates can apply online at kpesrb.kerala.gov.in from 20 November 2026 to 19 December 2026. 

## KPESRB Quick Summary

2026 · 1 Posts

COMPANY NAME Kerala Public Enterprises (Selection & Recruitment) Board

POST NAME Junior Manager (Personnel)

QUALIFICATION MBA/PGDM, MSW

VACANCY 1 Post

AGE LIMIT 36 Years (Max)

LAST DATE 19-12-2026

The Kerala Public Enterprises (Selection & Recruitment) Board has invited online applications for one contract post of Junior Manager (Personnel) in Kerala State Textile Corporation Ltd, under Category No. 025/2026. The consolidated pay is Rs 35,000. Applicants need MBA (Human Resources Management) or MSW (Personnel Management) and 2 years' post-qualification experience in an HR department. Applications opened on 20-11-2026 and close on 19-12-2026.

## About KPESRB and Kerala State Textile Corporation

The Board is based at Vellayambalam, VikasBhavan P.O, Thiruvananthapuram, and issued this notification. Kerala State Textile Corporation Ltd is a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956. It is a Public Sector Undertaking under the Industries Department, Government of Kerala, with seven textile Mills across Kerala and one textile testing lab.

## Who Can Apply

### Qualification

MBA (Human Resources Management) / MSW (Personnel Management) from a UGC recognized University, National Institutes established by the Central Government, or Institutions established by the Government of Kerala. The qualification must be the one obtained after a regular course of study. Applications with the above qualifications only will be considered.

### Experience

Minimum 2 years' post qualification experience in the HR department of a Government/Quasi Government or a Public/registered Private Sector Industrial Undertaking.

### Age Limit

Maximum age of 36 years as on the date of notification. Relaxation in upper age limit is applicable to eligible candidates as per rules; the notice prints no relaxation table.

### Reservation

The notice clarifies that community reservation for appointments is not applicable to contract appointments. Separately, it says SC/ST/OBC/EWS candidates get reservation benefits as per rules if they upload valid and updated certificates; the special note on contract appointments applies to this post.

Candidates should check the official notification for the full eligibility conditions.

## KPESRB Junior Manager (Personnel) Job Description

  * Maintain employee records, personnel files, and HR databases.
  * Process appointments, promotions, transfers, and leave, attendance, and retirement matters.
  * Monitor attendance, absenteeism, and shift schedules.
  * Assist in maintaining industrial relations and liaise with employees and trade unions.
  * Ensure compliance with labour laws, standing orders, and company policies.
  * Coordinate employee welfare schemes, employee amenities and statutory benefits.
  * Organize employee training and development programmes.
  * Prepare personnel reports, correspondence, and manpower statements.



## How to Apply for KPESRB Junior Manager (Personnel)

Applications must be submitted only through online mode; applications through any other medium are summarily rejected. The notice does not print the application website address or step-by-step instructions. It sets these conditions for the online application:

  * Upload the essential qualification and essential experience certificates while applying. Applications of those who fail to upload them are summarily rejected.
  * Give a valid email ID and mobile number, because all correspondence on the recruitment goes to the registered email ID and mobile number given in the online application.
  * To claim reservation benefits where applicable, upload a valid and updated Caste Certificate, Community Certificate, Non-Creamy Layer Certificate or EWS Certificate, as the case may be. Without valid certificates no reservation benefits are granted.



### Documents named in the notice

  * Essential qualification certificate
  * Essential experience certificate
  * Caste / Community / Non-Creamy Layer / EWS certificate, as the case may be



The application fee is Rs 600/-, and Rs 150/- for SC/ST candidates. The notice does not state how the fee is paid.

## Selection and Interview

The notice says admittance to the written examination/skill test/interview will only be provisional. The Board may decline to evaluate a candidate's performance, and reject the candidature, if a material discrepancy is found in the application or credentials at any stage. The notice does not say which of these stages apply to this post.
