## JMI Selection Process 2026

All non-teaching positions will be filled as per the Recruitment Rules of Jamia Millia Islamia. Selection will be based on the candidates' performance in the written / skill / trade test / interview, etc.

  * Screening and shortlisting: The University may constitute a Screening Committee to scrutinise applications and shortlist candidates. It reserves the right to limit the number of candidates called for interview, using qualifications and experience higher than the minimum or any other condition it deems fit.
  * Assistant Registrar: Appointment under direct recruitment will be made through an All India open competition by conducting a Written Test and Interview.
  * Call letters: Call letters for the written/skill/trade test/interview will be sent only to shortlisted candidates, at the e-mail address given in the application form. There will be no correspondence with applicants who are not shortlisted.
  * Provisional acceptance: Eligibility may not be scrutinised at the time of the Recruitment Examination, so applications are accepted provisionally. A candidature will be cancelled at any stage if a claim is not substantiated, including during the University's scrutiny of documents.
  * No TA/DA will be paid for appearing in any written/skill/trade test/interview for any post.

## JMI Recruitment 2026 - FAQs

### Q1. How many vacancies are there in JMI Recruitment 2026?

Advt. No. 01/2026-27 (NT) lists 81 non-teaching posts across 33 positions (Pharmacist and Store Keeper were withdrawn by the corrigendum dated 23.11.2026), including 10 Upper Division Clerk (UDC) posts and 6 Beldar posts. The notice says the number is tentative and may be increased or decreased.

### Q2. When does the JMI Recruitment 2026 online application start?

Online applications on jobs.jmi.ac.in start on 12 November 2026.

### Q3. What is the last date for JMI Recruitment 2026?

The last date for the online application is 11 December 2026. The hard copy with documents must reach the Recruitment & Promotion Section, Registrar's Office, JMI by 21 December 2026.

### Q4. What is the application fee for JMI Recruitment 2026?

For Group A posts (Pay Level 10 and above), the fee is 1000 for UR/OBC and 500 for SC/ST. For Group B and C posts (Pay Level 7 and below), it is 700 for UR/OBC and 350 for SC/ST. PwBD (Divyangjan) candidates pay NIL in both groups.

### Q5. What is the age limit for JMI non-teaching posts 2026?

The age limit is 40 years for 32 posts. It is preferably below 56 years for Internal Audit Officer, Superintendent Engineer (Civil) and Executive Engineer (Civil). Age is reckoned as on 11 December 2026, and relaxation applies as per Central Govt. orders.

### Q6. What qualification is required for JMI UDC 2026?

The UDC post requires a Bachelor's Degree and two years' experience as Lower Division Clerk or equivalent in a Central/State Govt. office, university or autonomous institution. Candidates also need English typing @ 35 wpm or Hindi/Urdu typing @ 30 wpm, and proficiency in computer operations.

### Q7. What is the salary in JMI Recruitment 2026?

Pay ranges from Pay Level 1 (₹18,000 – 56,900) for posts such as Beldar and Wireman to Pay Level 13 (₹1,23,100 – 2,15,900) for Superintendent Engineer (Civil). The UDC post is at Pay Level 4 (₹25,500 – 81,100).

### Q8. What is the selection process for JMI Recruitment 2026?

Selection is based on performance in the written / skill / trade test / interview, etc., after shortlisting. For Assistant Registrar, it is an All India open competition with a Written Test and Interview.

### Q9. Do candidates who applied under Advt. No. 02/2025-26 (NT) need to apply again?

Yes. Earlier applicants for the posts marked * must apply afresh online. They are exempted from the fee on enclosing the original acknowledgement receipt or a valid post/courier slip, and they get age relaxation if they met the age limit on the last date of that advertisement.
