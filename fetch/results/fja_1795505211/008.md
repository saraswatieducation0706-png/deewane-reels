## ECIL Apprentice Selection Process 2026

The ECIL Apprentice Selection Process 2026 is based on qualifying examination marks and original document verification. Candidates will be called for document verification at CLDC-ECIL Hyderabad based on their online application details. After successful verification, selections will be made according to merit in the qualifying Engineering Degree or Diploma examination.

### 1\. Online Application Scrutiny

ECIL will examine the information submitted through the online application portal to identify candidates for original document verification.

### 2\. Document Verification

Candidates called for verification must attend the prescribed venue with their original certificates and one set of photocopies. The verification process will be conducted at the Corporate Learning and Development Centre, ECIL, Hyderabad.

### 3\. Merit-Based Selection

After successful document verification, ECIL will select apprentices based on consolidated marks obtained in the qualifying B.E./B.Tech examination for Graduate Engineer Apprentices and the three-year Diploma examination for Technician Apprentices.

Candidates whose results are expressed as CGPA must produce a relevant CGPA-to-percentage conversion certificate issued by their college or university.

### 4\. Provisional Offer Letter

ECIL will send provisional offer letters to selected candidates through email between 23 and 24 December 2026, according to the published schedule.

Note: The official notification does not prescribe a written examination or interview as part of the apprenticeship selection process.
