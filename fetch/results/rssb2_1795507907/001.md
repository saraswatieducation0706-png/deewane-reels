## Selection Procedure

The detailed selection procedure has been mentioned in the official notification of RSSB JE Recruitment 2026. But we are giving you here the short summary of the selection procedure. The candidates are advised to refer to official notification for selection procedure. 

Selection Procedure:

  * Selection will be made through a Written Examination (Computer Based Test (CBT) / Tablet Based Test (TBT) / Offline (OMR) based examination) tentatively scheduled in February 2027.
  * The examination will be conducted separately for Junior Engineer (Civil) (Degree), Junior Engineer (Civil) (Diploma), and Junior Engineer (Mechanical/Electrical) (Diploma).
  * If the examination is conducted in more than one phase/session, normalization of marks will be applied.
  * Exam Pattern: Total 120 marks, 2 hours duration. Part A: General Knowledge (Rajasthan History, Art & Culture, Geography, Political & Administrative System) – 40 marks. Part B: Technical Subject (Civil/Mechanical/Electrical Engineering as applicable) – 80 marks. All questions are Multiple Choice (MCQ) with equal marks.
  * Minimum qualifying marks: 40% of total marks. Candidates scoring less than 40% will not be eligible for appointment.
  * Negative Marking: 1/3rd marks will be deducted for each wrong answer.
  * OMR-based exam: Each question has 5 options (A, B, C, D, E). If a question is not attempted, option E must be darkened. If none of the 5 circles is darkened for more than 10% of questions, the candidate will be disqualified.
  * Allocation of selected candidates to departments (PWD, Water Resources, Public Health Engineering) will be done on the basis of category-wise merit and priority filled by candidates in the online application.

## How to Apply

Here is the complete procedure to apply for RSSB JE Recruitment 2026. The candidates should read the complete procedure to apply in the official notification from the links given below. 

The application procedure for is not fully online. Candidates are advised to follow the detailed instructions given below. 

  1. Candidates must log in to the SSO Portal at http://sso.rajasthan.gov.in using their SSO ID.
  2. Select the Recruitment Portal available under Citizen Apps (G2C).
  3. Click on ‘Apply Now’ for the relevant post.
  4. If One Time Registration (OTR) fee has not been paid, complete OTR first by selecting your category, disability status, and home state, and pay the prescribed registration fee.
  5. After completing OTR, candidates can apply through SSO ID.
  6. Upload a recent photograph (not older than 1 month), fill in all required details including visible body mark, and upload scanned signature and thumb impression.
  7. Fill in educational qualifications and other required details carefully in the online application form.
  8. Submit the application form online. Note the Application ID generated after final submission.
  9. Take a printout of the submitted online application form for future reference.
  10. Applications can be filled from 18-11-2026 to 17-12-2026 till 23:59 hours.
