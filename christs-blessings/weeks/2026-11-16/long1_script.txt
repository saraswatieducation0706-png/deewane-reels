## When you don't have the words | candle
Some nights you want to pray, but the words won't come. Your mind is too full, or too tired, or both.
If that's you tonight, you're in good company. Jesus' own friends once felt the same. The Gospel of Luke tells us that after watching Him pray, one of them said, "Lord, teach us to pray." [pause]
And Jesus gave them a prayer short enough to remember and deep enough to last a lifetime. We call it the Lord's Prayer. Tonight we're going to pray it very slowly, one line at a time, and let each line settle us a little closer to sleep. [pause]
There's nothing to recite correctly and nothing to keep up with. If you drift off before we reach the end, that's alright. The prayer will keep going, and so will His care. [pause]
Let your head settle into the pillow. Let your arms lie heavy at your sides. [pause]
Breathe in slowly... and let the breath go, like a long, quiet sigh. [pause]
Once more. In... and all the way out. [long pause]
## Our Father | candle
In Matthew's Gospel, Jesus begins like this:
"After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name." [pause]
Our Father. Before we ask for anything, Jesus tells us who we are talking to. Not a stranger. Not a judge waiting at a desk. A Father. [pause]
And notice the first word. Our. Not my. Tonight, all around the world, in farmhouses and city apartments, in hospital rooms and on night shifts, people are praying these same words. You are not praying alone. [pause]
Which art in heaven. He is above all the worry of this day, and still close enough to hear a whisper from your pillow. [long pause]
Let's say it together in our hearts. [pause]
Our Father which art in heaven. [pause]

Hallowed means holy, set apart, honoured. [pause]
All day, other names have pulled at you. The names on your screen, the names on your to-do list, maybe the name of a worry you've repeated a hundred times. [pause]
For this moment, let one name stand above them all. Let His name be the biggest thing in the room. [long pause]
Breathe in slowly, and in your heart say, Hallowed... [pause]
And breathe out, be thy name. [pause]
Again. Hallowed... [pause] be thy name. [pause]
Feel your shoulders drop a little lower with each breath. The day is getting smaller. He is not. [long pause]
## Thy will be done | night_lake
"Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven." [pause]
Maybe today didn't go the way you planned. A conversation went wrong. A hope didn't happen. Something you prayed about is still unanswered. [pause]
Thy will be done isn't a sigh of defeat. It's an open hand. It's saying, Father, I've told You what I want, and now I trust You with what happens next. [pause]
Jesus Himself prayed these words in the garden, on the hardest night of His life. So if they come out quietly, or shakily tonight, He understands. [pause]
Let's try it with our hands. If you can, rest your hands on the blanket, palms facing up. [pause]
Picture the plans you're holding for tomorrow, the meeting, the phone call, the appointment, the person you're worried about. Let them rest in your open hands. [long pause]
Father, Thy will be done. [pause]
## Our daily bread | night_lake
"Give us this day our daily bread." [pause]
This day. Not this month. Not the whole of next year. Jesus teaches us to ask for enough for today. [pause]
Long ago, in the wilderness, God fed His people with manna. It came fresh every morning, and it couldn't be stored up for the next day. Each morning they had to trust Him again. And each morning, there was enough. [pause]
Today is nearly finished. Think back over it for a moment. Somewhere in it there was bread. A meal. A roof. A kind word. A little strength when you needed it. [long pause]
Thank You, Father, for today's bread. [pause]
Tomorrow's bread belongs to tomorrow. You don't have to gather it tonight. You can let the cupboard of tomorrow stay closed until morning. [pause]
## Forgive us, as we forgive | night_lake
"And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors." [pause]
Every day leaves a few things behind. A sharp word we said. A thing we meant to do and didn't. And sometimes a hurt someone else gave us. [pause]
This line is a gentle way of clearing the table before bed. Not to make you feel guilty, but to set you free. [pause]
First, let one thing come to mind that you'd like God to forgive. You don't need to explain it. He already knows. [long pause]
Father, forgive me. Thank You that Your mercy is bigger than my mistakes. [pause]
Now, if someone hurt you today, or long ago, picture handing that debt to God. You don't have to feel ready. You don't have to pretend it didn't matter. You're simply asking for help to let go, a little at a time. [long pause]
Father, help me forgive, as You have forgiven me. [pause]
## Lead us, deliver us | night_lake
"And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen." [pause]
Lead us. Like a shepherd walking in front of the flock, choosing the path. Tomorrow, you won't walk ahead of Him. He'll be ahead of you. [pause]
Deliver us from evil. Tonight we place ourselves, and the people we love, under His care, and ask Him to keep watch while we sleep. [long pause]
And then the prayer ends the way it began, by looking up. For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory. [pause]
The kingdom is His, so you don't have to run the world tonight. [pause]
The power is His, so you don't have to be strong right now. [pause]
The glory is His, so you don't have to prove anything. [pause]
For ever. Amen. [long pause]
