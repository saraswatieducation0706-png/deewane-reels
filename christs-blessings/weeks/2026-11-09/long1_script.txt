## Known, all the way through | candle
Have you ever lain awake wishing someone understood what today was really like? Not the version you told people. The real one, with all the small worries tucked inside it.
Tonight we're going to rest in a psalm written for exactly that feeling. Psalm 139 is David's song about being completely known by God, and still held. This is a sleep prayer, so there's nothing to get right. If you drift off before the end, that's alright. The music will carry on, and so will His care. [pause]
Before we begin, let the room grow a little darker. If a screen is near you, dim it, or turn it over. Let your head find the cool side of the pillow. [pause]
Now breathe in gently through your nose. [pause]
And let it out slowly, as if you were fogging up a cold window. [pause]
Once more. In... and out, all the way down. [long pause]
## Every sitting down, every lying down | candle
The psalm begins with God's attention.
"O LORD, thou hast searched me, and known me. Thou knowest my downsitting and mine uprising, thou understandest my thought afar off." [pause]
Downsitting and uprising. Every time you sat down today, and every time you got up again. The first coffee, the long drive, the moment you finally sat on the edge of the bed. None of it went unnoticed. [pause]
And He understands your thoughts from far off, even before they turn into words. So you don't need to explain yourself tonight. He already knows. [long pause]
Then David adds this:
"Thou compassest my path and my lying down, and art acquainted with all my ways." [pause]
My lying down. This very moment, as you settle into bed, is known to Him. He is acquainted with it, the way a friend knows the way you laugh, or the way you sigh when you're tired. [long pause]
## Hemmed in, behind and before | night_lake
"Thou hast beset me behind and before, and laid thine hand upon me." [pause]
To be beset is to be surrounded. Behind you is today, with all it held. Before you is tomorrow, which you haven't seen yet. And God is on both sides. [pause]
Let your body feel that now. Feel the mattress behind you, holding your back and shoulders. [pause] Feel the blanket in front of you, resting lightly on your chest. [pause] You're covered from behind and before. [long pause]
And "laid thine hand upon me." Imagine a warm hand resting on your shoulder, steady and unhurried. Let your shoulders drop under it. [pause]
Let your jaw loosen. Let your forehead go smooth. Let your hands lie open. [long pause]
You don't have to hold yourself together tonight. You're being held. [long pause]
## Nowhere too far | night_lake
Then David asks a question that sounds almost like he's trying to escape.
"Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee from thy presence?" [pause]
He imagines the farthest places he can think of. And then he says:
"If I take the wings of the morning, and dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea; Even there shall thy hand lead me, and thy right hand shall hold me." [pause]
The wings of the morning. Picture the first light of day, racing across the water to the far edge of the sea. Even if you could ride that light as far as it goes, you'd find His hand already waiting there. [pause]
Maybe some part of you feels far away tonight. Far from home, far from the person you used to be, far from feeling close to God. This psalm says there is no distance He hasn't already crossed. [long pause]
Let's pray quietly.
Lord, wherever I am tonight, You are already here. Lead me with Your hand, and hold me with Your right hand while I sleep. [long pause]
## When the night feels dark | night_lake
Now comes the part of the psalm that was made for nights like this one.
"If I say, Surely the darkness shall cover me; even the night shall be light about me. Yea, the darkness hideth not from thee; but the night shineth as the day: the darkness and the light are both alike to thee." [pause]
The darkness in your room is real. And some of us are walking through a darker season too, one that doesn't lift when the sun comes up. [pause]
But to God, the night isn't hiding anything. He sees you as clearly now as He did at noon. Your worries don't get bigger in His sight just because the lights are off. [long pause]
So if a worried thought comes now, you can let it pass, like a car's headlights sliding across the ceiling and then moving on. [pause]
Let it come. [pause]
Let it go. [long pause]
The darkness and the light are both alike to Him. You're seen, even now. [long pause]
## More than the sand | night_lake
Later in the psalm, David turns from being known to wondering at God's thoughts.
"How precious also are thy thoughts unto me, O God! how great is the sum of them! If I should count them, they are more in number than the sand: when I awake, I am still with thee." [pause]
Some people count sheep to fall asleep. Tonight, let's count something better. [pause]
Think of one grain of sand on a wide, quiet beach. Let that be one kind thought God has toward you. [pause]
And another grain. Another thought of care. [pause]
And another, and another, too many now to count. [long pause]
You don't need to finish counting. That's the point. There are more than you could ever reach. [long pause]
And notice the last line: "when I awake, I am still with thee." Whatever happens through the night, whatever time you wake, He will still be there. You don't have to stay awake to keep watch over anything. [long pause]
## Search me, and know my thoughts | night_lake
Near the end, David prays one more line, and it's a good one to borrow at bedtime.
"Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts:" [pause]
The thoughts David means here are the restless kind, the ones that keep turning over and won't settle. And instead of hiding them, he hands them to God to look at. [pause]
Let's do that now. Whatever is still turning in your mind, a conversation, a bill, a test, someone you're worried about, hold it out in your open hands. [long pause]
Lord, here are my thoughts. You know them better than I do. Keep them for me tonight. [long pause]
## A blessing for sleep | night_lake
Let's close with a blessing. Just let the words rest on you.
May the Lord who searched you and knows you keep you close tonight. [pause]
May His hand rest on you, behind you and before you. [pause]
May the darkness be as light to Him as the day, and may you rest in that. [pause]
May His thoughts toward you, more than the sand, be the last thing on your mind. [pause]
And when you wake, may you find that you are still with Him. [pause]
In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. [long pause]
You're known, and you're loved. Sleep now. Goodnight.
