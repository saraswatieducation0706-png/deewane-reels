## Settling in | night_lake
Hello, and welcome. If you've come here at the end of a long day, you're in the right place. There's nothing you need to do now. Nothing to fix, nothing to finish. Just rest.
Let your body settle wherever you're lying. Let the pillow hold your head. Let the bed hold your weight. [pause]
Take a slow breath in... and let it out gently. [pause]
Once more. Breathe in... and out. [pause]
The Bible says in Psalm 4, verse 8: "I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety."
Tonight, that is our prayer. To lie down in peace. To sleep. To know that we are safe in God's hands. [pause]
## Letting go of today | night_lake
Let's begin by setting down the day.
Think of one thing that happened today that is still sitting with you. Maybe a conversation. Maybe a task you didn't finish. Maybe a worry about tomorrow. [pause]
You don't have to solve it tonight. Picture yourself placing it gently into God's hands, the way you'd hand a heavy bag to someone you trust. [pause]
Lord, I give You this day. The good parts and the hard parts. The things I did well, and the things I wish I had done differently. I can't carry them through the night. You can. [pause]
First Peter, chapter 5, verse 7, says: "Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you."
All your care. Not just the big worries. The small ones too. The ones you feel silly about. He cares about all of it, because He cares about you. [pause]
If there's someone you're upset with tonight, you don't have to resolve it right now. Just say, "Lord, I bring this to You. Help me to forgive, and help me to be forgiven." [pause]
And if you're upset with yourself, hear this: God's mercy is not tired of you. His compassion has not run out. Tomorrow morning, it will be new again. [long pause]
Breathe in slowly... and let it go. [pause]
## Psalm 23, slowly | night_lake
Now let's walk through one of the most loved passages in all of Scripture, Psalm 23. We'll take it one line at a time. Let each line settle before we move on.
"The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want." [pause]
A shepherd stays with the sheep through the night. He doesn't sleep while they're in danger. Tonight, you have a Shepherd who watches over you. You are not unguarded. You are not forgotten. Whatever you lack, He knows. [long pause]
"He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters." [pause]
Picture a quiet field. Soft grass. A stream so still it reflects the stars. Sheep only lie down when they feel safe. He is making a safe place for you to rest, right now. [long pause]
"He restoreth my soul." [pause]
Whatever was drained out of you today, your patience, your energy, your hope, He knows how to restore it. Not all at once. Gently. Through the night. [long pause]
"He leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name's sake." [pause]
You don't have to know tomorrow's path tonight. He leads. You follow, one step at a time, and only when morning comes. [long pause]
"Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort me." [pause]
Some of us are walking through a valley right now. Illness, loss, fear, uncertainty. David didn't say the valley wasn't real. He said: thou art with me. You are not walking through it alone. [long pause]
"Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over." [pause]
Even when life feels hostile, God sets a table of provision. Tonight, think of one blessing from today, however small. A kind word. A meal. A moment of quiet. Your cup is not as empty as it feels. [long pause]
"Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the LORD for ever." [pause]
Goodness and mercy follow you, every day, including tomorrow. You can fall asleep knowing they'll be waiting when you wake. [long pause]
## Prayer for the people you love | night_lake
Now, let's bring the people we love before God.
Think of someone in your family. Picture their face. [pause] Lord, watch over them tonight. Give them rest. Protect them. Let them know they are loved. [long pause]
Think of a friend who is going through something hard. [pause] Lord, be near to them. Comfort them where I can't. [long pause]
Think of someone you've lost, or someone far away. [pause] Lord, I trust them to Your care. Hold what I can't hold. [long pause]
And Lord, for everyone listening to this prayer tonight, in every home and every city, the sleepless, the grieving, the worried, the lonely, give them Your peace. [long pause]
## Blessing for the night | night_lake
Jesus said in John 14, verse 27: "Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid."
Let not your heart be troubled. Not tonight. [pause]
Now receive this blessing.
May the Lord watch over your sleep. May His peace settle over your mind. May every worry grow quiet. May you rest in safety, and wake in mercy. In the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Amen. [long pause]
Rest now. The music will stay with you. Goodnight, and God bless you.
