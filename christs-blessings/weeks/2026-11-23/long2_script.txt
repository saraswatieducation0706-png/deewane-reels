## Forget not all his benefits | candle
Three thousand years ago, King David talked to his own heart, the way you might talk to a friend who's forgotten something important. [pause]
"Bless the LORD, O my soul: and all that is within me, bless his holy name. Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits:" [pause]
Forget not. Isn't that what we do? We remember every worry, every bill, every unkind word. But the kindnesses slip away so quickly. [pause]
So tonight, let's do what David did. Let's remind our own souls. [long pause]
