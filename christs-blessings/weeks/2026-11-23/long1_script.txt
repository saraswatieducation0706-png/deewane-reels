## Jesus wept | galilee_dusk
In John's Gospel, there's a moment when Jesus arrives in Bethany. His friend Lazarus has died. His sisters, Mary and Martha, are weeping, and their friends are weeping with them. [pause]
And then come the shortest words in the whole Bible. [pause]
"Jesus wept." [long pause]
He knew what He was about to do. And still, He didn't rush past their tears. He stood with them in the sorrow, and He cried. [pause]
So if you've cried today, or if you're too tired to cry, you are not failing at faith. You're in the place where Jesus Himself has stood. Your tears are not a sign that He is far away. They may be the very place He comes closest. [long pause]

## Blessed are they that mourn | night_lake
On a hillside in Galilee, Jesus began His most famous sermon with a list of surprising blessings. One of them was this: [pause]
"Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted." [pause]
It's a strange word, blessed, next to mourning. Jesus isn't saying grief is good, or that you should feel happy about it. He's saying that mourners are not forgotten. The comfort is promised to them by name. [pause]
Grief is the shape love takes when someone is gone. You mourn because you loved. And that love was never a mistake. [long pause]
Let your shoulders soften now. Let your jaw unclench. Let your hands lie open on the blanket. [pause]
Breathe in... comforted. [pause]
Breathe out... by Him. [long pause]

## Not without hope | night_lake
Paul wrote to grieving friends in Thessalonica, people who had lost someone they loved. He didn't tell them not to be sad. Listen carefully to what he said. [pause]
"But I would not have you to be ignorant, brethren, concerning them which are asleep, that ye sorrow not, even as others which have no hope." [pause]
Not that you sorrow not. But that you sorrow with hope. [pause]
Christian grief is still grief. It still hurts. But it has a horizon. There's a morning coming that death does not get to cancel. [long pause]
And in the very last book of the Bible, God gives us a picture of that morning. [pause]
"And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away." [long pause]
He will wipe away the tears Himself. Not a servant. Not an angel. God. [long pause]
