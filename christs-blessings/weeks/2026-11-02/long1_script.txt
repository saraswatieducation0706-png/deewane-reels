## Under the wings | night_lake
Picture a small bird at the end of a cold day. The wind picks up, the light is going, and it does the only thing it knows to do. It tucks itself under its mother's wing and goes still.
That picture is all through the Bible, and tonight we're going to rest inside it. This is a sleep prayer from Psalm 91, one of the oldest songs of shelter there is. You don't have to stay awake until the end. If sleep comes early, let it. The music will keep playing, and you'll still be under the wing. [pause]
Before we begin, let's make the room a little quieter. If there's a screen nearby, turn the brightness down or set it face down. Pull the blanket up to wherever it feels right. [pause]
Now let one long breath out, slower than usual. [pause]
And another. Let it go all the way out, until there's nothing left to push. [long pause]
## Coming in from the cold | night_lake
The psalm starts with a doorway. Listen.
"He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty." [pause]
To dwell is to live somewhere. Not to visit for an hour, but to unpack and stay. And a secret place is a hidden place, close and protected, the kind of room only family is invited into. [pause]
Tonight you're invited in. You don't need to knock with the right words, or arrive with a tidy heart. You can come in exactly as you are, tired, unfinished, still carrying a few loose ends from the day. [long pause]
Let's pray at the doorway.
Lord, I'm coming in from a long day. I don't have much left to give. But I'd like to stay here with You tonight. Let me rest in the secret place, under Your shadow. [long pause]
## Saying it out loud | night_lake
The next verse is the psalmist's reply, and it's personal.
"I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust." [pause]
Notice the words "I will say". Sometimes the heart needs to hear the mouth say what's true. So in a moment, if you'd like, whisper it, or just think it, slowly. [pause]
You are my refuge. [long pause]
You are my fortress. [long pause]
You are my God. [long pause]
In You I will trust. [long pause]
You don't have to feel brave to say these words. Trust is often a decision we make while we're still a little afraid. [pause]
## Feathers, not walls | night_lake
Now comes the gentlest image in the whole psalm.
"He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth shall be thy shield and buckler." [pause]
A fortress has stone walls. But a wing is soft and warm. The same God who is strong enough to be a fortress is tender enough to be a wing. [pause]
Let your body feel that softness now. Feel where the pillow holds your head. [pause] Feel the blanket resting on your arms. [pause] Feel the mattress carrying the weight of your back and legs, so you don't have to hold yourself up. [long pause]
Let your hands go loose. Let your feet go heavy. [pause]
You are covered. You don't have to keep guard over your own body tonight. [long pause]
And His truth is your shield. When an anxious thought arrives, you don't have to argue with it for hours. You can simply hold up something true: He is with me. He is covering me. [long pause]
## The fears that come after dark | night_lake
The psalm knows that night has its own kind of fear.
"Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow that flieth by day;" [pause]
Night fears are often quiet ones. A sound in the house. A worry about money, or health, or someone you love. The thought that comes at two in the morning and feels far bigger than it ever does at noon. [pause]
This verse doesn't say those things are imaginary. It says you don't have to be ruled by them. Fear may come to the door tonight, but it doesn't get the last word. [long pause]
Let's name them gently, one at a time, and hand them over.
If there's a worry about your body, your health, or your strength, bring it to the edge of the wing and leave it there. [long pause]
If there's a worry about money, or work, or the week ahead, leave that there too. [long pause]
If there's a worry about someone you love, put their name under the wing with you. [long pause]
And if there's a fear you can't even name, that's alright. He knows what it is. [long pause]
## Kept in all your ways | night_lake
Here's one more verse from the psalm, and it's one many people have whispered over their children at night.
"For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways." [pause]
A charge is an assignment. Someone has been given the job of watching over you. Not just tonight, but in all your ways: the road to work, the hospital corridor, the kitchen at home, the place you'll walk tomorrow that you haven't seen yet. [pause]
You can stop rehearsing tomorrow. All your ways are already known to Him. [long pause]
## Remembering upon my bed | night_lake
David once spent nights in the wilderness of Judah, far from home, sleeping wherever he could. And he wrote this, in Psalm 63:
"When I remember thee upon my bed, and meditate on thee in the night watches. Because thou hast been my help, therefore in the shadow of thy wings will I rejoice. My soul followeth hard after thee: thy right hand upholdeth me." [pause]
David didn't spend those wakeful hours replaying his problems. He spent them remembering. So let's do the same. [pause]
Think of one time God helped you. It might be a big moment, or a very small one: a door that opened, a friend who called at the right time, a morning you didn't think you'd reach. [long pause]
Stay with that memory for a moment. Let it remind you that you've been carried before. [long pause]
"Thy right hand upholdeth me." You are being held up, even now, even as you begin to drift. [long pause]
## A whispered blessing | night_lake
Let's close with a blessing. You don't need to answer. Just let the words settle over you like a blanket.
May the Most High be your secret place tonight. [pause]
May His wings cover you, and His truth be your shield. [pause]
May every fear that comes in the dark find the door already closed. [pause]
May the people you love be kept in all their ways. [pause]
And may you wake to remember that He was your help through every hour of the night. [pause]
In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. [long pause]
Stay under the wing now. Rest. Goodnight.
