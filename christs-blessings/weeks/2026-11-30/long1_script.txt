## Forgiveness with thee | candle
Then the psalm turns to something we all carry to bed sometimes. [pause]
"If thou, LORD, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand? But there is forgiveness with thee," [pause]
If God kept a record of every wrong, none of us could stand. But He doesn't keep that kind of list. With Him, there is forgiveness. [pause]
So if there's something from today that you regret, a word, a choice, something left undone, you can set it down now. [pause]
Lord, I'm sorry for what I got wrong today. Thank You that there is forgiveness with You. [long pause]
Let the weight of it slide off your shoulders. You're not lying down condemned. You're lying down forgiven. [long pause]

## Watchmen for the morning | night_lake
"My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: I say, more than they that watch for the morning." [pause]
Picture a watchman on a city wall, long ago. All night he stands in the cold, looking out into the dark. He can't make the sun rise. He can't hurry it. But he knows it's coming, and he keeps looking east. [pause]
The psalmist says it twice, as if he's looking twice himself. More than they that watch for the morning. [pause]
You don't have to make your morning come either. That isn't your job tonight. Your job is only to rest, and to keep facing toward Him. [long pause]
Let your eyes close now, if they haven't already. [pause]
Let your face soften. Let your forehead relax. [pause]
Let your breath be slow and long, like the hours before dawn. [long pause]
## Plenteous redemption | night_lake
"Let Israel hope in the LORD: for with the LORD there is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption." [pause]
Plenteous. Not barely enough. Not just enough for good people. Plenty. More mercy than you have need. [pause]
God's people waited a long time for that redemption. Generations lived and died watching for the morning. And then, one night in Bethlehem, the morning came, wrapped in swaddling clothes. [pause]
That's why Advent is a season of hope. The God who kept His word then will keep it now. [long pause]
