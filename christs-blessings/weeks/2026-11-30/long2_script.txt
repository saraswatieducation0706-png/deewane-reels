## Come unto me | storm
Jesus once looked out at a crowd of tired people, people carrying more than they could manage, and He said this: [pause]
"Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest." [pause]
He didn't say, come once you've finished. He didn't say, come once you've sorted yourself out. He said, come. As you are. Heavy laden. [pause]
Then He went on. [pause]
"Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls." [pause]
Meek and lowly in heart. That's the heart of the One you're coming to tonight. Gentle. Humble. Not impatient with you. Not disappointed. [long pause]
Lord Jesus, I'm coming to You tired. I'm bringing the whole heavy load. [long pause]
## As a mother comforts | candle
God gives us many pictures of His care. One of the tenderest is in the book of Isaiah. [pause]
"As one whom his mother comforteth, so will I comfort you;" [pause]
Think of a small child who's had a long, overwhelming day. Too many people, too much noise, too many tears. And then a mother scoops them up, holds them close, and says, shh, it's alright now. [pause]
That's the comfort God offers you tonight. Not a lecture. Not a list. Just being held. [long pause]
Let yourself be held now. Let the blanket be a reminder of His arms around you. [long pause]
## A quieted soul | candle
King David once described his soul like this: [pause]
"Surely I have behaved and quieted myself, as a child that is weaned of his mother: my soul is even as a weaned child." [pause]
A weaned child is not restless or demanding. It's simply content to rest against its mother, trusting that it's safe. [pause]
Let's quiet ourselves the same way, starting with the body. [pause]
Feel your feet grow heavy and warm. [pause]
Let your legs sink into the bed. [pause]
Let your stomach soften with each breath. [pause]
Let your chest rise and fall, slowly, by itself. [pause]
Let your arms and hands go still. [pause]
And let your face relax: your forehead, your eyes, your jaw. [long pause]
My soul is even as a weaned child. [long pause]
## He will rest in his love | night_lake
There's a verse tucked away in one of the smallest books of the Bible, the prophet Zephaniah. It's one of the most beautiful descriptions of how God feels about His people. [pause]
"The LORD thy God in the midst of thee is mighty; he will save, he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love, he will joy over thee with singing." [pause]
He will rejoice over thee. On a night when you feel you haven't done enough, God isn't looking at you with a frown. He rejoices over you. [pause]
He will rest in his love. His love for you isn't anxious or restless. It's settled. [pause]
He will joy over thee with singing. Let the music you're hearing be a small reminder of that. [long pause]

## For the weeks ahead | night_lake
Before we rest, let's ask God for help with the weeks ahead. [pause]
Lord, in this busy season, help me say yes to what matters, and no to what doesn't. [pause]
Help me rest without guilt, and give without strain. [pause]
Help me notice You in the middle of the noise. [pause]
And if I start to feel overwhelmed, remind me to come to You first. [long pause]
And if your tiredness is more than a busy season, if it has gone on for a long time or feels very dark, please talk with a doctor, a counselor, or someone you trust. That's part of caring for the life God gave you. [long pause]
